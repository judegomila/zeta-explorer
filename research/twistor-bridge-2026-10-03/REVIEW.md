# Mathematical consistency review

The following statements were independently checked before delivery on 3 October 2026. This is a mathematical review, not formal proof-assistant certification or a literature-priority claim.

1. **No-root theorem.** An operator root automatically commutes with its power. Away from w=2, strict decrease of the angular eigenvalue moduli forces preservation of each harmonic space, even without imposed equivariance. The first two nonconstant dimensions are 9 and 25, and their multipliers have simple zeros at 2. Determinant orders therefore preclude every integer root q>=2. The argument is local in w and does not assert impossibility of all width families in a.

2. **Finite and infinite enlargements.** On r complete copies, the divisibility condition is exactly q|r. The cyclic companion construction proves sufficiency and contractivity. A delay coordinate in L2([0,1)) yields a strongly continuous contractive semigroup with the amplified wide bridge as time-one map; no identification with the actual thin xi ratio follows.

3. **Arithmetic inverse kernel.** Independent beta inversion verifies g_a(exp(-u)) with no extra exp(-u) in the additive density. The positive Dirichlet coefficients of zeta(z-a)/zeta(z+a) begin with n=1, then n=2. The beta stochastic-order estimate is valid for every 0<a<=1 and gives a strictly negative full kernel on log(3/2)<u<log(2). Euler-line damping makes a finite signed measure. Fourier uniqueness proves the failure of a positive probability measure, while making no claim against Schur contractivity.

4. **Compactness.** For 0<a<1, removed neighborhoods of the finitely many singularity curves have Schur norm O(epsilon^a). For a>=1 the kernel is already bounded on the active compact square. One must not claim O(epsilon^a) for deleted smooth background when a>1. The first singularity proves failure of the Hilbert-Schmidt property at a<=1/2; compactness still holds.

5. **Unitary coordinates and local bound.** U f(v)=exp(v/2)f(exp(v)) gives the kernel exp(-(v+v')/2)g_a(exp(-(v+v'))). At ell0=exp(-EulerGamma)/(8*pi), L0=exp(ell0)=1.02259116413516259..., no prime n>=2 contributes. The pointwise bound and log-Gamma convexity prove norm<=2^(-a) for all 0<a<=1.

6. **Positivity and zero-width limits.** The Hankel operator is indefinite even when its kernel is pointwise nonnegative. The positive quadratic forms are I+H and I-H. Strong convergence to reflection as a decreases to zero forces the norm to tend to one and both inverse families to become unbounded; compactness does not turn this into operator-norm convergence.

7. **Primary-source comparison.** Suzuki, arXiv:1204.1827, equations (2.1)-(2.4) provides the exact kernels. Lemmas 4.2-4.4 establish all-window Hilbert-Schmidt and strict-contraction properties in the width>1/2 regime. The report derives an explicit local bound in the thin regime and elementary compactness beyond that Hilbert-Schmidt range. Whether equivalent statements exist elsewhere in the literature has not been assessed.

The next exact prime threshold is L=sqrt(2). Negative n=1 kernel entries occur before it, on 3/2<xy<2. A finite numerical compression cannot by itself certify the full operator norm or RH; a quantitative compression-error bound is required.
