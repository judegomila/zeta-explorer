# s1-prime-torus-orbit: screening of four torus combinations (2026-11-19)

Lane type: screener. Four combinations of graph nodes were turned into finite statistics on the prime-phase torus, computed for zeta, a Dirichlet L-function and the controls, and classified. Verdict: **0 survivors out of 4**. Each test separates zeta from a control only (a) where the prime cutoff exceeds the height (the explicit-formula regime, which reads the zeros at that height), (b) through coefficient size to the right of 1, (c) through a count of on-line zeros (Turing's method), or (d) at the level of the torus function, where the difference is "not an Euler product" (an input, not a consequence). The sign twist and the unimodular twist are not separated from zeta by any of the four statistics at any size reached; this is forced by Lemma R below.

Labels: PROVED (elementary, in this file), CLASSICAL, MEASURED (float64, screen), RECHECKED (float64 pipeline compared with 40-digit arithmetic at production size).

## 0. Setup shared by the four tests

- Torus: c_p(t) = t log p / (2 pi) mod 1 for primes p <= P (P = 1e6: 78,498 primes; P = 1e7: 664,579 primes). Heights t = t_int + delta, t_int an integer below 2^40 multiplied exactly against an 88-bit fixed-point value of frac(log p / 2 pi) (error below 2^-48 cycles), delta in float64. Composite phases c_n = sum v_p(n) c_p. Haar samples replace the base point by independent uniform numbers and keep the same flow in delta.
- Families. Euler type, per-prime multiplier u_p: ZETA (1), CHI (chi mod 5, chi(2) = i; the positive comparison), CHIB (its conjugate), SGN (random signs, fixed seed, completely multiplicative), LIO (u_p = -1), UNI (random unimodular, fixed seed). Not Euler type: DH = c1 L(chi) + c2 L(chibar) (functional equation checked to 3e-17); T-NR = zeta(s) m(s), m(s) = 3^(s-1/2) + 2.2 + 3^(1/2-s) (zeros on Re s = 0.90375 at heights (2k+1) pi / log 3, the lowest at 2.8596); E_a = zeta(s+a) zeta(s-a) (a = 0.1 in tests 1-2, a = 0.15 in test 4); HEAT = sum_n exp(tau log^2 n / 4) n^(-s), tau = -0.5, n <= 3e5 (the coefficient side of the backward heat deformation; the height-dependent factor of H_tau is not included; not multiplicative).
- Truncated objects: F_P(s) = prod_{p<=P} (1 - u_p p^(-s))^(-1) and the corresponding combinations for DH, T-NR, E_a.

**Lemma R (rotation; PROVED, elementary).** For a fixed prime set, the torus function of a twisted Euler product is the torus function of zeta composed with the rotation theta_p -> theta_p - arg u_p. Haar measure is rotation invariant and the flow t -> (t log p) commutes with rotations. Hence (i) every Haar statistic of F_P is the same for ZETA, SGN, LIO, UNI (and CHI apart from the missing factor at p = 5); (ii) an orbit statistic of a twist is the zeta statistic along the translated orbit {theta_0 + t omega}; (iii) the set of return times of the flow to a neighbourhood of its starting point is the same for all of them. A statistic that converges to its Haar value along the orbit (Kronecker-Weyl, for fixed P) cannot separate zeta from a twist in the limit; at finite T it can do so only through the orbit-against-Haar discrepancy, i.e. through where the orbit of zeta starts (the point theta = 0 at t = 0, the pole) or through correlations of p^(-it) between neighbouring primes when P > t (the prime number theorem with remainder, equivalently the zeros at height t).

Lemma R is the reason the sign twist and the unimodular twist pass every test below exactly as zeta does once t >= P.

## 3. Why there is no survivor

1. Sign twist and unimodular twist: Lemma R. Every statistic here is either a Haar statistic of the torus function (identical) or an orbit statistic at t >= P (equal to Haar within sampling error for all), or lives in the regime P > t, where the difference is the prime number theorem for the coefficients, i.e. the explicit formula at height t.
2. DH: separated (a) at Haar level because its torus function is a sum of two rotated Euler products (tests 1 and 4C show a different law; this is the absence of an Euler product, an input and not a consequence), (b) by coefficient growth of its logarithmic derivative (test 2; zeros to the right of 1), (c) by the deficit of sign changes on the line (test 3; a zero count). None is a consequence of the kind asked for.
3. T-NR: passes test 3 identically and test 4; differs in tests 1 and 2 only through its one local factor.
4. E_a: passes tests 1, 2, 4 like zeta up to its built-in shift; fails test 3 trivially (no sign changes), which is a zero count.
5. Backward heat coefficients: orbit equals Haar at every band; no test here sees the flow.

## 5. Limits of this screen (weakest points)

1. The route-45 source field itself (Raymond-chain amplitudes at the binding cells) and the route-46 supremum 0.5335 were not rebuilt; test 1 and test 2 use the generic prime fields at the same abscissa. The classification rests on Lemma R, which applies to the route-45 field as well (it is a function on the same torus), but its numbers were not reproduced.
2. Test 4 (B) evaluates the true functions only at 604 candidate tau; the minimum over all tau <= 1e12 of the function distance was not computed (cost 1e12 x 4e5 terms). The rate statement for functions is (C), at torus level with P = 1e4.
3. The backward heat control is the coefficient-side Dirichlet polynomial, not H_tau; it was not run in tests 3 and 4. The window bands at T = 1e2 and 1e3 overlap, so their z-values overstate significance.
