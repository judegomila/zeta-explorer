## Why the bridge is not closed

The Fisher bounds control an information-theoretic quantity. The target
Schur/coupling cost is measured after a source map, a positive-complement
inverse, degree-dependent whitening, exceptional directions, and a shrinking
reserve have all been included. A bound in the first metric is not a bound in
the second without a proved comparison. CP62 equations J3-J9 make this missing
comparison explicit.

The finite payments show that the desired mechanism is real in multiple bounded
settings: the existing odd-19 endpoint, the degree-2 Newman interval, the
gauged degree-5 Newman interval, an odd same-parity degree-9 endpoint reserve,
the complete-theta quadratic family, and the normalized degree-7 regional
variation payment. The corrected even2 arithmetic replay separately
reproduces another known finite contact after repairing a swapped-coordinate
implementation; it is not counted as a new payment theorem. None of these
results controls the constants as degree grows, so they do not fill the
downward arrow from metric/source transfer to uniform domination.

The remaining current-prefix correlated inequality is still open. Conditional

larger space” in the abstract; it is the concrete complete-theta inequality

`delta^2 >= -4 H_span(q',tq) sigma`

for `t^2q'`, or an adaptive old-data feature, at every rung. The residual
recurrence closes as an operator recurrence on two consecutive rungs but not
as a fixed scalar Gram state: multiplication creates a new highest layer and
new positive moments. This is not an impossibility result for a theta-specific
fixed-width inequality.

For the signed-cell branch, the complete physical correlation now supplies
an analytic infinite tail. The Bessel reduction proves eventual cell sign
`(-1)^(kappa+1)` and ratio `|e_(n+1)|/|e_n| -> exp(-2pi)`. A separate
contour-shift estimate supplies arbitrary-power absolute bounds. Therefore the
open Ewald dependency can be reduced to a certified finite head plus a proved
tail margin for three quantities: the half-seed, `S'`, and aggregate
`D=P'N-PN'`. The stronger termwise stochastic-tail inequalities are not a
uniform replacement: on odd rungs an eventual positive tail makes them fail
whenever a negative head cell exists. The aggregate reverse-order inequality
can still be paid by favorable finite-head compensation.

The endpoint derivative has the exact completed multiplier
`H_1=-s(2-s)pi^(-s/2)Gamma(s/2)zeta(s-1)`, but this identity supplies no
sign. Accordingly, the current Ewald arrow is a finite analytic head
enclosure over `1/2<=z<=1`, not another finite-cell table and not a
coefficientwise positivity theorem.

## Why the negative tail is constructive information

The `H_cut=1` counterexample rules out a convenient stronger premise: making every
cumulative spatial tail positive. It does not attack the complete kernel.
CP62 instead demonstrates the correct pattern on a restricted actual band:
the tail is negative, the central term is positive, and the complete kernel is
positive. Any viable proof must therefore preserve signed compensation rather
than certify the pieces separately.

The arithmetic tail theorem is consistent with this lesson. It signs only the
remote divisor remainder after a paid cutoff; it does not declare the finite
central arithmetic core positive. In the odd ladder the remote remainder is
itself negative and must be paid explicitly.
