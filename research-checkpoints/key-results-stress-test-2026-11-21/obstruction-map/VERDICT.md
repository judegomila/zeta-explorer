# Verdict: obstruction-map

## Claim as listed
"About 190 routes all stop at the same requirement (height-uniform, every-window bound), tested against a fixed panel of controls with the R1-R4 requirement score."

## What the material supports
A catalogue: 230 table rows (section 3 of `push/OBSTRUCTION_MAP.md`) and 220 campaign lane records, each giving a route towards RH or a zero-free statement, the step where it stops, and in most rows a named function (control) that satisfies the hypotheses the route uses while having zeros off the line or lacking one structure of zeta. No route produced an inequality locating zeros. All statements are internal: referees are internal passes; the label PROVED means two internal referee files.

The statement that survives:
> In this record, each of 230 examined routes either (i) is an identity or criterion equivalent to its target, (ii) uses only hypotheses also satisfied by an explicit function with off-line zeros (Davenport-Heilbronn, backward heat flow of xi, E_a = zeta(s+a)zeta(s-a), symmetric-multiplier twins of zeta, sign twists, prime-deletion products), or (iii) yields a mean-value or finite-height statement. No row is a theorem that no method exists; `STATEMENT_O.md` says so in its own text.

## Replay
- Control panel numerics: 18 quoted facts recomputed with independent mpmath code at 30-60 digits (DH coefficients, two DH off-line zeros, DH functional equation and kernel log-concavity on a grid, Liouville zero, NR-q zero and strip limit, HQ2 root and distance formula, NWM zero and symmetry, SG density identity and zero, ZQ density bound on zeros 6709/6710, QUAD value 7/6 and companion 0.2332, V6 mean 5/7, four backward-heat off-line zeros by Gaussian convolution of xi, a different method from the lane's). All agree to the printed digits. SG and ZQ density formulas and the ZQ bound re-derived by hand.
- Score table recomputed from `lanes.jsonl`: 220 of 220 lanes have r1 = r2 = r3 = r4 = false.
- Not replayed: the 230 row verdicts themselves, THIN/DEL (rest on short-interval prime theorems), the hitting-set table, the 7/8 calibration score.

## Stress findings
1. The R1-R4 score has zero variance (220/220 at 0). It does not rank or separate lanes; it restates that no lane proved a zero-free inequality. By METRIC.md any restatement or control-passing lane scores 0 by rule.
2. The score depends on the rule version. Through round 20 two lanes held R1 (headline "1 of 4"); both were set to 0 by the version-6 R1 test using control V6, constructed in round 21 for that test.
3. The panel is not fixed. METRIC v1 names 9 controls; the map now carries about 27 table controls and about 10 auxiliary ones, 14 or more added during the campaign, several built to defeat a lane that the earlier panel had not defeated.
4. "Same wall" is a grouping made afterwards. The lane records carry 160 distinct obstruction-kind strings; section 5 groups them into six parts of different nature. The part common to all (a proof must fail on functions with off-line zeros; must be uniform in height; must bound a largest value, not a mean) is a list of necessary conditions that follow from the statement of RH and from classical counterexamples; it is not an empirical regularity.
5. The panel does not test the class where RH is expected. The record states it has no control with zeta's functional equation, an Euler product with geometric factors and an off-line zero. Each control lacks at least one of FE, size, geometric local factors, integrality. R3 is therefore checked on fragments of the hypothesis set only.
6. Selection. Lanes are generated by a planner from earlier outcomes under a shared brief that names the controls in advance; 174540 of 183315 tag-pair families are untried; 53 or more of the 220 lanes are repair/record/referee lanes, not routes. Section 6 lists 25 directions not covered. Lanes are not independent samples of "methods".
7. Reliability. 21 PROVED sub-claims broken by later internal passes; 14 lanes did not hold as written; 132 lanes have one referee, 10 none. Residual errors in rows are likely; no external review.
8. Count: "about 190" is the round-12 figure; the file has 230 rows.

## Literature
The governing principle is standard: Davenport-Heilbronn 1936 (Titchmarsh 10.25; Spira; Bombieri-Ghosh 2011) for "functional equation without Euler product fails"; Diamond-Montgomery-Vorhauer 2006 for Beurling systems with zeros off the line; de Bruijn / Newman / Rodgers-Tao for the backward heat flow; Kaczorowski-Kulas, Saias-Weingartner, Booker-Thorne for periodic and linear-combination series; Hamburger and Knopp 1994 for identification by functional equation. A catalogue of this size with per-route controls was not found (5 general searches; no database search). The twin constructions (ZQ, SG, NR-q, HQ2-4, V6, CYC, NWM) are elementary; the record itself labels HQ2 an instance of Knopp 1994.

## Scores
- Arbitrariness 4 of 5. The individual control facts are canonical and reproducible (that part would score 1-2). The headline ("all stop at the same wall", "fixed panel", "R1-R4 score") depends on a scoring rule that changed, a panel that grew in response to survivors, a post-hoc grouping, and a planner-selected set of lanes.
- Importance 2 of 5. No consequence for RH, for zero-free regions or for Lambda. Use: an internal filter (three questions, pre-filter lemmas, a control harness) that saves re-entering closed routes; a few elementary lemmas (Lemma L, Lemma P, symmetric-multiplier twins) that a specialist would regard as exercises or known in kind. Told to an expert as "a proof of RH must use the Euler product and the functional equation jointly, uniformly in height", the statement is accepted as folklore; told as "190 routes hit the same wall", it would be read as a statement about the search procedure.

## Label
Catalogue of obstructions with internal refereeing; control numerics reproduced. Not a theorem, not a no-go result. Recommended public wording: "A table of 230 attempted routes, each with the step where it stops and, where one exists, an explicit function that satisfies the route's hypotheses and has zeros off the line. No route gave information on zeros. The table is a record of this search, not a proof that other routes fail."
