# Combining the two reviewed results

The universality obstruction and the adaptive contour estimate address different quantities and are consistent.

Fix 0<a<1/2. Let H_a(t) be the positive corrected N=1 head, and let D^times_(a,M(t)) retain all products nm<=M(t), with M(t)+1>=t/(sqrt(2)pi). The reviewed contour estimate and H_a(t)>=t³exp(-pi t/2) give

 |hat D^times_(a,M(t))(2t)/a-F_a(t)|/H_a(t)
       <=5t^(-3/2)exp[-(1-pi/4)t] ->0.

Therefore the adaptive product head divided by the N=1 head has every nonnegative real number as a subsequential limit and has limsup +infinity, just as the full shear does. In particular, no positive constant fraction of the N=1 head is a valid global lower bound for this adaptive head.

This does NOT rule out the exponentially smaller finite-head threshold required by the contour certificate. Classical universality here provides arbitrarily small fixed-target values at arbitrarily large heights, but gives no needed relation between the target size and the witnessing height. It would be invalid to infer that these universality heights make the head smaller than exp(-ct) for a specified c. No such comparison is claimed.

The exact remaining sufficient obligation is the correlated finite divisor-sum inequality

 hat D^times_(a,M(t))(2t)/a > 5t^(3/2)exp[-(1+pi/4)t].

It is explicitly computable and has a rigorous full tail allowance, but its all-height/all-shift proof remains open. Finite parameter certification, fixed-head domination, and global positivity must not be conflated.
