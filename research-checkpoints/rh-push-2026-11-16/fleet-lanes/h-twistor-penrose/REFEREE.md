## 2. Errors

**E1. Theorem 1, last clause: "Hecke_t := C[W_t(log p)] is a polynomial ring on the primes; J normalises it". False as
written.** By (1.4) `J W_t(a) J = W_t(-a) = W_t(a)^{-1}`, which is not in the polynomial ring. J maps the polynomial
ring onto the polynomial ring in the inverses. J normalises the Laurent ring (group algebra of Q_+^*), not the
ring the theorem names. Also `J (prod_p (1 - p^{-1/2} W_p)^{-1} f_t) = prod_p (1 - p^{-1/2} W_p^{-1})^{-1} J f_t`
with `J f != f`: the functional equation is not a consequence of J normalising the algebra and is not visible in the
product form. (P1) of Theorem 3 needs the same repair. Fix: replace the polynomial ring by the Laurent ring and
state `J kappa_t = kappa_t` as a separate input.

**E2. "Joint Hecke eigen-property" is a misnomer, and the EA control contradicts Prop. 4.**
(a) The datum `kappa_t` is not an eigenvector of any `W_t(log p)`: `W_t(log p) kappa_t = G_t D_{log p} kappa`, and
`kappa(q + log p)/kappa(q)` is not constant. What is an eigen-object is the coefficient sequence `a_n = 1` under
`U_p` (complete multiplicativity). Mechanism M in section 6 ("the datum is a joint eigen-object") is true only of
the coefficient sequence relative to a fixed archimedean profile.
(b) Prop. 4 defines DH's failure as "not a `U_p` eigenvector". Under that same test `zeta(s+a) zeta(s-a)` fails at
every prime: measured `b_{2n}/b_n` = 2.043, 1.554, 2.043, 1.400 for n = 1..4 (a = 0.3). Section 6 marks EA as P by
switching to an undefined "degree-2 algebra". The claim "EA passes the same step" is false for the step as stated.
The consistent criterion that gives DH = F, EA = P, HEAT = P is plain multiplicativity of the Dirichlet
coefficients. Under it the DH failure is located at products of two primes `= +-2 mod 5`
(`a_6 - a_2 a_3 = 1 + kappa_DH^2 = 1.0807`; `a_{pq} = a_p a_q` whenever one of p, q is `+-1 mod 5`).
(c) "Index-2 subalgebra generated by p = +-1 mod 5" (verdict item 3): the subalgebra generated by those primes
alone omits `U_2 U_3` and is not of index 2. Prop. 4's body ("classes +-1 mod 5", including products) is the
correct statement.

**E3. Prop. 4, last sentence: "kappa_DH,t ... is not [an operator Euler product] for any t". Not a defined property,
so not proved.** With the archimedean profile free, every kernel g is a trivial product (`b_n = delta_{n,1}`,
profile g). With the algebra free up to conjugation by a multiplication operator (which is what `G_t` is), every
kernel `m(q) kappa(q)` with m even and non-vanishing is an operator Euler product over
`M_m Hecke_0 M_m^{-1}`, normalised by J, with positive weights; `kappa > 0`, so `m = kappa_DH / kappa` is of this
form wherever `kappa_DH != 0`. The statement is true only after fixing (i) the algebra up to Jacobi-group
automorphisms and (ii) a regularity class for the profile (for example Mellin transform holomorphic and zero-free
in a right half-plane). Neither is stated. The same remark shows Theorem 1 has no content specific to the heat
flow: any invertible multiplier commuting with J gives the same theorem.

**E4. Prop. 6: "The Hecke correspondence at p restricted to this geodesic is q -> q +- log p". Wrong index.** With
`y = e^{2q}`, `T_p` (tau -> p tau, tau/p) moves q by `+-(1/2) log p`. The lane's `D_{log p}` is `y -> p^2 y`, the
extreme branches of `T_{p^2}`. This is the correct index for the weight-1/2 theta function (Shimura: only
`T_{p^2}`), so the operators used in sections 1-5 are right, but they are GL_1 (idele-class) dilations, and the
modular statement in Prop. 6 is mislabelled. "Restricted to the geodesic" is also not an operator statement: the
other `p^2 + p - 1` branches of `T_{p^2}` leave the geodesic. Prop. 6 is an analogy, not an identity. The test
proposed in section 9 ("commutator with `T_p` on Eisenstein series") inherits the error; on the weight-1/2 object it
must be `T_{p^2}`, and the weight-0 Eisenstein series on this geodesic has Mellin transform of zeta(s)^2 type, a
different object.

**E5. Theorem 3 is a tautology, not a theorem about arguments.** `G_t` commutes with J, so the triple
`(kappa_t, Hecke_t, J)` is conjugate to `(kappa, Hecke_0, J)` for every t; the zeros are read with the unconjugated
`ev_w`. "Conjugation-invariant data do not depend on t, zeros do" is the full content. The lane's weak step 1
concedes the informal status; the label PROVED should read "PROVED: properties P1-P4 hold for t < 0; the no-go is
a description of a class".

**E6. Measurement wording.** "Identities to 1e-30" applies to `X_0 = xi` and `W_2 W_3 = W_6`. The `cocycle` and
`conj` entries of `check1.py` compare an expression with an algebraic rewriting of itself (0.0 and 8e-31 are
rounding, not evidence). The sheared-series agreement is 4e-9 to 3e-6 (truncation), as RESULT.md states in the body.

**E7. Twistor dictionary.** Prop. 1 is the Fourier-Laplace representation of a 1+1 heat solution over its
characteristic parabola. It is an identity. Calling it a twistor/Penrose datum is an analogy (the lane says no
cohomology is involved). No link to the PR 10 `P(R^4)` model beyond the constant harmonic is established.

## 4. Surviving claim

For every real t, `G_t kappa = sum_n n^{-1/2} W_t(log n) G_t f` with `W_t(a) = e^{-t a^2/4} M_{-ta/2} D_a` a
homomorphism in a (conjugate of the dilation group by the Gaussian multiplier); the cocycle
`exp((t/2) log m log n)` is a coboundary; for t < 0 the evaluated series converges absolutely at every complex w
while zeros are off the line. Hence complete multiplicativity of the coefficient sequence relative to a fixed
profile, together with evenness, reality and positivity of the kernel, is preserved by the Gaussian multiplier and
cannot by itself give zero reality. DH fails multiplicativity at products of two primes `= +-2 mod 5`. This is the
known statement "heat control keeps a sheared Euler structure" (flow-euler-defect) in group language. It gives no
zero information and no new separator beyond multiplicativity.
