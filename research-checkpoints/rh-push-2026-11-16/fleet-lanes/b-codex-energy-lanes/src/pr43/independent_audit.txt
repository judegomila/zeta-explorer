1. FIXED-PULSE CRITERION, INCLUDING X=1/2
======================================
The claimed equivalence is supported by the argument in the note.

For g in L2(R) intersect L2(exp(Xu)du), the bilateral transform
Y(z)=integral g(u)exp(zu/2)du is absolutely convergent for 0<Re z<X.
Split the integral at zero and apply Cauchy-Schwarz with the two endpoint
weights. The same argument on compact substrips proves analyticity.
Plancherel and Holder interpolation give a uniformly bounded L2 norm on
vertical lines, hence the H2 strip property and the endpoint L2 traces.

D and N are bounded analytic multipliers on every finite vertical strip,
because their defining theta kernels have all exponential moments.
Consequently DY-NV belongs to H2 of the strip. Its zero boundary trace on
Re z=0 implies it vanishes identically by Hardy-space boundary uniqueness.
The pulse transform V(z)=2(exp(z/2)-1)/z has no zeros for Re z>0.
Thus every denominator zero in 0<Re z<X is removable in N/D.

The known zero strip gives Re(xi'/xi)(s)>0 on Re s>=1, including the boundary
line; thus D has no zeros for Re z>=1/2. At X=1/2, every possible genuine
right-half-plane pole is still inside the OPEN strip covered by the argument.
There is no missing endpoint pole at Re z=1/2.

Under RH, S is an inner right-half-plane function. With the stated Fourier
sign, its multiplier preserves functions supported in (-infinity,a].
Unitarity then gives right-tail norm contraction. Integrating that inequality
against X exp(Xa)da is legitimate by Tonelli and gives the asserted weighted
norm bound for the pulse. It does not assume the output's weighted norm is
finite in advance.

2. POLE-FREE S IMPLIES INNER: THE SPECIAL XI GROWTH IS ESSENTIAL
============================================================
The common-factor cancellation is legitimate, including multiple zeros.
D and N vanish together exactly at multiple zeros of F. If such a zero has
multiplicity m, the common order is m-1, and the removable quotient satisfies
S(z0+h)=-1+2h/(alpha*m)+O(h^2). Evenness of F allows the common canonical
factor to be chosen even. Boundary zeros are common and removable.

Here is an explicit uniform bound that strengthens the first paragraph of
Section 3. For sigma>1 and every real t,

 Re[xi'/xi](sigma+it)
 >= -log(pi)/2 + psi(sigma/2)/2 + zeta'(sigma)/zeta(sigma).

Indeed, Re psi(a+iy)>=psi(a) follows term by term from the digamma series;
the real parts of 1/s and 1/(s-1) are positive; and the absolutely convergent
Euler series gives Re(zeta'/zeta)(sigma+it)>=zeta'(sigma)/zeta(sigma).
The displayed lower bound tends to infinity as sigma tends to infinity,
uniformly in t. Hence N is zero-free sufficiently far to the right and D is
zero-free sufficiently far to the left. This proves the bounded vertical
strip assertion for the zeros of D.

After cancellation and assuming no genuine right-half-plane poles, the
remaining denominator zeros are in a bounded left vertical strip. Order-one
Hadamard factorization, grouped by conjugates, yields S(z)=exp(cz)B(z), with
c real and B a right-half-plane Blaschke product normalized consistently at
zero. Absolute convergence of the relevant real exponent corrections follows
from bounded real parts and sum |d_n|^(-2)<infinity.

The claimed sublinear logarithmic growth of B on the positive real axis is
also valid. Write its zeros as a_n+i*y_n, with 0<a_n<=A. For x sufficiently
large, each factor satisfies

  -log|b_n(x)|/x <= C*a_n/(x^2+y_n^2).

These terms are dominated by a summable multiple of a_n/(1+y_n^2), and each
tends to zero. Dominated convergence proves log|B(x)|/x -> 0. Since
F'(x)/F(x)->infinity and S(x)->-1, the exponential coefficient c is zero.
The inner function is nonconstant. Its taking value -1 at an interior F-zero
would violate the maximum-modulus principle. This deals with common factors
without a simplicity assumption.

3. TWO PRECISION FIXES FOR THE GRAPH-CORE DISCUSSION
=================================================
Fix A: explicitly qualify the bounded single-line multiplier assertion.
For X>=1/2 it is a bounded contraction by the known exterior positivity.
For an arbitrary X, the same graph-density argument identifies the maximal
CLOSED multiplier N(X+it)/D(X+it), which need not be bounded. If Section 4
inherits X>=1/2 from Section 2, its intended assertion is correct, but the
qualification should be repeated where the closure is stated.

The graph-density proof itself is valid. Finite exponential sums are dense
in L2(mu), where dmu=(|D|^2+|N|^2)dt is a finite measure: an orthogonal vector
would define a finite complex measure with identically zero Fourier transform.
The parameterization p -> (Dp,Np) identifies this space with the maximal
multiplier graph, since D is nonzero almost everywhere.

The simultaneous-domain obstruction is valid. For 0<sigma=Re p<X,

  ||ell_p||^2 = integral exp(sigma*u)/(1+exp(Xu))du
              = pi/[X*sin(pi*sigma/X)].

Translation gives ell_p(h_+(u+2b))=exp(-pb)D(p). The stated pulse-distance
lower bound and the derivative-evaluation annihilators therefore follow.
Separate density in the two endpoint spaces does not supply simultaneous
density. Conversely, simultaneous convergence would justify identification
with the literal unbounded conjugation by closedness of multiplication.

Fix B: specify the input coordinate in the toy-model cancellation statement.
The condition ell_p(f)=0 applies to the ORIGINAL unweighted input f. If the
continued vertical multiplier acts on v=exp(Xu/2)f, then the corresponding
condition is ell_(p-X)(v)=0. The two written kernels are not to be applied
to an identical unweighted f and compared without the conjugations.
The intended example and its domain obstruction are correct with this
coordinate convention made explicit.

6. ADDITIONAL SIGN CHECK FOR THE NEW BOUNDARY-ENERGY KERNEL
=========================================================
This extension was communicated after the three source files were read;
it is not part of the hashed versions above. Adopt the consistent notation
P_c=1_(-infinity,c] and Q_c=1_(c,infinity).
For the UNNORMALIZED h_+/-=(1+/-alpha*u/2)kappa(u), let

 H_c(u,v)=2*alpha*integral_[|c-(u+v)/2|,infinity]
          t*kappa(t-(u-v)/2)*kappa(t+(u-v)/2)dt.

Then the left-tail difference has a PLUS sign:

 ||P_c(h_-*a)||^2-||P_c(h_+*a)||^2 = <a,H_c a>.

The right-tail difference has a MINUS sign:

 ||Q_c(h_-*a)||^2-||Q_c(h_+*a)||^2 = -<a,H_c a>.

The convolution-product bracket is -2*alpha*t, while its integral from
-infinity to c-(u+v)/2 is the negative of the positive tail from the absolute
value of that endpoint. This verifies the signs. The root agent confirmed
the intended new identity used the right-tail projection and will standardize
the notation. No positive-semidefiniteness claim for H_c was established
by this sign calculation.
