The sixty source-document records include repository sources, earlier standalone papers, and notes extracted from archives. PDFs duplicating a TeX source are not counted as separate content reviews. A `targeted_content_review` means the relevant statements, proofs, or status sections were inspected; it does not mean every line of a long manuscript was audited. The ledger makes this deliberately conservative distinction.

Some tree filenames are dated later than the retrieval date. Their dates are treated as labels, not evidence that a future computation already occurred. Commit IDs and content hashes supply provenance. Historical top-level status summaries can be older than specialized branch modules. Neither a module's `done` status nor an archived assistant's theorem label is treated as an independent correctness certificate.

`source-ledger.json` contains source paths, repository links or Library identifiers, SHA-256 hashes, and review levels. The new package includes only the minimum arithmetic inputs and unchanged generator needed to reproduce its certificate; it does not vendor all old papers or the external 003 manuscript.
