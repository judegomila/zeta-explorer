# Relative-localization route ledger

- **Small total forcing mass ⇒ relative positivity:** false. The validated theta mass ratio about1.16717% is only integrated information; wedge_v5 supplies a Gaussian stationary counterexample.
- **All fixed-resolution positive averages ⇒ sign:** false even for analytic entire shears. The quadratic off-line-zero example in RELATIVE_LOCALIZATION.md has positive averages at every center for sufficiently large fixed radius while retaining a negative center.
- **Global unrestricted Carleson/Gram estimate is weaker than spectral domination:** false in the stationary full-test setting; Fourier uniqueness gives equivalence. Finite-cardinality or finite-resolution restrictions are genuinely weaker but need an error/regularity bridge.
- **A constant q<1 relative contraction at fixed A>1/2:** impossible for full theta, uniformly 0<a≤1/2, by the reviewed high-height comparison. The required gap collapses at infinity.
- **Ignore signed-cost nullspace blocks:** invalid. Green forcing is not positive semidefinite; use the full Schur criterion, not the special positive-cost kernel inclusion.
- **Infer a numerical result from slow direct xi convolution:** not done. Two exploratory quadrature processes were stopped before producing any numerical value; their output is not evidence.
- **Constructive retained route:** weighted local-average cancellation plus an explicit derivative cost, with displacement bounded by h/(1−e^(−πh/A))−A/π. The arithmetic inputs required by this criterion remain unproved globally.

The endpoint and adaptive-flat-boundary extension is developed separately in endpoint_v7, preserving this reviewed version.
