# Non-rigorous N=2 theta metric probe

An owned high-precision floating probe at normalized a=0 gave σ₁/σ₀≈11.74603 and σ₂/σ₀≈269.82850, then sampled the second logarithmic derivative of the h-weighted N=2 metric at h=0.01,0.02,...,0.80. No positive value was found; selected values were about −31.43 at h=0.15, −19.43 at h=0.20, and −30.61 at h=0.25. The script is theta_n2_metric_probe.py.

This uses truncated theta orbits and unvalidated floating quadrature, so it proves neither log-concavity nor absence of counterexamples. It does not invalidate the exact Gaussian N=2 counterexample, which concerns a different source. No next signed pivot or all-degree claim follows.
