# Exact exponential generating function for every grade

## Statement

Let

```text
P_0(z)=4z(2z-3),
P_(j+1)(z)=2z P_j'(z)+(1/2-2z)P_j(z),
P_j/P_0=A_j+c_j/(2z-3).
```

The complete exponential generating functions are

```text
P(t,z) := sum_{j>=0} P_j(z)t^j/j!
 = exp(t/2-z(exp(2t)-1)) P_0(z exp(2t)),              (1)

C(t) := sum_{j>=0} c_j t^j/j!
 = 3 exp(5t/2-(3/2)(exp(2t)-1))(exp(2t)-1),           (2)

A(t,z) := sum_{j>=0} A_j(z)t^j/j!
 = {exp(5t/2-z(exp(2t)-1))(2z exp(2t)-3)-C(t)}
   /(2z-3).                                            (3)
```

The apparent pole in (3) is removable. The even- and odd-grade ladders are
the exact symmetrizations

```text
A_even(t,z) = [A(t,z)+A(-t,z)]/2,
A_odd(t,z)  = [A(t,z)-A(-t,z)]/2.                     (4)
```

These formulas hold as formal power series, so each coefficient identity is
exact and arbitrary-degree.

## Proof

The recurrence for `P_j` is generated by the first-order operator

```text
L=2z partial_z + 1/2-2z.
```

Along the characteristic `z(s)=z exp(2s)`, the scalar multiplier is

```text
exp(integral_0^t [1/2-2z exp(2s)] ds)
 = exp(t/2-z(exp(2t)-1)).
```

Thus `P(t,z)=exp(tL)P_0(z)` is exactly (1).

Multiplying the one-pole decomposition by `P_0` gives

```text
P_j(z)=P_0(z)A_j(z)+4z c_j.
```

At `z=3/2`, this says `c_j=P_j(3/2)/6`. Substitution of `z=3/2` into
(1) gives (2). Finally divide (1) by `P_0(z)` and subtract
`C(t)/(2z-3)` to obtain (3). The numerator of (3) vanishes at `z=3/2`
by construction, proving removability. Equation (4) is coefficient parity.

## Consequences for the analytic search

1. The exact finite recurrence records are coefficients of one explicit
   entire-in-`t` expression; larger-degree generation is unnecessary for
   discovering the recurrence itself.
2. The scalar endpoint layer is completely encoded by `C(t)`. Any asymptotic
   or canonical-product argument can study its saddle geometry directly
   rather than infer it from a list of large exact coefficients.
3. Formula (4) makes the two Fourier-grade sectors explicit. Combined with
   the modular `h` versus `-h` identities in `ODD_CENTER_LEMMA.md`, it points
   to a two-channel parity factorization rather than a separate pointwise
   raw-orbit payment.
4. The formula does not give the sign of the complete reflected bridge Gram.
   The missing step remains a degree-uniform signed estimate or positive
   border recurrence in the actual bridge metric.

No finite computation or coefficient asymptotic derived from (1)--(4), by
itself, proves the reflected contraction or RH.
