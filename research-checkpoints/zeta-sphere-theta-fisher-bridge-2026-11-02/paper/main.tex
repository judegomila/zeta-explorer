\begin{abstract}
We organize a computational and analytic research program around a paired theta
kernel whose positive definiteness for every allowed shift is equivalent to the
Riemann hypothesis. The program has established two finite footholds: complete
$H_5$ positivity for all positive shifts and positivity of the full odd
$10\times10$ moment block through channel $19$ at normalized shift $a=0$.
It has also rigorously disproved a stronger shortcut: arbitrary spatial
cumulative tails need not be positive, even at the explicit positive shift
$a=10^{-4}$. A positive-time refinement exhibits the correct mechanism on a
restricted actual band: the tail is negative, the central contribution is
positive, and their complete sum is positive.

The new information-relaxation layer gives a genuine bridge Fisher bound
$I_a\le 2/17$, the regular endpoint bound $I_{a^2}(0)\le4/867$, and exponential
decay $I'(h)\le-14I(h)$ for the complete theta orbit law. These estimates are
then inserted into exact signed identities rather than treated as positivity
theorems. A two-ladder regression formula separates favorable innovation,
reflected innovation, and the mismatch action measured by the already-certified
lower block. Spatial heat preserves every zero and contributes only a removable
Gaussian reserve; Newman evolution retains a signed derivative-ladder forcing.
The central open lemma is a degree-uniform comparison showing that the actual
lower-metric innovation dominates regression mismatch, inverse feedback, and
signed forcing. Without that lemma the Riemann hypothesis remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section{The paired theta bridge}

Let $\psi$ be the complete even positive theta profile in the campaign
normalization, and write
\begin{equation}
  F(z):=\Xi(z)=\int_0^\infty \psi(u)\cos(zu)\,du,
  \qquad z=t+ia.
\end{equation}
For $a>0$ define the positive parent and its parameter derivative
\begin{align}
 K_a(v)&=\int_0^\infty \cosh(2ah)\psi(h-v)\psi(h+v)\,dh,\\
 C_a(v)&=\frac12\partial_a K_a(v)
       =\int_0^\infty h\sinh(2ah)\psi(h-v)\psi(h+v)\,dh.
\end{align}
With Fourier convention $\widehat f(k)=\int_\R f(v)e^{-ikv}\,dv$,
\begin{equation}
 \widehat K_a(2t)=|F(t+ia)|^2=:M(t,a),\qquad
 \widehat C_a(2t)=\frac12\partial_a M(t,a)=:W(t,a).
 \label{eq:bridge-fourier}
\end{equation}
The parent $K_a$ is a positive Gram for every $a$. The derivative $C_a$ is the
target and need not inherit positivity merely from its parent. Nonnegativity of
$W(t,a)$ for every real $t$ and every positive shift in the critical strip is
the horizontal-modulus formulation of the RH target, consistent with the
classical logarithmic-derivative criteria of Lagarias and the associated-kernel
literature \cite{Lagarias1999,Lagarias2005,SondowDumitrescu2010,Csordas2015}.

The exact paired-coordinate derivation is essential. An ordinary
two-dimensional integral receives no nonzero mass from the line $u=v$; that
line has two-dimensional Lebesgue measure zero. The repository's corrected
measure audit replaces the withdrawn ``positive diagonal channel'' with the
actual paired $(r,s)$ identity. Nothing in this paper uses the stale diagonal
argument.

For normalized moments put
\begin{equation}
 \mu_n(a)=(-1)^n\frac{C_a^{(2n)}(0)}{a},
\end{equation}
with the removable $a=0$ limit. The associated Hankel blocks are finite tests
of the full positivity hierarchy. They are useful footholds, but no fixed
finite order can discharge the universal quantifiers in RH.

\section{Certified finite footholds}

\subsection{Complete \texorpdfstring{$H_5$}{H5} positivity}

The odd three-channel block is
\begin{equation}
 G_a=\begin{pmatrix}
 \mu_1&\mu_2&\mu_3\\
 \mu_2&\mu_3&\mu_4\\
 \mu_3&\mu_4&\mu_5
 \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Validated integration over twelve closed shift bins, together with an analytic
theta-index tail and a spatial tail beyond $h=4$, proves $G_a\pd$ uniformly for
$0\le a\le1/2$. The minimum third normalized LDL pivot is greater than
$0.09778764$. A complementary tail-matrix proof covers every positive shift,
and the independently reviewed even block through $H_4$ completes the finite
$H_5$ result.

This theorem is not pointwise positivity in the spatial variable. Indeed, the
pointwise odd $3\times3$ determinant is negative at $h=0$. Integration over the
complete theta profile repairs that local sign through this finite order. That
distinction foreshadows the signed core/tail mechanism used below.

\subsection{The odd block through channel 19}

At normalized shift $a=0$, the full odd block
\begin{equation}
 \Gamma_{19}=[\mu_{i+j+1}(0)]_{i,j=0}^{9}
\end{equation}
is positive definite. The proof uses the actual split at $H=1/16$. After exact
rational scaling and congruence, the core residual has three negative and five
positive directions. The actual tail residual is positive, and the complete

\begin{equation}
  T_{--}-600N\pd,
  \qquad 0.995T_{--}-T_{-+}(P+T_{++})^{-1}T_{+-}\pd.

Hence the tail pays the negative core and its complement coupling. A direct

The result is exactly the odd channels $1,3,\ldots,19$ at $a=0$. It does not
include the even block through degree $18$, does not prove full $H_{19}$, and
does not give positive-shift or growing-degree control.

\section{A rigorous failed route and the lesson of signed compensation}

\subsection{Universal cumulative-tail positivity is false}

Let $T_{H,a}$ denote the normalized spatial tail beginning at $H$. At
\begin{equation}
 H=1,\qquad t_*=\frac{7005081715}{10^6},\qquad \xi_*=2t_*=14010.16343,
\end{equation}
the full-theta Arb/DFT certificate and analytic alias, truncation, and
periodization bounds prove
\begin{equation}
 e^{\pi t_*/2}\widehat T_{1,0}(2t_*)<-53766891745349<0.
 \label{eq:a0-tail}
\end{equation}
An independent positive-shift certificate proves
\begin{equation}
 e^{\pi t_*/2}\frac{\widehat T_{1,10^{-4}}(2t_*)}{10^{-4}}
 <-51718939276867<0.
 \label{eq:apos-tail}
\end{equation}
The computation evaluates the complete theta sum, not a finite-orbit
surrogate. These inequalities refute the stronger claim that every cumulative
tail is positive. They do not give a negative value for the complete bridge
kernel and are not counterexamples to RH.

\subsection{Positive heat retains the obstruction, but the full sum can be positive}

CP62 extends \eqref{eq:apos-tail} to true positive heat. Put
\begin{equation}
 \varepsilon_- =\frac{51718939276867}{3^{14012}},\qquad
 \varepsilon_+ =\frac{3194699959754}{3^{14012}}.
\end{equation}
For $a=10^{-4}$, $0\le\tau\le\varepsilon_-/8$, and
$|\xi-\xi_*|\le\varepsilon_-/8$, the $H=1$ tail Fourier density is less than
$-\varepsilon_-/2$. On the narrower $\varepsilon_+$ rectangle, the complete
$H=0$ density is greater than $\varepsilon_+/2$. Thus the central term is
strictly positive and compensates the negative tail on the same actual
spectral band.

Moreover, the truncated-tail convolution form has arbitrarily large negative
subspaces even on the union of reflection-even compactly supported smooth
sources. Any correction factoring through finitely many fixed linear
functionals has a nonzero kernel on a sufficiently large negative subspace and
cannot make the entire tail nonnegative. This closes a finite-rank tail-repair
shortcut while leaving correlated infinite-rank and restricted-source
strategies open.

The conceptual lesson is positive: a viable argument must preserve the signed
central/tail pair and the complete covariance, rather than prove every piece
positive separately.

\section{A genuine bridge Fisher bound}

\subsection{The positive bridge law}

For $a>0$ define the probability density
\begin{equation}
 \rho_a(h)=\frac{h\sinh(2ah)\psi(h)^2}{C_a(0)},\qquad h>0,
\end{equation}
and put
\begin{equation}
 r_a(h)=2h\coth(2ah),\qquad
 s_a=r_a-\E_{\rho_a}r_a,\qquad
 I_a=\Var_{\rho_a}(r_a).
\end{equation}
The campaign's audited theta curvature bound is
$(\log\psi)''<-17$. If $U_a=-\log\rho_a$, then
\begin{equation}
 U_a''(h)=\frac1{h^2}+4a^2\operatorname{csch}^2(2ah)
          -2(\log\psi)''(h)>34.
\end{equation}
Also $0<r_a'(h)<2$. The one-dimensional Brascamp--Lieb variance inequality
\cite{BrascampLieb1976,CarlenEtAl2013}
therefore gives the reviewed theorem
\begin{equation}
 I_a\le \E_{\rho_a}\frac{(r_a')^2}{U_a''}\le\frac2{17}.
 \label{eq:fisher-2-17}
\end{equation}
For a finite vector observable $V(a,h)$, differentiation retains both pieces:
\begin{equation}
 \partial_a\E_{\rho_a}V
 =\E_{\rho_a}(\partial_aV)+\Cov_{\rho_a}(V,r_a),
\end{equation}
Writing $e_a(V):=\Cov_{\rho_a}(V,r_a)$ for the covariance contribution, under
any fixed whitening,
\begin{equation}
 e_a(V)e_a(V)^*\preceq I_a\Cov_{\rho_a}(V).
\end{equation}
The small coefficient $2/17$ is uniform in degree; the covariance it multiplies
is not. Calling \eqref{eq:fisher-2-17} a uniform high-jet relative error would
be incorrect.

\subsection{The regular endpoint}

The singular term $1/a$ in $r_a$ disappears after centering. In the regular
parameter $\lambda=a^2$,
\begin{equation}
 s_\lambda\longrightarrow\frac23(h^2-\E_{\rho_0}h^2),
 \qquad
 I_\lambda(0)=\frac49\Var_{\rho_0}(h^2).
\end{equation}
Integration of $(h^3\psi^2)'$ and the curvature bound give
$\E_{\rho_0}h^2\le3/34$. A second Brascamp--Lieb estimate yields
$\Var(h^2)\le3/289$, and hence
\begin{equation}
 I_\lambda(0)\le\frac4{867}.
 \label{eq:endpoint}
\end{equation}
This is a nondegenerate endpoint information constant. It is not a free
all-order bridge estimate.

\section{Why Fisher positivity is not bridge positivity}

Normalize the full-line tilt
\begin{equation}
 p_a(u)=\frac{e^{2au}\psi(u)^2}{Z(a)},
 \qquad Z(a)=\int_\R e^{2au}\psi(u)^2\,du.
\end{equation}
Then the parent $K_a/K_a(0)$ is the Hellinger affinity between the translates
$p_a(u-v)$ and $p_a(u+v)$. This explains its positive definiteness. It does not
explain the sign of its parameter derivative.

With
\begin{equation}
 B_j(a,u)=(-2)^j\frac{(D+a)^j\psi(u)}{\psi(u)},
\end{equation}
the parent jet Gram is $(Z/2)\E_p[BB^*]$. On either differential parity block,
integration by parts cancels the explicit feature-drift integral and leaves
the exact target
\begin{equation}
 H=\frac Z2\E_p[uBB^*].
 \label{eq:signed-u}
\end{equation}
The measure is positive but the weight $u$ is signed.

Assume the complete previous target block $A$ is positive. If $R$ is the actual
same-parity regression residual for the next feature, its Schur residual is
\begin{equation}
 S=\frac Z2\E_p[uR^2]
  =\frac Z2\left(m_a\E_pR^2+\frac12\Cov_p(R^2,\sigma_a)\right),
 \label{eq:signed-schur}
\end{equation}
where $m_a=\E_pu$, $\sigma_a=2(u-m_a)$, and
$I_p(a):=\Var_p(\sigma_a)=4\Var_p(u)$. Fisher Cauchy gives the sufficient
condition
\begin{equation}
 m_a\E_pR^2\ge\frac12\sqrt{I_p(a)\Var_p(R^2)},
\end{equation}
but no uniform-in-degree proof of this inequality is known, and the absolute
bound loses useful reflection cancellation near $a=0$.

The positive Stein kernel $\tau_a$ makes the unpaid term even more explicit:
\begin{equation}
 \frac{2S}{Z}=m_a\E_pR^2+2\E_p[\tau_aRR'].
 \label{eq:stein-cost}
\end{equation}
Curvature and semigroup relaxation control magnitudes and decay
\cite{Gentil2010}; they do not
assign a favorable sign to the last term.

\section{Paid twin-ladder innovation}

The sharp finite-dimensional reduction uses both genuinely positive reflected
sides. Fix $\varepsilon\in\{0,1\}$ and put
$q_j(z)=z^{\varepsilon+2j}$. For that parity let $G^\pm$ be the positive Grams
built from
\begin{equation}
 F_j^\pm(a,h)=\sqrt h\,e^{\pm ah}q_j(D\pm a)\psi(h),
\end{equation}
so the actual target is $(G^+-G^-)/2$. Extend the old block by one feature and
write $\alpha_\pm$ and $\delta_\pm>0$ for the two positive-side regression
coefficients and innovations. Put $A=A_+-A_-\pd$ and
$\Delta=\alpha_--\alpha_+$. Exact block algebra gives
\begin{equation}
 \widetilde S=\delta_+-\delta_- -\Delta^*K\Delta,
 \qquad
 K=A_-+A_-A^{-1}A_-=A_-A^{-1}A_+\pd.
 \label{eq:paid-innovation}
\end{equation}
Thus the favorable innovation difference must pay the mismatch between the two
optimal regression paths in the actual already-certified lower geometry.
Equation \eqref{eq:paid-innovation} is an exact restatement, not a new sign
theorem.

At the normalized $a=0$ endpoint, if a dot denotes differentiation at zero,
\begin{equation}
 \lim_{a\to0}\frac{S_{\rm actual}(a)}a
 =\dot\delta-\dot\alpha^*G(0)[\dot G(0)]^{-1}G(0)\dot\alpha.
 \label{eq:endpoint-innovation}
\end{equation}
The apparent $A^{-1}\sim1/a$ singularity cancels, leaving derivative innovation
minus regression-drift energy. A constructive sufficient path estimate is
\begin{equation}
 \delta_+-\delta_-
 \ge 2a\int_{-a}^{a}\alpha'(t)^*K\alpha'(t)\,dt,
 \label{eq:action}
\end{equation}
or a sharper matrix-path version. No uniform theta proof of
\eqref{eq:action} is presently known. This is the cleanest form of the central
regression-action obstruction.

\section{Orbit information relaxation and its limits}

Write the complete positive orbit decomposition on $h\ge0$ as
\begin{equation}
 p_n(h)=\frac{f_n(h)}{\psi(h)},\qquad
 S_n(h)=\partial_h\log f_n(h).
\end{equation}
The full-tail estimates prove $p_1>400/401$. Under positive transport from
$h$ to $h+s$, put $P=p(h)\otimes p(h)$ and
$Q=p(h+s)\otimes p(h+s)$. The one-orbit chi-square distance is less than
$1/400$, and the independent-pair distance is less than $801/160000$.
Therefore, for every finite vector observable $V$, with
$e_{P,Q}(V):=\E_QV-\E_PV$,
\begin{equation}
 e_{P,Q}(V)e_{P,Q}(V)^*\preceq\frac{801}{160000}\Cov_P(V),
 \label{eq:chi-cov}
\end{equation}
including after any fixed whitening. The covariance must remain in the bound.

The actual orbit Fisher information
\begin{equation}
 I(h)=\Var_p(S)=\sum_{n<m}p_np_m(S_n-S_m)^2
\end{equation}
satisfies the reviewed differential inequality
\begin{equation}
 I'(h)\le-14I(h),\qquad 0<I(h)<2e^{-14h}.
 \label{eq:orbit-fisher}
\end{equation}
Every pairwise Fisher summand contracts. Nevertheless, differentiated
observables may have covariance growing rapidly with degree. In fact the
modular-radius argument produces an unbounded subsequence on which first-orbit
approximation fails relatively. Small probability distance therefore does not
become a small relative high-jet error.

The exact graded orbit algebra has one polynomial channel and one residue
channel. That residue functional is unbounded relative to the raw discrete
polynomial Gram as degree grows. A separate fixed-parity sampling theorem shows
that continuous Hermite/Poisson $L^2$ cannot uniformly control lattice
evaluation. These are method guardrails, not negative bridge tests: the full
residue channel, spatial integration, and signed correlations may still repair
the target.

\section{Heat, Ornstein--Uhlenbeck, and Newman evolution}

\subsection{Spatial heat preserves the original zeros}

Let $\psi_s=e^{s\partial_u^2}\psi$. Its transform is
\begin{equation}
 F_s(z)=e^{-sz^2}F(z),
\end{equation}
so every zero and its multiplicity are unchanged. With
$E=e^{-2s(t^2-a^2)}$,
\begin{equation}
 M_s=EM,\qquad W_s=E(W+2saM).
\end{equation}
Equivalently,
\begin{equation}
 C_s=e^{2a^2s}e^{(s/2)\partial_v^2}(C_0+2saK_0).
\end{equation}
The positive term $2saK_0$ is a Gaussian reserve. Subtracting it gives
\begin{equation}
 C_s-2saK_s=e^{2a^2s}e^{(s/2)\partial_v^2}C_0,
\end{equation}
whose Fourier sign is equivalent to the original bridge sign. The reserve
cannot cure an off-axis zero: near a simple zero the negative term in $W$ is
linear in transverse displacement, whereas the reserve is quadratic.
Ornstein--Uhlenbeck relaxation likewise rescales nonreal zeros rather than
making them real.

\subsection{Newman forcing remains signed}

For the de Bruijn--Newman deformation \cite{RodgersTao2020},
\begin{equation}
 F_\lambda(z)=\int_0^\infty e^{\lambda u^2}\psi(u)\cos(zu)\,du,
\end{equation}
define
\begin{equation}
 W[G](t,a):=\Re\!\left(iG'(t+ia)\overline{G(t+ia)}\right),
 \qquad W_\lambda:=W[F_\lambda].
\end{equation}
Then one has spectral backward heat
\begin{equation}
 \partial_\lambda F_\lambda=-\partial_z^2F_\lambda.
\end{equation}
Product differentiation gives
\begin{equation}
 \partial_\lambda W_\lambda
 =-\partial_t^2W_\lambda+2W[F_\lambda'].
 \label{eq:newman-forcing}
\end{equation}
The derivative-ladder term $2W[F_\lambda']$ has no proved favorable sign.
It cannot be suppressed under the name of entropy dissipation.

For a polynomial $p$, define the signed quadratic forms
\begin{align}
 Q_\lambda[p]&:=\int_\R |p(t)|^2W[F_\lambda](t,a)\,dt,\\
 Q_\lambda^{[1]}[p]&:=\int_\R |p(t)|^2W[F_\lambda'](t,a)\,dt.
\end{align}
For the monic $Q_\lambda$-Schur residual polynomial $p_\lambda^*$, exact heat
transport of the test polynomial yields
\begin{equation}
 S_\lambda'=2\left(
 Q_\lambda^{[1]}[p_\lambda^*]
 -Q_\lambda[(p_\lambda^*)']\right).
 \label{eq:schur-flow}
\end{equation}
A safe positive Sobolev envelope pays both signed terms, leading to a finite
certificate template: certify the lower block along the interval, certify a
terminal reserve, and prove that the integrated positive energy budget is
smaller than that reserve. This template has not been closed uniformly in
degree.

\section{A positive death chain and a still-signed bridge}

The orbit marginals admit an exact time-inhomogeneous pure-death process. For
$m<n$ set
\begin{equation}
 q_{n,m}(h)=p_m(h)(S_m(h)-S_n(h)),
\end{equation}
with no upward jumps. The forward equation is precisely
$p_n'=p_n(S_n-\E S)$. This supplies a positive Markov realization and a
nonnegative jump energy, while retaining the signed moving-feature drift.

For a finite observable $V$, the compensated process has exact covariance cost
in any already-certified lower metric $A\pd$:
\begin{equation}
 \Gamma_u(V)(n):=\sum_{m<n}q_{n,m}(u)
 [V(m)-V(n)][V(m)-V(n)]^*\succeq0.
\end{equation}
Writing $M_t$ for the compensated jump martingale,
\begin{equation}
 \E[M_t^*A^{-1}M_t]
 =\int_s^t\E\operatorname{tr}(A^{-1}\Gamma_u(V))\,du.
\end{equation}
At a random spatial horizon with the genuine bridge density, the same process
gives an exact forcing representation for the Schur residual. It also gives an
exact conditional mismatch identity in which positive innovation and initial
or horizon covariance are subtracted from the raw second moment.

The pair-chain common-atom estimate proves a degree-independent conditional
innovation fraction greater than $0.508$ from $0$ to $1/16$ and greater than
$0.9955$ from $0$ to $1/4$. These are fractions of each terminal feature's own
conditional orbit covariance. They are not fractions of the bridge Schur
surplus, whose covariance and spatial means may grow with degree.

\section{The exact remaining lemma}

All successful routes now point to the same missing comparison.

\begin{obligation}[uniform innovation domination]
For every degree in each parity and every allowed shift, compare the positive
twin-ladder innovation and the exact orbit/death-chain innovation with the
actual complete lower-block metric, and prove that the resulting gain dominates
all of the following in one inequality:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the reflected innovation;
\item the regression mismatch $\Delta^*K\Delta$ or its endpoint drift form;
\item the positive-complement inverse and exceptional-direction feedback;
\item the signed spatial/parameter feature drift;
\item the Newman derivative-ladder forcing or its fully paid Sobolev envelope;
\item every shrinking reserve, switching, tail, and limiting/domain cost.
\end{enumerate}
The constants must remain uniform as the degree grows.
\end{obligation}

CP62 gives an equivalent finite-matrix interface. After the full positive
complement inverse and after subtracting the specified reserve, a congruence
splits the signed residual into a negative block $N$ and a positive block $P$.
The exact target is the angular Schur inequality
\begin{equation}
 \Omega_{--}-\Omega_{-+}(P+\Omega_{++})^{-1}\Omega_{+-}\succeq N.
 \label{eq:j8}
\end{equation}
A source-specific proof of \eqref{eq:j8}, or the equivalent paid innovation
inequality \eqref{eq:paid-innovation}, would be genuine progress. Writing the
identity, choosing a different anchor, or invoking Fisher positivity does not
prove it.

The current evidence narrows the target substantially:
\begin{itemize}
\item finite $H_5$ and odd-degree-19 certificates show that actual signed
  repair can work at fixed degree;
\item the $H=1$ obstruction forbids an arbitrary-tail positivity shortcut;
\item Fisher and orbit contraction provide real, explicit information gains;
\item regression and death-chain identities state exactly where those gains
  must enter;
\item residue and sampling obstructions forbid replacing the actual metric by
  simpler degree-uniform surrogates.
\end{itemize}
What remains is the quantitative theta-specific comparison itself.

\section{Reproducibility and parallel continuation}

The repository checkpoint contains the editable sources, exact evidence
manifests, certificate outputs, and bounded verification scripts. The default
archive verifier checks 574 retained v18 payload files and 34 CP62 files against
their source hashes. The dependency-light smoke suite reruns exact rational
checks for the $H_5$ analytic tail, fixed grouped Gram, zero window, confluent
cluster, and both CP62 algebra packets. It does not claim to rerun the Arb
$2^{21}$-point DFT, all interval quadratures, or every historical dependency.

Parallel work uses isolated worktrees. Evidence and provenance are frozen;
proof, experiment, audit, and paper lanes submit claim deltas to a single
integration branch. Numerical observations cannot be promoted to theorems
without a finite domain, arithmetic/error model, verifier, and replay receipt.
No internal audit is labeled peer review.

This manuscript was prepared with AI assistance for organization, derivation
checking, bounded replays, typesetting, and editing. Jude Gomila is the named
author and retains responsibility for the research claims. The evidence audits
are campaign-internal independent checks, not external refereeing.

\section*{Conclusion}

The theta--Fisher route has moved beyond a loose information-theoretic analogy.
It now has genuine bridge and orbit Fisher inequalities, exact paid regression
identities, a positive stochastic realization, and rigorous counterexamples
that prevent convenient but false shortcuts. The evidence also says precisely
why this is not yet a proof of RH: none of the information gains has been shown
to dominate the actual all-degree signed coupling cost. The next useful result
is therefore not another generic contraction theorem, but the source-specific,
degree-uniform innovation inequality in the complete bridge metric.
