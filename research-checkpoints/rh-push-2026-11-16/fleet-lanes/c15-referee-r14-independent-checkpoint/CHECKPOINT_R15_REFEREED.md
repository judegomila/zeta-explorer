# Checkpoint after round 14, refereed (classes on which the record is silent)

Written 2026-11-19 by `c15-referee-r14-independent-checkpoint` from `campaign/CHECKPOINT_R14_DRAFT.md`, after an
independent pass over round-14 lanes 1 to 6 (files REFEREE2_*.md in this directory). One referee. This is a
record of what the obstruction record does not cover. It is not a result about zeros of zeta, it is not a list of
leads, and the conjunction of its statements is not asserted. Codes as in Statement O version 3, section A.

Each statement: *if the decisive step of a device reads the set as described, no line of O and no closed device
row read by this pass applies to it.* "Entry test" = what a device must show on paper before any work.

**U3. Identification classes (stated first because U1 and U2 are cells of it).**
{FE of zeta, INT} (Hamburger 1921, Satz 1; absolute convergence for sigma > 1); degree one with FE, INT and the
Euler-product axiom (Kaczorowski-Perelli 1999); {POS, GEOM at every rational prime, INT} (O row 1b, Lemma I).
No control can lie in such a class. R3 is tested by relaxing one input inside a family where the conclusion is
false: INT (T-NR in the Laurent form and symmetric multipliers: zeta's own functional equation is kept exactly),
FE (U, THIN, DEL), FE at all heights (WT), GEOM at every prime (E_a), POS (sign twists).
Correction carried by this entry: zeta's own functional equation alone identifies nothing; controls on file have it.

**U1. {FE, GEOM, INT}, both used in one one-sided step of type X2.** Cell of U3.
Entry test: the step is not (i) an exact identity valid for the controls; (ii) only an identification; (iii)
stable under Blaschke factors when posed on a scattering coefficient; (iv) linear in the target or a division by
it; (v) a twist-transfer identity, where the multiplicativity consumed is that of the twisting character (TW);
(vi) on an Eisenstein carrier, a Hecke relation supplied by the wave group (Lemma Q) or holding identically in
the parameter (Lemma T). It must fail for T-NR (Laurent form) at the exact covariance at p0.
No candidate on file.

**U2. {FE, SIZE, POS at the log level, INT} in one inequality (type X1 with FE).** Cell of U3.
Entry test: the inequality is not a criterion (Weil, Li, Nyman-Beurling and map section 3.1 are restatements);
it is not read through finitely many ratios L(chi)/zeta (Lemma C: T-NR at a split prime passes). It must fail
for T-NR at the sign or size of the logarithmic coefficients at p0.
No candidate on file.

**U4. Joint non-cone functionals of zeta and twists, outside five closed types.**
Closed: (i) functions of finitely many ratios (T-NR at a split prime, Lemma C); (ii) Gram and Hankel-block forms
of the pair of unitary symbols (BLS); (iii) twist-transfer Weil forms (TW; DH, T-NR, E_a pass); (iv) dictionary
DJ of sign functionals homogeneous in each kernel (MEASURED, finite search; the outcome holds with the canonical
lifts T-NR, E_a, HEAT alone on the grid stage); (v) functionals invariant under a common pointwise scaling of the
members: pointwise quotients, cross-ratios, one-point triple expressions (HEAT with the same tau passes exactly;
Lemma J1', one derivation, unrefereed).
Open: joint functionals of none of these types, for example quotients at different points, a logarithm of one
member alone, kernels with polar terms, triples read through the group law at several points. Lift rules for any
search: LIFT_RULING.md.

**U5. FE entering through a number of conditions that grows with the height.** Unchanged from the draft.
Between "a fixed number of windows" (WT; rows P40, S12) and "a mean value" (line 10) the record has no row.

**U6. Attached objects outside the closed carriers.**
(a) Hecke eigen-objects with parameter rho: rank >= 2 non-Borel or metaplectic objects. In rank one (any cover,
level, vector) the LRS-type count is closed (pole minus gamma pole equals 1; relative to an unopened source for
the pole position) and the twelve catalogued inequalities are closed for torus parameters; isobaric sums with
cuspidal data are argued closed, not proved. A device that reads theta-orthogonality must fix the level or read
the Whittaker functional on the whole representation (control NR-vec, with the repaired definition), and must
use Lemma 1' (the factor zeta(u) in every square-index coefficient), not "b(1) = 0".
(b) Lax-Phillips norms: non-solid norms outside the Hecke-sup, L^q and zero-mode families, and norms on invariant
subspaces smaller than the mean-zero subspace.
(c) Global Whittaker constants of a genuine cusp form on the double cover of GL(3). Unchanged.
(d) Rank >= 2 compact-at-infinity squares without a Hecke rule. Unchanged.
(e) States of the Bost-Connes system outside the three classes of row D8. Unchanged.
(f) Functionals of the unsummed complex isogenous points that are neither linear nor a second moment. Unchanged.
(g) Scattering inequalities that read an exact normalisation, the archimedean factor, or f(z) = f(1 - z); rank
above one; the two-variable affine kernel. Schur, bounded-analytic and Pick conditions on a zero-type coefficient
are closed (BLS).
First controls: EIS-w and BLS for (a), (b), (g); T-NR for (e), (f).

**U7. Sections of the prime chain with P(t) growing like a power of t; J-expansive sections.** Unchanged.
First control DH; none run.

**U8. Single-object kernel conditions beyond the searched range (order above 4, more than three parameters, nodes
outside [-2.6, 2.6], other normalisations).**
Excluded from the class by proved statements: minors on one lattice (T-NR with c >= 2); Polya-frequency orders of
the xi-kernel (E_a); monomial inequalities prod K(u_i)^(e_i) <= lam with real values, lam >= 0 and
sum e_i u_i^2 >= 0, for kernels without polar terms (backward heat inherits them: the monomial is multiplied by
exp(tau sum e_i u_i^2 / 4) in (0, 1]; one derivation, unrefereed).
