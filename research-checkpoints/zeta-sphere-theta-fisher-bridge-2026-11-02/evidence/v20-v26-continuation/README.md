without replacing or rewriting `../v18-curated/` or `../v19-delta/`.

source allowlists. Together they contain 174 rows for 146 unique authored
mathematical, audit, certificate, and clearly labelled diagnostic files; the
28 repeated rows are byte-identical dependency carry-forwards. Of the 146
public payloads, 139 are byte-identical to source and seven version indexes
have only their private Library identifiers replaced by prior-archive
SHA-256 values. The source and public hashes for those changes are recorded in
`../../provenance/v20-v26-redactions.json`.
