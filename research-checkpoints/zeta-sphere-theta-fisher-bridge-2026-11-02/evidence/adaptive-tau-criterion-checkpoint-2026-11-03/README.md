# Adaptive-τ oscillator criterion: research checkpoint

This append-only, AI-assisted analytic checkpoint extends the separate
fixed-τ oscillator obstruction. It is not peer review or a machine-formalized
proof. The historical internal-review status in the preserved note is a source
claim, not an additional independent certification.

At a=0, for each parity separately, either a signed old Gram fails to be
positive at some finite level, or from some level onward no choice of τ>0,
including τ varying with degree, satisfies the particular absolute-Cauchy
sufficient criterion √U_N(τ)+ρ_N(τ)<1/τ. This is not a failed selected Schur
pivot, a disproof of bridge positivity or RH, an inverse blow-up result, or an
obstruction at positive shift. A genuinely correlated estimate, including one
using a varying τ, remains possible.

The proof uses one fixed old test to cover small τ and a finite collection of
polynomial localizers, obtained by compactness from a short real-zero gap, to
cover larger τ uniformly. Its dependencies are the form criterion, centered
oscillator identity, and zero-gap argument preserved in the adjacent
`fixed-tau-oscillator-checkpoint-2026-11-03` lane. As stated there, the
shrinking-gap argument depends on Conrey's 1989 positive-proportion theorem for
simple critical-line zeros; the external zero result was not recomputed here.
See https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1989.399.1 for the publisher record. The
source PDF was inaccessible in this environment and search-indexed excerpts
were used to check the statement.

This stronger criterion-specific conclusion narrows the earlier statement
that changing τ was not ruled out by the fixed-τ argument; it does not rule
out varying τ in other methods or alter any earlier result.
