# Primary-literature scope audit: no novelty claim

2 November 2026. Read the actual cited primary texts, not just abstracts. This is a bounded audit of relevant hypotheses, not an exhaustive novelty search or a re-proof of the literature.

1. Suzuki, arXiv1204.1827v2, §§2,4.1,5 (source already preserved in v8). The explicit canonical-system theorem assumes omega>1. Finite compression compactness for every omega>0 is discussed; the small-omega all-support contraction strategy in §5 uses innerness/RH. Local norm-smallness for sufficiently small support follows routinely from the integrable kernel. Our pre-threshold small-support observation is therefore not presented as a new general phenomenon.

2. Suzuki, *Hamiltonians arising from L-functions in the Selberg class*, arXiv1606.05726v3 (1 October2020), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.05726 . Theorem2.1 and Proposition4.2 assume nu*omega*d_L>1 and prove unconditional invertibility only on some initial interval[0,tau). Proposition5.1 proves all-support strict contraction, but §5.1 explicitly assumes E in HB (equivalently the relevant innerness); that assumption is unconditional above the half-shift and conditional on GRH for small shifts. Removing that subsection hypothesis would be an error. Our nu=1,omega≈.4 zeta kernel does not satisfy the regularity hypothesis nu*omega>1.

3. Suzuki, *Integral operators arising from the Riemann zeta function*, arXiv1907.07302v2 (6 March2020), https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.07302 . Introduction and Theorem1.1 distinguish local unconditional determinant nonvanishing from the global RH-dependent statement. Theorem1.1 assumes the determinant interval rather than proving it globally. Its separate exponential-log-derivative kernel uses theta>1 and is not our nu=1 small-shift kernel.

4. Suzuki, *Chains of reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces generated by unimodular functions*, arXiv2012.11121v2 (3 October2023), https://arxiv.org/pdf/2012.11121 . Condition(O1) is a strict finite-compression norm hypothesis. In §3.4 the L-function application assumes both innerness and nu*omega*d_L>1 before identifying the abstract unimodular multiplier operator with the right-half-plane arithmetic kernel. A real-axis unimodular multiplier is not automatically the same causal arithmetic kernel when innerness is unknown. Thus abstract conjugation is not an unconditional bypass.

What v8 adds to this bounded comparison is an explicit numerical interval and resolvent constant for the actual weakly singular nu=1 kernel crossing n=2, with detailed certificate assumptions. We do not claim that no prior author has computed such an interval. No all-support continuation, determinant regularization, or RH consequence follows.
