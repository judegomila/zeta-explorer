## 2. Adaptation rules

- An explore lane (from version 6: an inverse-explore lane) that passes stage A (no control passes every step) becomes the lead of section 4.0 in the next round and receives 3 lanes: one for the proof step, one for the controls run numerically on the server, one independent referee of the control table. The other slots shrink in the order compute (items 2 and 3), combine, deepen.
- The same three-lane rule applies when a deepen or combine lane produces a non-identity statement that no control on file passes at a named step (referee-confirmed R3), and in particular to a quantitative form of POS-FE (section 4.0): an inequality that bounds the distance of the zeros of G from the line by the size of the negative part of Lambda_G. ## 3. Stopping rule for a family

Two refereed obstructions of the same kind close a family (tag pair). A closed family is reopened only by a new input named in the lane plan (a new theorem, a new 2026 result, or a tool listed as missing in the closing record). One refereed obstruction marks the family "tried, not closed": one more lane is allowed, and it must attack the stated obstruction, not repeat the route. A lane whose angle dies pivots once to the strongest adjacent idea; it does not cycle.

Campaign-level: the campaign does not stop on a claimed proof; a score-4 lane goes to three referees and all controls. It does stop spending on a lead after its second same-kind obstruction.
