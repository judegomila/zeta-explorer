# Actual near-null spectral head: analytic proof-use package

This is a separate analytic module, not a finite-support positivity computation. Its result concerns the literal finite arithmetic image of an explicitly selected signed-positive prolate head. It establishes an eventual small **actual supported Weil operator action**, a rank lower bound, and the corresponding physical head/bridge estimates. It does not establish a lower bound on the whole physical complement, identify the ground state, or prove RH.

## Included evidence

`DEPENDENCY_INVENTORY.json` identifies 18 selected current proof-use units and 54 original-to-published source/audit files. All selected mathematical source sections are retained. The inventory gives the exact limited use of each source and the dependency edges; a result imported only for a spectral-row estimate is not promoted to an unrelated stronger energy theorem. Every source has its corresponding internal mathematical audit and audit receipt or source-hash receipt.

The proof chain separates:

1. The signed-positive finite-Fourier head and physical coordinate map.
2. The full mean-zero output Gram, with physical main reserve, compact channel limits, collar bounds and Hardy estimates.
3. Polynomial-deficit action-strip, endpoint leakage, and complete divided differences.
4. The restricted spectral row and weighted exterior Fourier tail.
5. The radical source domain and actual tail-to-window operator estimate.
6. Bandwidth-scale origin/endpoint repairs, followed by the uniform critical power-four cutoff.

The Gram is used for injectivity and output normalization only. It is never substituted for the actual operator-action estimate. The physical projection is the orthogonal projection in logarithmic-window L2, not an inverse-evenization coordinate projector.

## Availability and review scope

**Analytic reproducibility/source availability: partial.** All 18 selected current sources and their paired audit notes/receipts are included. Their classical analytic inputs are explicitly named; those published proofs are not reprinted. Auxiliary exact-scalar check receipts referenced within audits, earlier historical dependency manifests, and every prior proof variant are not exhaustively included. There is no runnable numerical certificate in this unit and no claim of a self-contained formal proof. The module displays the deep analytic lemmas as inputs and derives the head theorem from them.

Historical producer headings can say that review was requested. These are retained to avoid silently rewriting the research record; the paired audit and the module's stated current scope determine acceptance. Internal independent review is not external peer review or a second formalization.

## Deliberate separation

The complete finite `X = 16001/1000` spectral replay is a different review unit with different proof machinery. It does not depend on this asymptotic head construction. Conversely, the near-null-head theorem does not enlarge that finite positivity endpoint or supply the missing complement floor. The signed-complement module can cite this theory package for its inherited actual-head estimate, but its all-complement problem stays open.

Superseded trace-null and iterated-log output-Gram routes, obsolete trace/jet obstructions, old low-prefix frames, canonical-ground comparisons, pending work, large numerical arrays, and private research context are excluded. The focused inventory is not a claim that every historical reference is an independent input to the current theorem.
