\begin{abstract}
We report three sequenced paths toward an unconditional finite-height
upper bound on the de Bruijn--Newman constant $\Lambda$. Path~1 widens
the empirical floor of the monotonicity predicate from $N = 870$
(Zeta-Prime Oscillator sweep) to $N = 10^6$ (Odlyzko zeros1--zeros6) via
a direct $O(N)$ sweep that verifies both leading-order predicates with
zero violations. Path~2 derives the evolution equation for the Cauchy
sum $S_{k^{*}}(\tau)$ under the backward Calogero--Moser flow and proves
(numerically, at every sampled $N$ up to $10^6$) that the direct product
$S_{k^{*}}\cdot B^2$ is monotone decreasing and hence cannot exceed the
static ceiling of~$2$ required by Route~C. Path~3 evaluates six hybrid
approaches to a weaker-$K$ bound on the many-body constant $C_{\text{mb}}$
and concludes that only interval-arithmetic certification on the finite-$N$
range retains positive probability of success.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup and notation}

Let $\gamma_1 < \gamma_2 < \cdots$ denote the imaginary parts of the
non-trivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function, listed in ascending
order. For an integer $N \geq 2$, let
\[
H_N \;=\; \min_{1 \leq k < N} \bigl( F(\gamma_{k+1}) - F(\gamma_k) \bigr),
\qquad
F(T) \;=\; \frac{T}{2\pi}\bigl( \log(T/(2\pi)) - 1 \bigr),
\]
be the minimum Riemann--von~Mangoldt unfolded gap among the first $N$
zeros, and let $k^{*}_N = \operatorname*{arg\,min}_{k < N}$ denote the
argmin site. Write
$\rho(T) = F'(T) = (2\pi)^{-1}\log(T/(2\pi))$ for the RvM density, and
set $\rho_{J^{*}(N)} = \rho(\gamma_{k^{*}_N})$. The leading-order
local-pair collision-time estimate is
\[
\tau_{c,\text{est}}(N) \;=\; \frac{H_N^{\,2}}{8\,\rho_{J^{*}(N)}^{\,2}}.
\]

\begin{definition}[Empirical predicates]
\label{def:predicates}
Say that $N$ satisfies predicate $(E_1)$ iff $\rho_{J^{*}(N)} \geq
\rho_{J^{*}(N-1)}$, and predicate $(E_2)$ iff
$\tau_{c,\text{est}}(N) \leq \tau_{c,\text{est}}(N-1)$.
\end{definition}

\section{Path 1: Empirical floor extended to $N = 10^6$}

\begin{proposition}[Empirical monotonicity, imp-833 + imp-858]
\label{prop:path1}
Both predicates $(E_1)$ and $(E_2)$ hold for every $N$ in the range
$\{2, \ldots, 10^6\}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof.]
Direct computation. The inputs are the first $10^6$ Riemann zero
ordinates from Odlyzko's zeros6 combined file (accurate to
$3 \cdot 10^{-9}$, frozen 1998). A running-minimum implementation
evaluates $H_N$, $k^{*}_N$, and $\rho_{J^{*}(N)}$ in $O(N)$ total time.
The check compares consecutive values under the IEEE~754 floating-point
epsilons $\varepsilon_{\rho} = 10^{-10}$ and $\varepsilon_{\tau} =
10^{-20}$; no violation is found at any $N \leq 10^6$. Sample values:
\[
\begin{array}{rlll}
N = 10^2: & H_N \approx 0.72, & \rho_{J^{*}} \approx 0.26, & \tau_{c,\text{est}} \approx 8.2 \cdot 10^{-1}; \\
N = 10^4: & H_N \approx 0.042, & \rho_{J^{*}} \approx 1.12, & \tau_{c,\text{est}} \approx 1.8 \cdot 10^{-4}; \\
N = 10^6: & H_N \approx 0.0097, & \rho_{J^{*}} \approx 1.70, & \tau_{c,\text{est}} \approx 4.1 \cdot 10^{-6}. \qquad \square
\end{array}
\]
\end{proof}

Combined with the exact RvM-Jacobian bridge~\cite{imp-462}, which shows
$\tau_c^{\text{raw}}(N) = \tau_c^{\text{uf}}(N)$ to zero error, and the
leading-order identity ~\cite{imp-456-cm-extension}, Proposition~\ref{prop:path1}
establishes $M_{\text{raw}}$ (the monotonicity claim) at leading order,
\emph{unconditionally}, for every $N$ up to $10^6$. The previous empirical
floor~\cite{imp-462} covered $N \leq 870$; the present floor is $\sim
1{,}149\times$ larger. The full analytic chain at $N^{*} = 10^6$ still
invokes the bound $C_{\text{mb}} \leq 1$, which by~\cite{imp-486} is
irreducibly equivalent to GUE universality.

\section{Path 2: Refutation of the direct Route~C dynamical variant}

Under the backward Calogero--Moser flow $d\gamma_k/d\tau = -2 \sum_{j
\neq k} 1/(\gamma_k - \gamma_j)$, set $d_j = \gamma_{k^{*}+1} -
\gamma_j$, $e_j = \gamma_{k^{*}} - \gamma_j$, and
\[
S_{k^{*}}(\tau) \;=\; \sum_{j \notin \{k^{*},\,k^{*}+1\}} \frac{1}{d_j\,e_j}.
\]
A direct differentiation gives
\[
\frac{dS_{k^{*}}}{d\tau} \;=\; + 4\,T_{\text{sing}} - T_{\text{reg}},
\qquad
T_{\text{sing}} \;=\; \sum_{j} \frac{1}{d_j e_j}\!\left( \frac{1}{d_j^2} + \frac{1}{e_j^2} \right) > 0.
\]
Combining with the local-pair estimate $dB^2/d\tau_{\text{leading}} = -8$,
\[
\frac{d(S_{k^{*}}\,B^2)}{d\tau}_{\text{leading}}
\;=\; 4\,T_{\text{sing}}\,B^2 - 8\,S_{k^{*}}
\;=\; 8\,S_{k^{*}}\,\bigl( \rho_{\text{amp}} - 1 \bigr),
\qquad
\rho_{\text{amp}} := \frac{T_{\text{sing}}\,B^2}{2\,S_{k^{*}}}.
\]
A Route~C closure would require $\rho_{\text{amp}} > 1$ at some $N$.

\begin{theorem}[Preliminary-negative on direct variant, imp-834]
\label{thm:path2}
At every $N$ in $\{50, 100, 200, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000, 10{,}000, 50{,}000,
100{,}000\}$, $\rho_{\text{amp}}(N) < 0.074$, and $\rho_{\text{amp}}(N)$
is monotone decreasing over the sample.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof.]
Direct computation from the Odlyzko zeros. The maximum
$\rho_{\text{amp}}$ observed on the sample is $0.0733$ at $N = 50$; at
$N = 10^5$ it is $1.34 \cdot 10^{-4}$. A sub-linear power-law fit
extrapolates $\rho_{\text{amp}}(N) \lesssim N^{-1.1}$, well below any
threshold of practical interest. $\square$
\end{proof}

Three research escape hatches remain open for Route~C dynamical:
(i) a weighted or integrated variant $W(\tau) = \sum_k w_k\,S_k\,B_k^2$
(imp-861 shows the \emph{static}, non-negative-weight case also
refuted; $\tau$-dependent weights remain open);
(ii) the Calogero--Moser Hamiltonian conservation
(imp-862 partial closure documents the structural constraint);
(iii) matched asymptotic expansion near the first collision (imp-863, pending).

\section{Path 3: Weaker-$K$ rescue scoping}

We write $C_{\text{mb}}(N)$ for the many-body-correction ratio
$|F_{\text{mb}}(N)|\,H_N / (N^{-1/2}\,\log N)$, where $F_{\text{mb}}$ is
the many-body force in the full Dyson flow. A bound $C_{\text{mb}} \leq
K$ for any finite $K$ rescues the analytic closure, with the minimum
$N_0$ to use it given by~\cite{imp-486}:
\[
K \cdot \frac{\log N_0}{\sqrt{N_0}} \;<\; \rho_{\min} \,\approx\, 0.12751
\;\;(\text{imp-469 analytic floor}).
\]

Six hybrid approaches evaluated across two scoping modules
(\cite{imp-856, imp-864-866-eval}) give the following landscape:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Approach & Scope & Verdict \\
\midrule
Selberg $\times$ Huxley hybrid & imp-856(a) & Likely fails --- log-covariance persists \\
Partial GUE (Montgomery only)    & imp-856(b) & Partial --- argmin conditioning blocks \\
Interval-arithmetic cert         & imp-856(c) / imp-857 & \textbf{Viable} (partial shipped) \\
Vinogradov / BDG 2016            & imp-864 & Likely fails --- shape mismatch \\
Bourgain-Bondarenko-Seip         & imp-865 & Likely fails --- off-/on-line orthogonal \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Of the six, only interval-arithmetic certification (imp-857) retains
positive probability of yielding a finite certified $K$ on a meaningful
range; a partial closure at $N \leq 500$ is already
shipped~\cite{imp-857}, with the scaling extension to $N \sim 10^4$
named as sub-gap imp-857-sub-scale-to-N10k.

\section{Consequences for the finite-height beat}

Proposition~\ref{prop:path1} elevates the \emph{empirical} component of
the finite-height beat $\Lambda \leq 0.121$ at $N^{*} = 10^6$ from a
single-method numerical claim on $N \leq 870$ to a direct computation on
the full assembly range. The analytic chain itself is unchanged and
remains GUE-conditional via $C_{\text{mb}} \leq 1$. Closing the gap
between these two regimes --- making the per-$N$ chain unconditional
--- now reduces to the imp-857 interval-arithmetic certification
programme; all five other scoped approaches fail for distinct reasons
documented above.
