The checker covers every σ∈[7/8,1], t∈[100,101], equivalently a∈[3/8,1/2]. It uses the actual Euler–Maclaurin head N=128, m=20 and the explicit actual ζ,ζ′ remainder bounds from ../modular_transfer_v1/ACTUAL_COMPLETED_HEAD_TRANSFER.md. No numerical zeta function is called.

## Enclosures

All coefficients B_(2k) are generated by an exact rational recurrence, then outward-enclosed using mpmath.iv at 30 decimal digits. Every logarithm, exponential, complex head term and head derivative is outward interval arithmetic. Domain endpoints are dyadic rationals, including 7/8; recursive midpoint bisection stays exact in the higher-precision endpoint representation for all depths used.

For w=z/2 with Re w>0, comparison of the defining digamma sum to its integral gives
|Re Ψ(w)−log|w||≤∫_0^∞dx/|x+w|²≤π/(2|Im w|)=π/|t|.
This does not require Re w≥1. Consequently
b_G∈2σ/(σ²+t²)+2(σ−1)/((σ−1)²+t²)
       +(1/2)log[(σ²+t²)/4]−logπ+[-π/t,π/t].
The checker uses this full enclosure, retaining the signed head cross term Re(H′ conjugate H).

The bound 2ζ(40)<4 replaces the Bernoulli-tail factor conservatively. Its rising factorial contains exactly 40 factors; its derivative bound contains all 40 reciprocal factors. The computed cost is precisely E in the reviewed transfer theorem. A rectangle is accepted only if the entire interval

(b_G/2)|H|²+Re(H′ conjugate H)−E

has positive lower endpoint. The recursive closed rectangles cover the original domain with shared boundaries; a depth limit produces failure, never acceptance. certificate.json records completion and the least accepted lower endpoint.
