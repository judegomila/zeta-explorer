# Stage-2 design: signed paid-twin border falsifier

## Purpose

The next bounded computation is not another degree sweep. It is a
theorem-design falsifier for the signed Schur inequality in `RESULTS.md`. Its
job is to expose the actual target-metric forcing innovations rung by rung,
verify the exact inverse-energy decomposition on already certified endpoint
rungs, and reject any proposed proof step that deletes or absolutizes the
modular cross term.

Finite success would validate algebra and rule out overly strong lemmas. It
would not prove the all-degree inequality or RH.

## Implementation status

Mode 1 is complete as a SHA-bound **midpoint diagnostic**, with primary and
180-digit replay outputs. It exposes the target LDL pivots, forcing
innovations and each `eta_k^2/s_k` payment for the certified odd rungs 3--19.
The archived intervals, not the new midpoint coordinates, remain the finite
certificate. `analysis-stage2.json` validates the queue, source/input/result
hashes, receipts, execution events and replay stability.

Modes 2 and 3 are not implemented. In particular, the original `16/24` fixed
orbit proposal is unsafe for direct evaluation on the negative side at large
positive `h`; it must use an audited modular transform or a validated adaptive
cutoff. A future direct-channel manifest must also state its spatial domain,
quadrature/tail rules, stabilization tolerances, fixed regression coefficients
and which of the three off-diagonal `(1,rho,kappa)` terms each ablation changes.

  constructs floating target pivots and checks the normalized prefix identity

- `evidence/v20-v26-continuation/modular_innovation_payment_v1/probe_payment.py`

The archived scripts hardcode `/workspace/shared` paths and must not be run
verbatim. A new worker must use local, manifest-bound relative inputs.

## Minimal worker

Add `src/paid_twin_border_falsifier.py` with three explicit modes.

### 1. Certified endpoint reconstruction

For the odd rungs 3 through 19:

1. SHA-bind the exact EGF manifest, parent interval data, actual target
   moments, and archived endpoint certificate.
2. Reconstruct the positive-parent regression, target block, forcing column
   `w`, and innovation derivative.
3. Factor the old actual target block as
   `A=L diag(s_k) L^T` and set `eta=L^-1 w`.
4. Verify

   \[
   w^TA^{-1}w=\sum_k\frac{\eta_k^2}{s_k},
   \qquad
   \sigma_n=\dot\delta_n-\sum_k\frac{\eta_k^2}{s_k}.
   \]

5. Require every reconstructed gain, cost, and surplus to overlap its archived
   certified interval.

This adds the missing rung-by-rung forcing coordinates without changing the
already certified finite claim.

### 2. Direct modular-channel audit

Generate the exact graded polynomials through grade 19. Convert each paid
regression residual and opposite-parity drift into their polynomial channels
and the two linked residues `rho` and `kappa`, generated by `-C(t)` and
`-tC(t)`. At every positive quadrature node compare independently:

- direct theta derivative sums;
- `F_A=Psi_A-ell(A) chi`;
- the exact Hadamard reconstruction;
- reflected parity.

Recompute

\[
\dot\delta=2\langle hr,r\rangle+2\langle r,k\rangle
\]

and every component of `w` while retaining all signed cross terms. Evaluate
the full signed form and two ablations: cross term deleted, and cross term
replaced by its absolute value. A negative ablated margin falsifies that proof
route; it is an experimental result, not a worker failure.

### 3. Optional finite-shift replay

On the already studied `N=2,4,5`, `a=1/4` cases, factor
`A=A_+-A_-`, decompose `w=A_- Delta`, and verify

\[
\Delta^T K\Delta
=\Delta^TA_-\Delta+\sum_k\frac{\eta_k^2}{s_k}.
\]

## Hard gates

- Every bound input hash and the EGF exact-pass field match.
- Direct derivative, one-pole, parity, and Hadamard identities agree.
- Every old actual-target LDL pivot is positive.
- Direct inverse cost agrees with `sum eta_k^2/s_k`.
- Direct and matrix evaluations of `dot-delta`, `w`, and `sigma` agree.
- Primary/replay logarithmic metrics stabilize.
- Archived certified intervals contain the reconstructed endpoint quantities.
- The raw/half-difference factor-of-two convention is recorded and checked
  against both archived implementations.

## Bounded queue and resource contract

- Primary: 100 decimal digits, spatial step `1/64`, 16 theta orbits.
- Replay: 160 decimal digits, spatial step `1/128`, 24 theta orbits.
- One worker per task; single-threaded libraries; 600-second cooperative phase
  guard; 512 MiB design envelope; 4 MiB output cap.
- Estimated total: under 10 core-minutes.
- Use a new task manifest and run directory under this workstream. Launch only
  through the existing lock/load governor after revalidating the protected 4CT
  PID, command, and exact working directory.

## Known blockers

- Archived certified endpoint data cover only the odd ladder through degree
  19; there is no comparable stored high-degree even certificate.
- The first modular-channel integration would be a floating falsification
  diagnostic. A new certificate would require porting the rigorous spatial and
  orbit tail machinery; a tiny last term is not a tail proof.
- The stage-1 center interpolants are adversarial signed-pair probes, not
  paid-twin regression residuals, and cannot be substituted into this border
  recurrence.

The analytic goal remains the arbitrary-degree signed Schur inequality. This
stage is justified only as a guard against pursuing a false factorization.
