# Theorem-status ledger

## Established within the stated models or internally checked calculations

- **Fixed support, all modes:** the arithmetic Weil form on support `[-6/5,6/5]` has the internally replayed certificate `Q(f) >= 10^-55 ||f||_2^2`. The high complement is included; this is not merely a finite Ritz test. The resulting canonical shifted-xi bound is `||H_(omega,6/5)|| <= exp(-9e-56 omega)` for `0 < omega <= 10^-29`.
- **Information-relaxation model:** with a fixed standard-Gaussian equilibrium and two damping rails of total budget one, the sharp all-law, all-time, prefactor-one relative-entropy rate is `2 min(sigma,1-sigma)`. Balance uniquely maximizes this rate. Arnold–Erb already imply this exact specialization. It is not a new theorem about where zeta zeros must lie.
- **Actual-xi probability family:** the real exponential tilt of the positive xi density has cumulant curvature equal to its variance/Fisher information. Its relative entropy to the central member is uniquely minimized at the central member. The family appears in Nakamura; the KL identities are standard exponential-family consequences. Neither Fisher information itself nor Shannon entropy has thereby been proved minimized at balance.
- **Fixed-support duality:** finite outside-support null cosines form a complete lower-certificate language. A fixed frequency lattice instead gives a periodic-envelope Toeplitz relaxation. Adding off-lattice constraints recovers fixed-support completeness, without a rate or uniform complexity bound.
- **Network transform:** the exact Weil form can be reorganized into positive jump energy, a signed multiplication defect, and a signed rank-two pole contribution. The pole introduces a negative variance term when absorbed into the scalar row. The required global weighted inequality remains unproved.

## Negative or limited results

- At `L=5/4`, both the specified `N=160` and `N=180` sufficient comparisons failed. A rigorously negative fixed lower-model trial is not actual-Weil-form negativity: its redistributed masses have no certified continuum comparison error.
- At `L=11/20`, period `64 pi` cannot give a nonnegative scalar null-certificate, even with every allowed harmonic. A negative relaxed measure need not be the Fourier square of an admissible supported function.
- At period `128 pi`, adding the one fixed term `-(1/10) cos(4t/3)` improves the full periodic relaxation but leaves a negative relaxed upper bound. This rules out positivity only for that fixed coefficient and old lattice, not all coefficients or all frequencies.
- The weights `1` and `cosh(x/2)` have negative completed operator rows already at `L=1/5` and `L=6/5`. A negative row is not a negative quadratic-form value.
- Phase-flow pictures, dissipative vortex trajectories, and de Bruijn–Newman heat-flow pictures do not combine into a zero-location proof. Fixed-field trajectories and moving zeros of a heat-deformed function are different objects.

## Not established

No RH proof; no new `7/8` zero-free result; no all-support positive reserve; no transfer of the older `0.03` relative-leakage reserve to support `6/5`; no state-of-the-art or literature-priority claim; no external peer review or formal proof.

The concrete missing arithmetic task is an all-support lower bound with the required uniform, subexponential loss, or an equivalent exact signed-network/storage inequality. Fixed-support positivity, an abstract complete dual, and optimal relaxation in an auxiliary positive Gaussian model do not supply that bound.
