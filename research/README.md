# Twistor research packages

These packages accompany the rigorous twistor model. Each distinguishes proved identities, local certificates, conditional targets, and unresolved global estimates. They do not establish RH or a new global de Bruijn–Newman bound.

- [Projective/dual twistor bridge and signed Hankel certificate](twistor-bridge-2026-10-03/README.md): the exact width-3/2 bridge, constant-mode obstruction, all-window criterion, and the separate heat-time-zero certificate near arithmetic width 1/4.
- [Heat-compatible fibers and scalar freeze supplement](heat-fiber-freeze-2026-10-03/README.md): the exact heat correction and conditional completion theorem, one positive-area scalar cell at 40/60-digit precision, and a reviewed far-tail proof for index N ≥ 10^17. The target 0.143637595 remains conditional; the enormous finite coverage gap and inherited barrier replay remain open.

The existing interactive model and its certificate controls are unchanged by the heat supplement. Heat time, arithmetic width, harmonic index, incidence line, and finite Hankel truncation length are distinct parameters.
