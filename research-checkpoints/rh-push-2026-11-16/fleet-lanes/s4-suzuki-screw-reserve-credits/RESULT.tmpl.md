## 1. Shared definitions and validation

For an object F with completed function N^{s/2} prod_j Gamma_R(s + mu_j) F(s), log-derivative coefficients c(n)
(-F'/F = sum c(n) n^{-s}) and poles at 1/2 +- kappa, the box functional (test function = indicator of an interval of length r) is

    Psi_F(r) = C_F(r) - sum_{n <= e^r} c(n) n^{-1/2} (r - log n),
    C_F(r)   = A0 + A1 r - sum_j U_{c_j}(r) + sum_poles (2/kappa^2)(cosh(kappa r) - 1),
    U_c(r) = sum_{k>=0} e^{-(2k+2c) r}/(2k+2c)^2,  c_j = (1/2 + mu_j)/2,  A0 = sum_j psi'(c_j)/4,
    A1 = (1/2)[log N - d log pi + sum_j psi(c_j)].

PROVED (explicit formula applied to the box; the gamma-factor term by the translation-energy series of
`normalization-and-constant-box.md` section 2 with 2c in place of 1/2; checked against the zero side below): Psi_F(r) = sum over
all zeros of (1 - cos(gamma r))/gamma^2, gamma = (rho - 1/2)/i. For zeta this is Suzuki's Psi = a r + T(r) - D(r) (the two forms agree
to 7e-40 at the first 40 events), with the reserve R_n, credits c_q and debts d_q of the source notes (PR 21/23,
`research/rh/suzuki/notes/closed-reserve-and-debt.md`, `proper-power-memory.md`, `weighted-credit-variation.md`).
If all zeros are on the line then 0 <= Psi_F(r) <= 2 sum 1/gamma^2 (band): 0.092420 for zeta, 0.312059 for L(s,chi_-4),
0.313828 for L(s,chi_5) (from (log Lambda)''(1/2)).

Objects: zeta; L(s,chi_-4), L(s,chi_5) (positive comparison); DH (conductor 5, odd gamma factor, no Euler product);
E_a = zeta(s+a) zeta(s-a), a = 0.05, 0.10, 0.25 (own gamma factors and poles); T-NR(q,c) = zeta(s)(1 + c q^{1/2-s} + q^{1-2s}),
(q,c) = (3,2.2) with zeros at Re s = 0.9038 and (3,2.01) with zeros at Re s = 0.5910, heights (2k+1) pi/log 3, own bridge (conductor
q^2) and zeta's bridge; sign twists eps_p Lambda with zeta's bridge: Liouville, random signs, flips on a random 10% of the primes
("flip10", first flipped prime 59); unimodular twist cos(3 log n) Lambda(n) with zeta's bridge. Backward heat (tau < 0): no Dirichlet
series, so none of the four prime-side statistics can be formed (N in the table); every statistic below is a finite set of
continuous functions of finitely many zeros with a positive margin for zeta at the sizes reached, so it is unchanged in sign for
small |tau| (flow-blindness, as in the brief).

## 4. Combination 2 (proper-power memory, credit variation, reserve update): CLOSED

(a) Credit variation V(X) = sum_{X <= p_n <= N} |c_{n+1} - c_n| (credits at ordinary primes; "tilde" = prime-only renormalised
credits of the source; bound (23 L + 34)/sqrt X proved there for L >= 512 only; "model" = (2/e)(2L+4)/sqrt X minus the same
expression at N, i.e. gaps with E|g/log p - 1| = 2/e).

__C2VAR__

Does the bound use only |Lambda|? PROVED (reading of the source proof, equations (4)-(11) of `weighted-credit-variation.md`): it
uses (i) that the credits are samples c_n = f(r_n -) of one function f = w (r - 2 log(H/2)) with the smooth weight w = r e^{-r/2},
i.e. Lambda(p) = log p with the same sign at every prime; (ii) the envelope e^{r/2} <= H <= 7 e^{r/2} (Chebyshev bounds and the pole
at 1). Nothing about zeros enters. Consequences, all MEASURED above and in `c2_twplus.py` (1e8):

- T-NR(3,2.2) (zeros at Re s = 0.904) and T-NR(3,2.01) satisfy the bound at every X with the same order of constant as zeta.
- Thinned primes (keep each prime with probability 1/2; 0 <= eps <= 1, no functional equation): V/bound = 0.050-0.055 for
  L = 8..14, as zeta.
- Three flipped primes, or a smooth amplitude 1 + 0.5 sin(log p): V/bound <= 0.07.
- Sign flips on a positive proportion of primes: V diverges (factor 8e2 to 1.4e6 above the bound, growing with X), because consecutive
  credits of opposite sign differ by 2|c| and |c| is of order w once H is not 2 sqrt q (1 + o(1)). Liouville: H <= 0 at p = 29.
- DH: N (coefficients on all n, no pole, no state H). Heat: N.

The measured constant V sqrt X/L = 1.37-1.43 for zeta is 2 E|g/log p - 1| (prime gaps), a statistic of gaps, not of zeros.

(b) Proper-power memory B(r) = sum_{p^k <= e^r, k >= 2} c(p^k) p^{-k/2} against r/2 + kappa (kappa = 1.1442541; primes to 1e8):

__C2B__

The r/2 comes from the squares, where a real sign twist has eps_p^2 = 1: PROVED, the even-exponent part of B is identical for zeta,
Liouville and every real sign twist (measured difference 0), and L(chi) differs by the ramified primes only. Controls fail only
through |amplitude| != 1 at a prime (E_a at every p; T-NR at the single prime q, from the event q^2 on: local non-temperedness,
the E2 detector of s2) or through a non-real phase (unimodular twist: the pole of the square part moves off s = 1/2). The zero
content of B is the term A_1(e^{r/2}) - r/2 + gamma = O(e^{-r/4}), below the cube term -2 e^{-r/6}.

Not a survivor: (a) is passed by a control with off-line zeros and by thinned primes; (b) is passed by sign twists and fails for
T-NR through one prime.
