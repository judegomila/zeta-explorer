## Why the bridge is not closed

The Fisher bounds control an information-theoretic quantity. The target
Schur/coupling cost is measured after a source map, a positive-complement
inverse, degree-dependent whitening, exceptional directions, and a shrinking
reserve have all been included. A bound in the first metric is not a bound in
the second without a proved comparison. CP62 equations J3-J9 make this missing
comparison explicit.

The finite payments show that the desired mechanism is real in multiple bounded
settings: the existing odd-19 endpoint, the degree-2 Newman interval, the
gauged degree-5 Newman interval, an odd same-parity degree-9 endpoint reserve,
the complete-theta quadratic family, and the normalized degree-7 regional
variation payment. The corrected even2 arithmetic replay separately
reproduces another known finite contact after repairing a swapped-coordinate
implementation; it is not counted as a new payment theorem. None of these
results controls the constants as degree grows, so they do not fill the
downward arrow from metric/source transfer to uniform domination.

The v21 positive-parent theorem supplies strong all-finite-degree spatial
moment control, but in the physical positive `L2` norm rather than the signed
target geometry. The v22--v26 identities sharpen one normalized-endpoint
route: joint block transport preserves the cancellation of local inverse
terms, modular reflection isolates exactly one negative direction, and the
canonical actual source cancels the separately estimated inverse feedback.
The remaining current-prefix correlated inequality is still open. Conditional
failure of a uniform mixed-metric spectral gap rules out one sufficient
shortcut, not the correlated inequality itself.

The v27 trace identities sharpen the canonical source without changing that
status. Boundary factorization makes the boundary correction nonpositive and
determines the highest regression coefficient, but the sign of `tr R`, every
lower source component, and the general higher-degree signed quadratic remain
open. A generic curvature counterfamily rules out replacing this
theta-specific comparison by positive finite pivots plus a strong
log-curvature bound.

The v29--v30 arithmetic route is a second, separate normalized-endpoint
reduction. Scalar resolvent positivity, adaptive rational completion, and the
correlated conditional-mean formula lead to an exact complete-theta divisor
grouping. Beyond an explicit degree-only `O(N^2)` cutoff, every even summand is
positive. The adjacent odd summands have the opposite eventual sign, with an
explicit absolute tail payment. What remains is finite but signed: prove the
actual even core positive and the actual odd partial sum larger than its
payment, uniformly in degree. The v31 corrected low-rung replays do not supply
that uniform bound.

The v31 theta-specific square pays every quadratic `t^2-alpha` by combining a
literal square with the global bound `4k^2+k''>1254`. At normalized degree 7,
the regional certificate shows a different mechanism: global-regression
variation pays a negative region-optimized minimum. These are genuine signed
compensations, but neither produces a degree-uniform source matrix, partition,
or shift estimate. The invalid coordinate-swapped even2 output is retained as
implementation history only; the corrected replay reproduces a known contact
and leaves the all-degree obligations unchanged.

The v32 interval theorem advances the finite frontier independently of those
uniform routes. It proves both actual parity blocks through degree 9 on
`0 <= a <= 1/2, 0 <= H_cut <= 1/8`; at `H_cut=0`, the separate outer-shift
argument extends the finite Gram to every positive shift, with the normalized
endpoint included. An exact inverse-residual reproduction, finite-width signed
transport, and modular cancellation checks expose the conditioning and
cancellation mechanisms at known finite rungs. The sharp dyadic threshold
`107521` improves the normalized arithmetic tail comparison. None of these
finite results supplies the degree-uniform signed-core payment represented by
the downward arrows above, so all-degree closure remains open.

V33 advances the finite frontier in two logically different ways. The direct
six-channel replay adds the even degree-10 channel on the same
`0 <= a <= 1/2, 0 <= H_cut <= 1/8` rectangle. Combined with the existing odd
degree-9 block, this gives full degree-at-most-10 positivity there. The
corrected odd-degree-11 enclosure is inconclusive, while an earlier adaptation
that left the sixth row and column unpopulated is invalid implementation
history, not a negative matrix.

The much larger global theorem does not iterate the finite arithmetic-core or
innovation routes. It combines the published Platt--Trudgian verification
through exact height `3000175332800` (weakened to `3*10^12`), the exact
zero-pair halo, a freshly replayed theta central reserve, a uniform complete
gamma envelope, and polynomial concentration. With spectral window
`H_spec=2.9*10^12`, the possible negative complement is
`<exp(-2*10^11)` through degree `30000000000`, against central density
`>0.00172`. The external zero computation is an explicit dependency and was
not rerun. The quantitative bound covers `0<=a<=1/2`; the pre-existing outer
argument completes all positive shifts only at global `H_cut=0`. This is a
finite-degree theorem, not new zero verification, arbitrary positive-cutoff
positivity, all-degree closure, or RH.

The remaining v33 analytic results diagnose why the downward arrows stay open.
Hardy compression turns all subset costs into one actual-prefix translation
norm but does not pay it. A degree-dependent critical band reaches inward by
only about `1/(6 log n)`. At a fixed interior shift, polynomial localization
detects any genuine negative density interval, so total signed-tail smallness
cannot prove all degrees. The exact endpoint flux/nonlinear-theta module and
finite atomic dual retain the signed terms, but neither supplies a positive
all-degree remainder. Actual degree-20 local and scalar-gauge obstructions
refute restricted repairs only: they are not negative integrated pivots or
negative full optimized-dual witnesses. Rigid dual transport is likewise
false, while adaptive reoptimization remains unclosed in the critical range.

Accordingly the degree-30-billion theorem bypasses, rather than solves, the
finite signed arithmetic-core obligation. It does not establish the signs of
those individual cores through its degree range, and it supplies no induction
beyond that finite threshold.

## Why the negative tail is constructive information

The `H_cut=1` counterexample rules out a convenient stronger premise: making every
cumulative spatial tail positive. It does not attack the complete kernel.
CP62 instead demonstrates the correct pattern on a restricted actual band:
the tail is negative, the central term is positive, and the complete kernel is
positive. Any viable proof must therefore preserve signed compensation rather
than certify the pieces separately.

The arithmetic tail theorem is consistent with this lesson. It signs only the
remote divisor remainder after a paid cutoff; it does not declare the finite
central arithmetic core positive. In the odd ladder the remote remainder is
itself negative and must be paid explicitly.
