## 0. Verdict

**Precise obstruction, with the exact structure asked for.**

1. The task's guess is correct in an exact sense: the de Bruijn-Newman multiplier `e^{t q^2/4}` is Weil's operator
   attached to a second-degree character of the log-scale line. It normalises the Heisenberg group generated by
   dilations and Mellin weight shifts and acts on it by a symplectic shear. Conjugation carries the Hecke algebra of
   dilations `D_{log p}` to a commuting algebra of Heisenberg elements `W_t(log p)`, and
   `kappa_t = prod_p (1 - p^{-1/2} W_t(log p))^{-1} f_t` for every real t. **H_t is an operator Euler product for
   every t** (Theorem 1). The scalar cocycle `exp((t/2) log m log n)` is the central term of that homomorphism.
2. One correction to the premise: the cocycle is symmetric, hence a coboundary; it defines no central extension by
   itself. The Heisenberg group enters through the symmetric morphism `a -> -(t/2) a` from the line to its dual
   (Weil 1964), not through the extension class of the cocycle (Prop. 2).
3. Davenport-Heilbronn fails the operator Euler product at a named step: its coefficient vector is a joint
   eigenvector only of the index-2 Hecke subalgebra generated by `p = +-1 mod 5` and fails at every `p = +-2 mod 5`,
   for every t (Prop. 4). So this is a structural separator inside the geometry.
4. It gives no zero information, for a proved reason: the separator is invariant under the shear, so it holds for
   the backward heat control `t < 0`, where the product even converges at every complex point and the zeros leave
   every strip (Theorem 3; zero statement from b-escape-law Thm A and Rodgers-Tao). What distinguishes `t = 0` is
   not the Euler/Hecke property but the **alignment** of two Lagrangian lines: at `t = 0` the Hecke torus fixes
   each evaluation point `w`; at `t != 0` the Hecke operator at p moves the evaluation point horizontally by
   `-(t/2) log p`. RH is a statement about the aligned position only. No transformation law for zero reality under
   the group exists beyond de Bruijn monotonicity on the real one-parameter subgroup.
5. Twistor data: the whole family `X_t(w)` has one t-independent datum, the measure `kappa(q) dq` on the parabola
   `lambda = mu^2/4` (the characteristic curve of the heat equation). Reality structures on the datum give only
   setwise symmetry of the zero set. The pointwise condition is a positivity condition on a different object
   (Schoenberg: Fourier transform of `1/X_t` on the real axis is a Polya frequency function), preserved forward in t,
   and it is an equivalence that holds verbatim for DH (section 2).

## 3. Part (2a): the twistor datum of the heat family and its reality conditions

**Prop. 1 (datum). PROVED.** The exponential solutions of `d_t X = (1/4) d_w^2 X` on C^2 are `e^{lambda t + mu w}`
with `(lambda, mu)` on the parabola `P = {lambda = mu^2/4}`. The family is

    X(t, w) = int_{P(R)} e^{lambda t + mu w} kappa(mu) d mu.

The datum `kappa(mu) d mu` does not depend on t. The fibre coordinate t only selects which exponential is paired
with it. (Fourier-Laplace representation over the characteristic variety; for general entire solutions this is the
Ehrenpreis-Palamodov principle, CLASSICAL. It is the heat-equation counterpart of the Whittaker/Penrose contour
formula; for one space dimension the "twistor space" is the curve P and no cohomology is involved.)

**Reality structures on the datum. PROVED (elementary).**
* (R1) kappa real: `X_t(conj w) = conj X_t(w)`.
* (R2) kappa even (J kappa = kappa): `X_t(-w) = X_t(w)`.
* Both hold for every real t and are destroyed for non-real t (R1 fails for `t = i theta`).
* Consequence: the zero set is invariant as a set under `sigma: w -> -conj w`. "All zeros on the line" says each
  zero is a fixed point of sigma. A real structure on the datum gives setwise invariance only. Pointwise
  invariance is never a reality condition; it is a positivity/self-adjointness condition.

**The positivity condition equivalent to real zeros. CLASSICAL (Schoenberg 1951; Karlin, Total Positivity ch. 7).**
With z = -2iw (so `H_t(z) = X_t(iz/2)/8`), `H_t` is even, of order 1, positive on the imaginary z axis. H_t has
only real zeros iff

    Lambda_t(x) := (1/2 pi) int_R e^{iyx} / H_t(iy) dy = (4/pi) int_R e^{iyx} / X_t(y/2) dy

is a Polya frequency function (all minors `det[Lambda_t(x_i - y_j)] >= 0`). The condition is on the Fourier
transform of the reciprocal on the real w axis, not on kappa. Along the fibre it is preserved for increasing t
(de Bruijn, CLASSICAL) and fails for t < Lambda. `1/X_{t+s}` is not a Gaussian convolution of `1/X_t`, so the
preservation is not the "PF * Gaussian = PF" lemma; no simpler mechanism was found.
DH control: the equivalence holds verbatim for the DH kernel; only the conclusion differs. Backward heat: the
condition fails for every t < 0. So this condition is the target, not an input.

**Positive/negative frequency splitting.** Splitting the datum at a cutoff, `kappa = 1_{(c,inf)} kappa + 1_{(-inf,c)}
kappa`, is the Hardy-type splitting used by the bridge energy `H_c` of g-spinor-square-root (refereed:
`H_c >= 0 iff Gamma_- = 0 iff RH`, valid as an equivalence for any even order-one F including DH). In twistor
language that is the positivity statement on the datum; it is already recorded and is DH-blind as an equivalence.

## 4. Part (2b) and (3): Euler product in the datum; the Heisenberg/metaplectic structure

**Prop. 2 (where the product lives; the cocycle is a coboundary). PROVED.**
(a) By (1.1), `kappa = E * f` with `E = sum_n n^{-1/2} delta_{-log n} = conv_p E_p`,
`E_p = sum_k p^{-k/2} delta_{-k log p}`. The datum is a convolution product over primes of local combs, times the
archimedean profile f, with respect to addition of the parameter mu on P.
(b) The map `mu -> (mu^2/4, mu)` from (R, +) to (C^2, +) is not a homomorphism:
`lambda(mu_1 + mu_2) = lambda(mu_1) + lambda(mu_2) + mu_1 mu_2 / 2`. Pairing with `e^{lambda t + mu w}` is a
character of (R,+) in mu iff t = 0. For t != 0 the defect is `exp(t mu_1 mu_2 / 2)`, which at `mu_i = log m, log n`
is the cocycle of flow-euler-defect (2t log m log n in its units).
(c) `beta_t(a, a') = exp(t a a'/2)` is a symmetric 2-cocycle on R (and on `Q_+^* = direct sum of Z`). It equals the
coboundary of `c_t(a) = exp(t a^2/4)`. Every symmetric 2-cocycle on an abelian group gives an abelian extension,
and here it is split. **The cocycle does not define a Heisenberg group.**
(d) The Heisenberg group enters by Weil's construction: `c_t` is a character of second degree; its bicharacter
defines the symmetric morphism `rho_t: R -> R^, a -> (q -> e^{-(t/2) a q})`; multiplication by `c_t` normalises
the Heisenberg group `{z M_b D_a}` and acts on it by `(a, b) -> (a, b + rho_t(a)) = (a, b - ta/2)`, which is (1.3).
For `t = i theta` this is the unitary lower-unipotent element of the metaplectic group Mp_2(R) (CLASSICAL, Weil
1964). For real t < 0 it is a positive self-adjoint contraction in the closure of Howe's oscillator semigroup; for
t > 0 it is the unbounded inverse of such an element (CLASSICAL framing; the closure statement is not checked in
detail here).

**Theorem 1 (operator Euler product for every t). PROVED.** For every real t and every real q,

    kappa_t(q) := (G_t kappa)(q) = sum_n n^{-1/2} (W_t(log n) f_t)(q) = [ prod_p (1 - p^{-1/2} W_t(log p))^{-1} f_t ](q),

`f_t := G_t f`. The operators `W_t(a)` satisfy `W_t(a) W_t(a') = W_t(a + a')` and commute. The algebra
`Hecke_t := C[W_t(log p) : p prime] = G_t Hecke_0 G_t^{-1}` is a polynomial ring on the primes; J normalises it
by (1.4), exactly as at t = 0.

Proof. Apply `G_t` to (1.1) and insert `G_t^{-1} G_t` before f; (1.3). `a -> W_t(a)` is a homomorphism because it
is a conjugate of `a -> D_a`. Convergence is that of (1.1), multiplied by the fixed number `e^{t q^2/4}`. Partial
products over p <= P are the sums over P-smooth n. QED

`c_6/(c_2 c_3) = exp((t/2) log 2 log 3)` (`check1.py`).

**Corollary 1 (evaluation; where t = 0 is special). PROVED.** On the Mellin side
`(W^_t(a) F)(w) = e^{t a^2/4} e^{-wa} F(w - ta/2)` and `W^_t(a) = e^{(t/4) d_w^2} e^{-wa} e^{-(t/4) d_w^2}`.

**Prop. 3 (alignment invariant). PROVED.** In the (a, b) plane of Heisenberg directions, the Hecke line is
`l_t = {(a, -ta/2)}` and the line whose eigenfunctionals are the evaluations is `l_0 = {(a, 0)}`. `l_t = l_0` iff
t = 0. `G_t` is the unique element of the unipotent subgroup fixing the weight-shift line `{(0, b)}` that carries
`l_0` to `l_t`. The scalar Euler product (multiplicativity of the Dirichlet coefficients, local factors that are
functions of w) is equivalent to `l_t = l_0`. The operator Euler product does not depend on t.

**Prop. 4 (DH inside the same structure). PROVED.** Let `a_n = c chi(n) + conj(c) conj(chi)(n)`, chi the complex
character mod 5 with chi(2) = i, c = (1 - i kappa_DH)/2. With `(U_p a)_n = a_{pn}`:
* p = +-1 mod 5: `U_p a = chi(p) a` (chi(p) = +-1 real). Eigen.
* p = +-2 mod 5: `U_p a = i (c chi - conj(c) conj(chi))` up to sign, not proportional to a. Not eigen.
* The products `U_p U_q` with p, q = +-2 mod 5 are eigen (chi(pq) real).
So the DH datum is a joint eigenvector of the index-2 subalgebra generated by the classes `+-1 mod 5` and lies in a
2-dimensional module on which the remaining operators act by the matrix diag(i, -i) in the basis (chi, conj chi).
`kappa_DH,t = c K_chi,t + conj(c) K_conj(chi),t` is a sum of two operator Euler products for every t and is not one
for any t, because Ad(G_t) is an algebra isomorphism. The failing step is the same at all t.

**Prop. 5 (two flows exchanged by the Weil element). PROVED.** Convolution of kappa with a Gaussian in q commutes
with every `D_a` and multiplies `X_0(w)` by `e^{c w^2}`: zeros unchanged, Hecke-equivariant. Multiplication of
kappa by a Gaussian is the de Bruijn-Newman flow: zeros move, Hecke algebra sheared. The Fourier transform on the
q-line exchanges the two operations. So the dBN flow is the Weil-conjugate of the Hecke-equivariant heat flow. The
Hecke-equivariant one is spectral multiplication (what convolution by a bi-K-invariant kernel does to an
automorphic object) and carries no zero information.

**Prop. 6 (torus reading). PROVED (substitution).** With `y = e^{2q}` the modulus of the rectangular torus `E_{iy}`
and d the hyperbolic (Teichmueller) distance, `d(i, iy) = 2|q|`, so `G_t` is multiplication by
`exp(t d(i, iy)^2 / 16)` on the geodesic of rectangular tori, matching the brief's control kernel
`e^{tau u^2/16} kappa`. The Hecke correspondence at p restricted to this geodesic is `q -> q +- log p` up to the
normalising power of p; conjugating by a Gaussian in d produces the factor `e^{-t log^2 p/4} e^{-/+ (t/2) q log p}`
of (1.3). The flow weights each torus by a Gaussian in its distance from the square torus; it is a multiplication
operator on the modular parameter, not a convolution, which is why it does not commute with isogenies.

**Two Heisenberg groups (to avoid a conflation).** (I) The arithmetic one: Heis(A_Q) and the Weil representation
that produce `theta(y) = sum e^{-pi n^2 y}` and its modularity; metaplectic covers and the cubic/sextic theta
functions of the 7/8 mechanism live here. (II) The scaling one: Heis of the modulus line R with its dual,
generated by `D_a` and `M_b`. The heat flow is a metaplectic element of (II). It is not an element of (I), and
kappa is not automorphic for any lattice in the Jacobi group of (II): `G_t` (t != 0), `D_a` (a != 0), `M_b`
(b != 0) do not fix kappa up to scalars (growth comparison, PROVED); that the full stabiliser is `{1, J}` times
scalars is OPEN. Hence `X(t, w)` satisfies no Jacobi-form transformation law in t. This agrees with
g-symmetry-schur (finite symmetry group of one loaded torus).

## 5. The no-go and what RH is in this language

**Theorem 3 (shear-invariant Hecke structure cannot give zero reality). PROVED from Theorem 1 + CLASSICAL/prior
inputs.** For every t < 0 the kernel `kappa_t` has all of:
* (P1) functional equation `J kappa_t = kappa_t`, and J normalises Hecke_t;
* (P2) operator Euler product over the commutative algebra Hecke_t, isomorphic to the GL_1 Hecke algebra by an
  automorphism of the Jacobi group, with evaluated product convergent at every complex w;
* (P3) positivity: `kappa_t > 0`, coefficients `n^{-1/2} > 0`, and
  `-sum_p log(1 - p^{-1/2} W_t(log p)) = sum_n (Lambda(n)/log n) n^{-1/2} W_t(log n)` with nonnegative weights;
* (P4) reality (R1), evenness (R2);
and `X_t` has non-real zeros (Rodgers-Tao, CLASSICAL), in fact zeros outside every strip (b-escape-law Thm A,
internal, referee-corrected). Therefore no argument that uses the functional equation, positivity, and the
Euler/Hecke property only in a form invariant under conjugation by the metaplectic shear can prove that zeros are
on the line. An argument must use Prop. 3: that the local factors are scalar functions of the evaluation point.

Reading of the escape curve: by Corollary 1(i) the n-th Hecke translate samples the archimedean factor at
`w + (|t|/2) log n`. The zero curve `|Im z| ~ (tau/2) log(x/4 pi)` of b-escape-law is where these displaced terms
balance (its `s_eff` has real part shifted by `-tau L/4`). The same displacement with the opposite sign is why no
termwise expansion exists for t > 0.

**RH in this language (reformulation, no new content).** Let `T = {t real : all zeros of X_t on the line}`.
CLASSICAL: `T = [Lambda, inf)`, `0 <= Lambda <= 0.2`. RH iff `0 in T` iff the real-zero locus in the one-parameter
group `{G_t}` reaches the aligned position `l_t = l_0`. For DH, `T_DH = [Lambda_DH, inf)` with `Lambda_DH > 0`
while the alignment statement of Prop. 3 is the same. The group supplies no transformation law for zero reality:
the unitary elements `G_{i theta}` destroy (R1); the real elements act monotonically (de Bruijn) and that is all.
This is the sign dictionary of flow-euler-defect Prop. 3 with the cocycle replaced by the slope of `l_t`.
