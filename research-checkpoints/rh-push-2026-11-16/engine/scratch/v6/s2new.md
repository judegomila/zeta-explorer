- Checkpoint after round 20: **applied in version 6** (section 0c). Text of the rule as written in version 5: "If by then no lane has passed stage A and no silent cell has produced a non-identity statement that no control passes, explore falls to 0 and the campaign spends its remaining rounds on: second referees and certified conversions for every line of Statement O still labelled MEASURED or ARGUED; the remaining silent cells in the order of section 4.0; and one reopening of the frontier slot only with a named new input. The campaign result stays in the form of section 0a."
- Lead BR (version 6). Three-lane rule: if a BR lane produces a non-identity statement in regime 2 or 3 that no control on file passes at a named step (a referee confirms R3), the next round gives it three lanes (proof step; controls on the server; independent referee of the control table). An identification (G = zeta in a regime) or a constructed twin enters the map as a row and does not trigger the rule. Stopping rule for the lead: two refereed obstructions of the same kind on BR-2 or on BR-3 close that regime (section 3); if BR-0 decides regime 3 from rows on file, BR-3 is not launched.
- R1 test (version 6, from round-20 lesson 5). A flag "multiplicativity essential" is recorded only when the lane names a non-multiplicative control that fails the marked step and no multiplicative control on file fails it; the learn step does not count a flag that a referee file rejects.
- Twin screen (version 6). From round 22 no lane is launched on a device, statistic or screening combination before its row of the twin screen TS exists. A row to which one of the three reasons of section 0c applies is not launched.
- Screening arm (version 6). 160 combinations without a survivor: by the version-5 rule the arm is one batch per round. A combination is screened only if it passes the four exclusions of version 5 and has a row of TS with "none of the three". If the next two batches have no admissible combination the arm stops and its server share goes to the compute row.
- Checkpoint after round 26. If by then the lead BR is closed in both regimes without a non-identity statement, the inverse-explore slot has no stage-A pass, and W1 and W2 are done, the campaign spends its remaining rounds on the obstruction record with second referees for each of its statements, and on one frontier lane only if an input is named (W3). No other lane is launched; unused rounds are not filled.
