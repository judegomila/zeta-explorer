## 1. Allocation rule, version 6: 8 lanes per round, of which 2 to 3 use the server

Version 6 allocation: **0 explore (cell-by-cell), 3 deepen, 1 combine, 2 compute (up to 3 when a registered question exists), 2 referee-repair-record; 0 or 1 inverse-explore lane from round 23, taken from a slot that has no item** (version 5: 1 / 2 / 1 / 1 to 3 / 2). Frontier stays withdrawn (one reopening, only with a named new input: item W3). At most two lanes per round work on the 7/8 estimate machinery (user directive); in round 21 none does.

Two phases. Rounds 21 and 22: the checkpoint work W1 and W2 (about 8 to 11 paper lanes and 1 to 3 server jobs by the estimate of the decision sheet) together with the lead BR of section 4.0. From round 23: the lead for as long as its stopping rule allows, the record (Statement O and the obstruction record of `OBSTRUCTION_RECORD_OUTLINE.md`), and the inverse-explore slot.
