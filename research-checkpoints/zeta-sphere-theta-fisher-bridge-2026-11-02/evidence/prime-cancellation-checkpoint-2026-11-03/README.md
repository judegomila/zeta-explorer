# Research checkpoint: theta source–prime cancellation

This append-only checkpoint preserves two analytic notes and the local parts of
indispensable dependencies, without changing the earlier v33 evidence lane or
paper. The notes are AI-assisted research, not peer-reviewed or mechanically
formalized proofs. Their historical internal-review status lines are reproduced
as source claims, not independent certification by this repository. The source
note leaves its localization corollary under separate review.

## Contents and scope

- `SOBOLEV_SOURCE_PRIME_CANCELLATION.md`: for fixed τ>0 and 0≤a<1/2,
  the finite-product source and prime vectors sum exactly to a fixed vector in
  the even Sobolev-parent L² space. The divergent prime vector's stated PNT
  norm asymptotics force source–prime cancellation and its leading mass escapes
  each fixed polynomial-degree window. This is for the constant old test.
- `THETA_CORRELATION_NONUNIFORMITY.md`: for the separate weight
  |F(t+ia)|², fixed-degree correlations decay while their supremum over
  degrees does not; a fixed-old-space construction and Parseval exhibit
  escaping cross-correlation mass. These are not claims about the specific next
  residual or the actual signed inverse.
- `SHIFTED_FORCING_HARD_EDGE.md` supplies the Sobolev estimate, bounded
  weight ratio and finite symmetric Euler-product identity. Its conditional
  hard-edge discussion is not established by this checkpoint.
- `THETA_PRIME_CORRELATION_TAIL.md` supplies the complete-theta fixed-degree
  autocorrelation decay and prime-power tail. Its cited related research notes
  are contextual; the derivative and tail argument needed here appears in this
  file. The fixed-degree estimates do not control constants as degree grows.

The first finding uses w_{τ,a}; the second uses |F(t+ia)|². Their bases and
norms must not be interchanged. The Euler expansion requires a<1/2; its fixed-a
asymptotics are not uniform near the endpoints. The prime number theorem and
complete-theta decay are analytic dependencies, not numerical replays.

## Remaining gaps

signed inverse uniformly in degree; nor is there a proof of a positive next

fixed-old-space examples do not disprove the specific selected-residual bound.
No prior finite result is extended by this checkpoint. The mathematical notes
