# Growing-transition local-cross versus equality-representative cancellation barrier

## 0. Result and fail-closed boundary

Use the locked schedule

\[
 \lambda={p\over2},\qquad c=2\pi\lambda^2,\qquad
 L=2\log\lambda,\qquad h={\pi\over4}.                 \tag{0.1}
\]

Put

\[
 \beta_\lambda={2\over\pi^2}\log(200\lambda^2+25),\qquad
 b_\lambda=6+\lceil\beta_\lambda\log20\rceil,\qquad
 n_*=4\lambda^2+b_\lambda.                              \tag{0.1a}
\]

Let `I` be a finite set of prolate labels satisfying

\[
 n\equiv0\pmod4,\qquad 0\leq n<n_*\quad(n\in I),       \tag{0.1b}
\]

and let its coefficient synthesis have range in the zero-integral source
hyperplane.  These are the plus-family and strict label-range hypotheses of
the frozen equality Garding theorem.  Let `D` be the synthesis's exact full
exterior Gram, and work on the exact quotient by `ker D`.  Denote by
`Q_loc` the Hermitian continuous `m=l=1` local direct-source block and by
`Q_eq` the raw sampler-equality conductor form on this same admitted
coefficient synthesis.

The exact conclusion is

\[
 \boxed{\text{No frozen identity identifies }Q_{\rm loc}
 \text{ as a physical summand or compression of }Q_{\rm eq}.} \tag{0.2}
\]

This is a statement about the licensed physical decomposition.  It does
not assert the categorical impossibility of an accidental matrix equality
or compression on an exceptional finite subspace.

The obstruction is an orientation, not a missing scalar.  The direct
representative of the complete central form is

\[
 Q_X=-\mathcal W_Z(X,O),                               \tag{0.3}
\]

and pairs the inward profile generated by `f+H` with the outward profile
generated by `H`.  The equality representative lies in the alternative
complete identity

\[
 Q_X=\mathcal W_Z(O,O),                                \tag{0.4}
\]

and pairs two raw outward `H` tails.  Only the fully assembled expressions
in (0.3)--(0.4) are equal.  Their individual Archimedean, prime, reflected,
and boundary pieces may not be pasted together term by term.

There is an exact endpoint-multiplier overlap.  In an oriented modal entry,
the direct factor is `(1+chi_j)chi_k`, while the raw `H,H` factor is
`chi_j chi_k`.  The latter is only the `H` summand of the inward direct
profile; it does not identify the corresponding cross-boundary kernel with
the outward--outward equality kernel.

Write

\[
 \gamma_L={L\over4}-{1\over4}\log L-C.                \tag{0.5}
\]

The proved equality estimate `Q_eq succeq gamma_L D` gives a large formal
matrix gap between `Q_eq` and a negative `Q_loc`, but it does not turn that
gap into a physical remainder.  The only proved same-scale cancellation is
the complete-remainder statement obtained from absolute outer sampling.
In particular, this note proves no equality-sector cancellation of
`Q_loc`, no complete central sign, no determinant conclusion, and no RH
statement.  The project state remains `P0_INVALIDATED`.

## 2. The two orientations

Let `H=F_a f`.  The exact inward and outward physical profiles are

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 a_f(s)&={\sqrt\lambda\over2}\sum_{m\geq1}
              \{f+H\}(m\lambda e^{-s}),&&0\leq s\leq L,\\
 b_f(t)&={\sqrt\lambda e^t\over2}\sum_{l\geq1}
              H(l\lambda e^t),&&t>0.
 \end{aligned}                                         \tag{2.1}
\]

For a pure plus-phase prolate mode, put

\[
 H_j=\chi_jf_j\quad\hbox{on }[-\lambda,\lambda],\qquad
 e_j=f_j(\lambda),\qquad d_j=1-\chi_j^2.              \tag{2.2}
\]

The local `m=l=1` profiles are therefore

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 a_j^{(1)}(s)&={\sqrt\lambda\over2}(1+\chi_j)e_jp_j(U),\\
 b_k^{(1)}(t)&={\sqrt\lambda\over2}\chi_ke_k
                    (1+V/c)q_k(V),
 \end{aligned}                                         \tag{2.3}
\]

where `U=c(1-e^(-s))` and `V=c(e^t-1)`.  Its exact oriented entry is

\[
 Q_{{\rm loc},jk}^{\uparrow}
 ={\lambda e_je_k\over4c}(1+\chi_j)\chi_k\,\mathcal I_{jk},
                                                               \tag{2.4}
\]

\[
 \mathcal I_{jk}=
 \int_0^{U_L}\!\int_0^\infty
 {p_j(U)q_k(V)\over
  (c-U)\log\{(c+V)/(c-U)\}}\,dV\,dU.                \tag{2.5}
\]

This is a cross-boundary Hankel geometry: in logarithmic variables the
singular principal depends on `s+t`.

By contrast, the equality theorem starts with the positive raw Fourier
tail

\[
 \phi_f(t)={\sqrt\lambda\over2}e^{t/2}H(\lambda e^t),
 \qquad
 R_m\phi(s)={\bf1}_{\{s\geq0\}}m^{-1/2}
                 \phi(s+\log m),                       \tag{2.6}
\]

and defines

\[
 Q_{\rm eq}(f)
 =2\sum_{m\geq1}\langle R_m\phi,a(D)R_m\phi\rangle
  -4\int_0^\infty L_{\lfloor e^t\rfloor}|\phi(t)|^2\,dt.
                                                               \tag{2.7}
\]

Both slots in (2.7) are outward raw `H` tails.  At the shared
Archimedean principal, (2.4) is a negative-time/positive-time cross
quadrant, whereas the `m=1` term of (2.7) is a positive-time/positive-time
quadratic compression.  An off-diagonal quadrant is not a compression of
the diagonal quadrant.  The equality `Q_X=-W_Z(X,O)=W_Z(O,O)` holds only
after every quadrant and every explicit-form block has been recombined.

## 3. Exact modal multiplier comparison

Split the direct inward factor before taking any limit:

\[
 (1+\chi_j)\chi_k=\chi_k+\chi_j\chi_k.                \tag{3.1}
\]

Consequently (2.4) has the exact algebraic decomposition

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q_{{\rm loc},jk}^{fH}
   &={\lambda e_je_k\over4c}\chi_k\mathcal I_{jk},\\
 Q_{{\rm loc},jk}^{HH,\times}
   &={\lambda e_je_k\over4c}\chi_j\chi_k\mathcal I_{jk}.
 \end{aligned}                                         \tag{3.2}
\]

The superscript `times` records that the second line is still a
cross-boundary `H,H` term.  It is not the outward--outward `m=1` equality
term in (2.7).

For exact exterior-defect whitening, set

\[
 z_j={e_j\over\sqrt{d_j}},\qquad
 \widehat z_j=\chi_jz_j={H_j(\lambda)\over\sqrt{d_j}}. \tag{3.3}
\]

At the level of endpoint multipliers, the raw `H,H` pair and direct pair
are

\[
 \chi_j\chi_kz_jz_k=\widehat z_j\widehat z_k,
 \qquad
 (1+\chi_j)\chi_kz_jz_k
 =\widehat z_j\widehat z_k{1+\chi_j\over\chi_j}.       \tag{3.4}
\]

Thus the exact oriented ratio is

\[
 \boxed{{(1+\chi_j)\chi_k\over\chi_j\chi_k}
 =1+{1\over\chi_j}.}                                  \tag{3.5}
\]

On the diagonal this compares `(1+chi)chi` with `chi^2`.  If an
independent leakage theorem gives `chi_j -> 1`, the ratio is `2+o(1)`:
the raw `H,H` endpoint factor accounts asymptotically for only one half of
the direct multiplier.  This scalar statement does not remove the kernel
orientation mismatch in Section 2.

For reference, define the endpoint-normalized oriented flat local kernel by

\[
 R(\alpha,\beta)
 ={2\{e^{h(\alpha-\beta)}-1\}\over\alpha-\beta},
 \qquad R(\alpha,\alpha)={\pi\over2}.                  \tag{3.6}
\]

The corresponding flat Hermitian direct kernel is

\[
 T_{\rm loc}(\alpha,\beta)
 ={1\over2}\left\{
 (1+\chi_\alpha)\chi_\beta R(\alpha,\beta)
 +(1+\chi_\beta)\chi_\alpha R(\beta,\alpha)\right\}. \tag{3.7}
\]

Replacing `(1+chi)` by `chi` in the last display constructs the cross-boundary
`H,H` summand of `Q_loc`; it still does not construct `Q_eq`.

## 4. What the equality floor does force internally

Separate (2.7) exactly as

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 A_1^{=}&=2\langle\phi,a(D)\phi\rangle,\\
 A_{\geq2}^{=}&=2\sum_{m\geq2}
                   \langle R_m\phi,a(D)R_m\phi\rangle,\\
 P^{=}&=4\int_0^\infty
              L_{\lfloor e^t\rfloor}|\phi(t)|^2\,dt\succeq0.
 \end{aligned}                                         \tag{4.1}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{Q_{\rm eq}=A_1^{=}+A_{\geq2}^{=}-P^{=}
 \succeq\gamma_LD.}                                   \tag{4.2}
\]

The same-prime divisor equality enters as `-P^=`, so it cannot by itself
give positive Loewner compensation to a negative direction.  Moreover,
`L_1=0`, and hence its density vanishes on

\[
 0\leq t<\log2.                                        \tag{4.3}
\]

It does not erase the full-profile corner principal.  Terms supported away
from that corner may nevertheless cancel a compressed finite modal matrix;
the source theorem explicitly leaves that possibility open.

There is one useful conditional consequence entirely inside the equality
representative.  If some subspace `E` were independently shown to satisfy

\[
 P_EA_1^{=}P_E\preceq-\tau_HP_EDP_E,                    \tag{4.4}
\]

then (4.2) would force

\[
 P_E(A_{\geq2}^{=}-P^{=})P_E
 \succeq(\tau_H+\gamma_L)P_EDP_E.                     \tag{4.5}
\]

Because `-P^= preceq 0`, the burden on `A_(>=2)^=` is at least as large.
No frozen input proves (4.4) with `A_1^=Q_loc`; Section 2 proves that this
identification is false.

## 5. Formal comparison and physical forced cancellation

Pass to exact `D`-white coordinates and use hats for the whitened forms.
Suppose a nonzero subspace `E` satisfies

\[
 P_E\widehat Q_{\rm loc}P_E\preceq-\tau P_E.          \tag{5.1}
\]

The equality floor gives the rigorous formal comparison

\[
 \boxed{
 P_E(\widehat Q_{\rm eq}-\widehat Q_{\rm loc})P_E
 \succeq(\tau+\gamma_L)P_E.}                          \tag{5.2}
\]

Equation (5.2) is only a difference of two well-defined matrices.  It is
not a cancellation theorem, because no identity
`Q_eq=Q_loc+Q_eq,rest` has been proved.

For the complete central form, absolute outer sampling does give

\[
 -B_{{\rm out},q}I\preceq\widehat Q_X
 \preceq B_{{\rm out},q}I,
 \qquad B_{{\rm out},q}=c^{1/2+o(1)}                  \tag{5.3}
\]

for fixed modal count.  Define the actual complete remainder by

\[
 \widehat R_{\rm comp}=\widehat Q_X-\widehat Q_{\rm loc}.
                                                               \tag{5.4}
\]

Now (5.1) and the lower half of (5.3) prove

\[
 \boxed{P_E\widehat R_{\rm comp}P_E
 \succeq(\tau-B_{{\rm out},q})P_E.}                   \tag{5.5}
\]

For the realized local scale

\[
 \tau_L={c^{hC+o(1)}\over L^{2m}},                    \tag{5.6}
\]

the right side of (5.5) is `(1-o(1))tau_L` when `hC>1/2`.
This is the only currently proved same-scale forced-cancellation statement.

One may display exactly how little of it is supplied by the proved
equality floor.  Put

\[
 \widehat S_{\rm eq}=\widehat Q_{\rm eq}-\gamma_LI\succeq0,
 \qquad
 \widehat R_{\rm other}^{\rm formal}
 =\widehat Q_X-\widehat Q_{\rm loc}-\widehat Q_{\rm eq}.
                                                               \tag{5.7}
\]

Then

\[
 P_E(\widehat S_{\rm eq}
       +\widehat R_{\rm other}^{\rm formal})P_E
 \succeq(\tau-B_{{\rm out},q}-\gamma_L)P_E.           \tag{5.8}
\]

The word `formal` is essential: an additional common-representative
identity is required before its two summands can be named as disjoint
physical channels.  Since `gamma_L=O(L)`, the guaranteed equality floor is
`o(tau_L)` for every fixed `hC>0`.  It is consistent with a much larger
positive equality compression, but no such upper-scale statement is
proved.

## 6. Cancellation inventory

The frozen continuous theorem makes the pole and Archimedean-regular
`m=l=1` pieces lower order at the local scale.  The frozen curved-shell
theorem makes the complete curved `m=l=1` prime shell lower order as well.
The equality prime term `-P^=` is negative semidefinite and absent at the
corner.  None of these is a proved positive same-scale canceller.

After exact source and endpoint rows have been nulled, the still-viable
same-scale sectors are:

1. the excess of the raw equality Archimedean channels beyond the proved
   conductor floor, including `A_(>=2)^=`;
2. continuous mixed and full-profile sampler axes and unequal-sample
   Archimedean correlations;
3. non-equality prime fixed-product channels, including unresolved mixed
   and higher samplers;
4. reflected-tail correlations; and
5. any source or endpoint rows deliberately retained rather than nulled.

For `hC>1/2`, equation (5.5) proves that their correctly recombined complete
assembly must have a positive compression of the local exponential scale.
It neither selects one sector nor proves that the equality sector alone
does the job.  Any continuation must first put the proposed cancelling
terms into one exact representative and only then take a signed Loewner
estimate.
