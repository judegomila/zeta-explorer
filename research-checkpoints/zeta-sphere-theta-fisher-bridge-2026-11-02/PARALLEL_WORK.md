# Parallel work contract

This checkpoint is designed to let proof, computation, audit, and paper work
proceed simultaneously without rewriting the validated baseline.

## Frozen ownership

- `evidence/v18-curated/`, `evidence/cp62/`, `evidence/v19-delta/`,
  `evidence/v20-v26-continuation/`, `evidence/v27-v30-continuation/`, and
  `evidence/v31-continuation/`, `evidence/v32-continuation/`, and
  `evidence/v33-continuation/` are frozen. Accepted later work is appended in a
  new versioned evidence directory; it never replaces old bytes or silently
  extends an existing version union.
- Versioned provenance receipts are append-only. The root `SHA256SUMS` is
  regenerated by the integration owner after an accepted delta so it binds the
  new complete public tree.
- `CLAIMS.md` and `paper/` have a single integration owner on the checkpoint
  branch. Workstreams propose claim deltas rather than editing the same rows in
  parallel.
- Generated exploratory output never goes into `evidence/`.

## Branch lanes

Use one isolated worktree per branch:

- `zsv/proof-<lemma>` - analytic proof work;
- `zsv/experiment-<question>` - exploratory numerics, clearly non-certifying;
- `zsv/audit-<artifact>` - independent replay or adversarial review;
- `zsv/paper-<section>` - exposition only;
- `zsv/integration-<date>` - controlled merges of accepted deltas.

Do not force-push a shared lane. Do not merge directly into `main`; open a
draft PR and preserve the evidence grade in the title and description.

## Workstream shape

Copy `workstreams/TEMPLATE/` to `workstreams/<name>/` and keep:

- `STATUS.md` - hypothesis, current result, and precise blocker;
- `CLAIM_DELTA.md` - proposed new/changed ledger rows;
- `src/` - authored derivations or code;
- `tests/` - bounded checks;
- `results/` - generated outputs with commands and environment;
- `MANIFEST.json` - hashes, byte counts, rerun status, and provenance.

## Promotion gates

A numerical observation may become a `finite-certificate` claim only when its
finite domain, arithmetic model, analytic tail/error terms, verifier, and
replay receipt are all frozen. It may become `proved` only when the analytic
argument and every imported premise are identified. No claim becomes “peer
reviewed” through an internal audit.

The central target should remain one bounded statement: prove the actual
lower-metric innovation domination with all degree, source-map, inverse, and
reserve constants visible. Adjacent experiments should be separate lanes.

For the normalized-endpoint modular lane, the current bounded target is the
correlated current-prefix inequality recorded as
`ZSV-CANONICAL-CORRELATED-TARGET`. Work on auxiliary positive regressions,
mixed-metric gaps, or floating finite-rung trends must remain separate so those
surrogates are not mistaken for payment of the actual target.

The v30 arithmetic baseline opens three additional, non-overlapping lanes:

- `zsv/proof-finite-arithmetic-core` owns exact or interval evaluation of the
  actual finite divisor core. It must freeze the current-prefix coefficients,
  summation convention, arithmetic error, and parity-specific stopping rule.
- `zsv/proof-adaptive-resolvent` owns the degree-dependent choice of `s` and
  the comparison between `J_low-rho_N` and the explicit `B_N/s` cost. A
  fixed-`s` or scalar-only result is not promotion of this target.
- `zsv/proof-odd-tail-payment` owns the odd partial-sum lower bound and the
  explicit negative-tail payment. `Partial>0` is exploratory only;
  certification requires `Partial>Tail` in the same normalization.

The v31 continuation opens three further, non-overlapping lanes:

- `zsv/proof-regional-variation-payment` owns any attempt to turn the
  normalized degree-7 `|u|=1/8` mechanism into a degree- or shift-uniform
  theorem. It must retain the regional old metrics, the globally selected
  coefficients, complete spatial tails, and the distinction between a
  negative regional optimum and the globally selected residual.
- `zsv/proof-quadratic-square-extension` owns any higher-degree analogue of
  the complete-theta square payment. The quadratic identity and
  `4k^2+k''>1254` may be used as frozen input, but they cannot be promoted to a
  positive higher source matrix without a new integrated argument.
- `zsv/audit-regression-coordinate-order` owns regression-basis ordering
  guards and reproducibility checks. Tests must bind each inverse component to
  its named basis vector and reject the invalid swapped even2 artifacts; a
  corrected low-rung replay is not a new pivot.

These lanes take the curated v31 checkpoint as read-only input and submit
separate claim deltas. None may rewrite the frozen v27--v31 files, present an
even-tail sign as an odd-tail reserve, or infer a uniform theorem from the
finite quadratic, degree-7, or corrected even2 reproductions.

The v32 continuation added a frozen finite frontier and four separate
follow-on lanes. V33 closes the original degree-10 lane but not its successor:

- `zsv/proof-degree11-plus` owns any direct interval extension beyond the
  certified degree-at-most-10 Gram on the positive-cutoff rectangle. It must
  preserve both parity blocks, the exact normalization, complete orbit/spatial
  tails, and inverse costs. The corrected odd-degree-11 run is inconclusive,
  not negative; the earlier unpopulated sixth channel is invalid history.
- `zsv/proof-positive-cutoff-outer-shift` owns any attempt to extend the
  positive-cutoff rectangle beyond `a=1/2` or `H_cut=1/8`. The existing
  paired-Hadamard outer argument is available only at `H_cut=0` and may not be
  reused silently for truncated kernels.
- `zsv/proof-finite-signed-arithmetic-core` continues the all-degree arithmetic
  route beyond the separately certified finite degrees. Even partial sums must
  retain the degree-dependent cutoff; odd partial sums must exceed the explicit
  negative-tail payment in the same normalization.
- `zsv/audit-signed-transport-conditioning` owns independent checks of
  residual conditioning, regional transport, and moving-flux proposals.
  Unweighted residual size, local source sign, subdivision, or one failed
  Lyapunov weight may not be promoted into a target theorem.

These lanes now take the curated v33 checkpoint as read-only input. They must
use `H_cut` for the spatial lower limit, keep the raw bridge distinct from its
divided-by-`a` endpoint, and treat both the direct degree-10 rectangle and the
global degree-30-billion theorem as finite--not as all-degree positivity,
arbitrary-configuration positivity, or RH.

The v33 continuation adds four non-overlapping lanes:

- `zsv/proof-verified-height-extension` owns any attempt to raise the global
  finite-degree threshold by a larger verified spectral height or sharper
  central/tail constants. It must name `H_spec` separately from `H_cut`, cite
  the exact external theorem and height, record whether the underlying large
  computation was rerun, and preserve the distinction between real `p^2` and
  complex `abs(p)^2`. A citation to a larger computation is not a new zero
  verification by this project.
- `zsv/proof-fixed-shift-density` owns any all-degree theorem at one fixed
  interior shift. Because polynomial localization detects every genuine
  negative density interval, central reserve or small total negative mass is
  insufficient. This lane must control the complete pointwise theta density
  without assuming the desired zero location or silently discarding exterior
  zero-pair contributions.
- `zsv/proof-selected-dual-full-vector` owns the normalized-endpoint atomic
  dual and adaptive reoptimization route. Any negative witness must satisfy all
  `N` cross-moment constraints exactly and pay its reservoir repair; scalar or
  two-point proportional-gauge failures are only restricted-route
  obstructions. Rigid transport is not an admissible substitute for
  reoptimization.
- `zsv/proof-endpoint-flux-selected` owns the nonlinear theta/SOS flux route.
  It must retain the exact inverse-selected coefficients, every root-deletion
  term, the weighted boundary, and the signed remainder. Factorial coefficient
  growth and negative remainders in the larger real-rooted class prohibit
  promotion of a coefficientwise or ambient-cyclic positivity claim, but do
  not themselves decide the actual selected pivot.

An independent audit lane for the global finite-degree theorem must keep the
four proof layers separate: published zero-height theorem, zero-pair sign,
owned theta central/gamma bounds, and polynomial-tail arithmetic. Replaying the
owned small certificate and arithmetic does not count as rerunning the
Platt--Trudgian computation. No lane may present internal or AI-assisted review
as external peer review or a literature novelty determination.
