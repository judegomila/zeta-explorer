## A. Items against recorded lanes

**A1. `c11-rs3-debranges-positivity-family`, `screen.py` / `screen.json`, DH windows at height >= 1000. Numeric defect; verdict stands.**
- Recorded: DH on-line zeros, 1000 < t < 1020, min q_1 = -0.3348103021277858 (RESULT section 5 table: "-0.335").
- Battery: -0.33053338578645175 at the same zero (the second of the 17, ordinate 1001.7040109...).
- Cause, demonstrated: `zeros_on_line` calls `mpmath.findroot(..., solver='anderson')` on Re X(1/2 + iu) with the default tolerance. X is of size 1e-340 at this height, so the stopping test on the function value is met before the ordinate has converged. `devices/d1_diagnostic.py` re-runs that finder: its 17 ordinates differ from the battery's by 4.5e-6 to 1.68e-3, and each difference equals the Newton residual X/X' evaluated at the record's ordinate. At the record's ordinates q_1 reproduces the recorded minimum to all 16 digits; at converged ordinates it is -0.33053.
- Consequences: fail counts unchanged (0, 0, 0, 3, 10, 16); the lane's verdict and the control-table cell "DH fails at the same step as zeta on its on-line zeros" stand. The numbers in the DH rows at t = 1000 and (not re-run) t = 1e4, and in the GAM rows at t = 1000 and 1e4, come from the same finder and should be recomputed with a relative tolerance or a Newton polish. At t < 200 the record's numbers agree with the battery to 16 digits.
- Check requested from an independent referee: recompute one ordinate (the battery gives 1001.70401094...; table `zeros_DH.json`) by any method and confirm which of the two values of q_1 it gives.

**A2. Same lane, RESULT section 5 second bullet and flag R2 ("h <= 0.5 not seen to fail").** The sentence is true of the three sampled windows and is not true of the range below 1e4. Battery, all zeta zeros below 2000, ball-certified signs: q_{1/2} < 0 at zeros 922, 996, 1308, 1496 (values -0.0542512604954, -0.1215334371661, -0.0411205964716, -0.2784730517174). The proposed map row could read "zero 34 at h = 1; zero 922 at h = 1/2 (measured)".

**A3. `c3-lemmab-referee-dh-poisson` and map row P43 ("relative error at most 5.4e-12").** The eight recorded residuals are +3.88e-12 to +4.63e-12, all of one sign; the replay with an independent zero table gives +3.83e-12 to +4.62e-12. A residual that does not move when the zero table changes, and has one sign, is not noise of the zero side. Test (`devices/d3_variant.py`, `logs/d3_variant.txt`): archimedean quadrature step divided by 2 and by 4 gives -2.256e-11 and -2.170e-11 in the first two cases at both refinements; a window wider by 1.4 changes nothing. The identity holds for DH (verdict stands); the figure 5.4e-12 in the map row is specific to one step size and should read "about 2e-11". The origin of the remaining 2e-11 (float64 kappa in the coefficients, the 1e-18 cut-off of the residue sum, or the zero-side window) was not isolated.

## B. Attack on this lane's own claims

**B1. Is the threshold met?** No, as registered. PLAN.md requires each number within the recorded tolerance; D1 has one number outside. RESULT.md states this in its first sentence. Counting it as met because the record is at fault would be changing the criterion after the run.

**B2. Could the battery, not the record, be wrong at A1?** Checks: (i) the battery's DH ordinates below 1000 agree with a third list (`c2-euler-at-zeros/dh_zeros_1000.json`, a different code path) to 1.2e-13, and the window in question lies above it (1000 to 1020), so that list does not cover it; (ii) the battery's ordinates there are Newton-converged to 1e-36 relative with residuals below 1e-45 at 170 bits; (iii) the box counts for 1000.0137 to 1020.0137 close; (iv) the record's number is reproduced exactly by the record's finder, and the residual at its ordinates is 1e-3 to 1e-6, not 1e-30. (i) is weaker than it looks; (ii) to (iv) settle it.

**B3. Are the replays independent of the records?** D1: same formula, different library (python-flint series derivative against mpmath numerical `diff`), independent zeros. D2: same node rule and LDL, different arithmetic (balls), log-derivatives through `acb_series` with Hurwitz zeta against mpmath `dirichlet`. D3: the record's own float64 formulas re-typed; independent inputs are the zero table and f'/f on the lattice (series derivative against central difference). The quadrature is the same, which is why A3 shows up identically in both. D5: the set is rebuilt by a vectorised rule with float64 floors and ball fallback; sums in balls. D4: no recorded numbers.

**B4. Tautologies.** D4 for real coefficients tests the tables, not the device (stated as weakest step 1). D5 check "stage identity" is algebra. D2's positivity for zeta below height... holds for any function with all zeros on the line in the range the nodes see; for DH it is the record's finding. None of this is hidden in RESULT.md.

**B5. Winding counts.** The DH box counts use 64-bit evaluations at 250 to 4000 points per side with the rule "refine until the largest phase step is below 1 rad" (largest accepted step 0.97 rad). Box edges are at 0.0137 + 10k. Measured afterwards from `zeros_DH.json`: the smallest distance of a zero ordinate to a box edge is 1.58e-4. A count error would need a phase change of more than 2 pi between two adjacent samples, which the step rule excludes for a function that is smooth on the scale of the sample spacing; and every box count agrees with an independent count of zeros found in that box (on-line by sign changes, off-line by Newton). This is two-sided evidence, not an interval certificate.

**B6. HEAT.** The scan step 0.05 can miss a pair of on-line zeros closer than 0.05 (it then shows as a local minimum and Newton is started above it, which converges to a real zero and is discarded). The count diagnostic catches the total deficit, not its location. Final diagnostics: tau = -0.1: 1435 on-line + 2 x 41 = 1517, balanced in each of the five bands; tau = -0.5: 929 + 2 x 277 = 1483, short by 34, all of it above height 500 (2, 21, 11 in the bands 500-1000-1500-2000). "Balanced" is evidence of completeness, with the caveat that zeros can cross a band edge under the flow (windows of 50 show +-1). The off-line list for tau = -0.5 above 500 is incomplete and is labelled so in the file. The first build of the HEAT value tables was wrong above t = 950 (RESULT section 3); the zero tables do not depend on those values.

**B7. Seeds and reproducibility.** U and SGN depend on numpy's PCG64 stream and on `Generator.random` / `Generator.integers`. Checked on both machines (numpy 2.5.3 on the Mac, 2.5.5 on the server): the parameter arrays (`u_params(0)` sha 9b11a529c6a885e1, `sgn_params(0)` sha efe57362a7d97106), the additive phase table and the integer coefficient tables are bit-identical. The complex table exp(2 pi i theta) is not: its sha256 differs between the machines (last-bit differences of the platform's exp), so the sha256 of `coef_U-*.npy` and `coef_WT.npy` in the manifest identify the server files only. The parameter files `local_U.npy` and `local_SGN.npy` are the authoritative record.

**B8. First-build defects.** Two defects of this lane's first build were found by its own checks (HEAT scale underflow, found by D4; ball inflation in the stage tables at Re s <= 5/8, found by the sanity line) and repaired by reruns (units `rh-c12-fix-1119120416`, `rh-c12-stage2-1119121254`). A third class would not be caught by any check here: a table that is wrong in a way consistent with the conjugation rule and with the proved lower bound on Re s = 2. The spot check (300 random points per closed table against direct evaluation, 1.4e-32) covers the closed tables only; the stage, product, BEU and HEAT tables have no independent second evaluation beyond the consistency sums at s = 2, 3, 2 + 7i and the tau = 0 check. An independent referee should recompute a sample of those.

**B9. Things claimed in README/RESULT that were not run.** GPU: not used. T-NR, E_a, HEAT were not replayed through D1, D2, D3 (the records do not have those cells either). The 30-digit claim for HEAT rests on ball accuracy and the tau = 0 check at two heights, not on an enclosure of the quadrature error.

## C. Verdict of the self-check

The battery is usable for stage B of an explore lane on the objects with values in the strip (ZETA, DH, EA, TNR, WT, SGN-LIOU, HEAT, BEU; THIN and DEL as stages); for U and SGN it supports devices that live on Re s > 1 or on coefficients only. ## Errors and corrections

**E1 (correction required). The record's DH ordinates are not converged already below height 200; the defect is not "near t = 1000", and the sentence "At t < 200 the record's numbers agree with the battery to 16 digits" (author's item A1 above) is false.**
- `records/screen.json`, DH window t0-200: min q_2 = -0.9298201600999175. Battery (`tables/device_D1.json`, DH_t1_200): -0.92847936282289. Difference 1.34e-3. The battery's own output file contains this disagreement; the replay did not compare it because PLAN.md registered only min q_1 for this window.
- Decided here: with converged ordinates the value is -0.92847936282289 (battery correct). Re-running the record's finder (mpmath `findroot`, anderson, 30 digits, the record's lattice 1 + 0.05 k) on 185 < t < 200 gives 12 ordinates with Newton residuals 1.3e-5 to 1.4e-3; at its ordinate 194.0766501626 (true zero 194.0752534430) q_2 = -0.9298201600999174, the recorded number. The completed DH function is of size 1e-65 at that height, far below the finder's absolute tolerance, so the mechanism is the one named in A1; it sets in at heights of order 100, not 1000.
- Consequences for RESULT.md: (i) section 0 and 4.1 should say that every DH ordinate of the record above the height where abs(X) drops below the finder tolerance is unconverged, with a measured instance at t = 194; (ii) "19 of 20" is the count over the 20 registered checks and is correct as that; over all 24 recorded min q values of the four replayed windows the count is 17 of 24 (DH 1000-1020: 6 of 6 differ, by 7e-6 to 1.3e-2; DH below 200: 1 of 6 differs); (iii) the registered D1 check "DH 1 < t < 200, min q_1" passed because its minimiser (zero 16, t = 40.16) lies where the record's finder still converged. Fail counts are unchanged in both windows (confirmed here for the lower window), so the verdict of the recorded lane is not affected. The headline claim of this lane (5 of 5 verdicts; registered threshold not met) is unchanged.

**E2 (minor). Section 5, D1 row: "at the same rate (h = 1: 144/1517 against 127/1861 below 2000)".** The rates are 9.5 per cent and 6.8 per cent. "Fails at the same step" is supported; "at the same rate" is not what the two numbers say. No verdict depends on it.

**E3 (minor, wording). Section 2: "HEAT quadrature at tau = 0 against xi(1/2 + it)/2".** With the usual xi(s) = (1/2) s(s - 1) pi^(-s/2) Gamma(s/2) zeta(s) the identity is F_0(z) = xi(1/2 + z) (checked here: ratio 1 to 149 digits). The result's "xi/2" uses xi without the factor 1/2 (the convention of `core.Zeta.completed`); `heatlib.py`'s docstring and `heatlib.xi` use the other one. The numbers are right; the convention should be stated once.

**E4 (minor, scope). D4 "18 of 18 checks of the plan" counted under "numbers within tolerance".** The record has no numbers, the check is an identity for real coefficients, and the result says so in weakest step 1. The claim line "Every recorded number reproduced: 4 of 5" therefore has independent numerical content in three devices (D2, D3, D5 set data), is vacuous for D4, and fails for D1. Read that way the claim is accurate.
