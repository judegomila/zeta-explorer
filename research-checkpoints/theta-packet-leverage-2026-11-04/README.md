## What the package establishes

Read [the source README](src/README.md), [the sharper comparison](src/CHEBYSHEV_MONIC_COMPARISON.md), and [its AI-assisted audit](src/SHARPER_CHEBYSHEV_AUDIT.md). Under the supplied fixed-parameter family and uniform packet-norm hypotheses, the achieved selected-leverage upper rate is -(2 log 3 - 2) per actual polynomial degree. Optimality is not claimed.

For a complete same-parity triangular hierarchy of these exact-degree packets, this forces exponentially large inverse-Gram diagonal, selected diagonal coefficient, and Gram condition number. The weighted absolute derivative synthesis sum diverges. The result rules out a uniform Riesz lower bound and the specified uniformly bounded or uniformly localized absolute synthesis-column criterion for this family.

Large signed derivative contributions may cancel. Divergence of an absolute synthesis sum does not lower-bound the derivative energy of the selected polynomial. Cancellation-sensitive synthesis, a different near-extremal packet family, redundant nontriangular representations, and direct selected estimates remain open. Neither Delta_n positivity nor U_n=o(Delta_n) is established, and there is no RH conclusion.
