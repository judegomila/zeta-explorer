# Fixed-τ oscillator form obstruction: research checkpoint

This append-only analytic checkpoint concerns the actual complete-theta source
at the normalized endpoint a=0. It is AI-assisted research, not peer-reviewed
or a machine-formalized proof. Historical internal-review labels in the
preserved notes are source claims, not additional independent certification.
It does not alter the v33 paper or earlier evidence.

For each fixed τ>0 and parity, the exact arctangent identity implies that
∫λ/w over each interval between consecutive distinct real zeros equals π/√τ.
The external zero-count result below implies that such gaps become arbitrarily
short, and hence λ/w is unbounded above. Polynomial localization gives an
unbounded upper parent-relative form spectrum without signed-prefix positivity.
If the signed old Gram stays positive at every degree in that parity, the
auxiliary worst-case constants ρ_N in the sufficient form criterion instead
necessarily diverge, so that fixed-τ sufficient criterion eventually fails.
If a signed old Gram ceases to be positive, that criterion's hypothesis fails.
