# v10 index erratum: ratio orientation

2 November 2026. This corrects one summary phrase in V10_INDEX.md only. The archived v10 ZIP remains unchanged.

The index says: “Classical universality gives reference/full spectral ratio subsequential limits 2|c|²”.

Correct wording: **Classical universality gives full/reference spectral ratio subsequential limits 2|c|²**, namely

    C_hat_a(2t) / D_hat_a(2t) -> 2|c|²,

along the specified universality sequences, for fixed 0<a<1/2 and each nonzero constant c.

The underlying theorem cosh_core_reference_v1/EXACT_RESIDUAL_AND_TRANSFER_LIMIT.md, equation (5), and first_theta_reference_audit/TRANSFER_AUDIT.md already use the correct orientation. Their proofs, consequences, constants and review status are unchanged. The reciprocal reference/full ratio would instead tend to 1/(2|c|²) on those fixed nonzero-c sequences.
