## 1. Referee-confirmed statements

None of these is about zeros of zeta. None is confirmed as new: where novelty was examined the referee found the statement classical or a short corollary of classical facts. They are listed because the proofs were re-derived and the statements are usable as tools or controls.

### 1.1 Self-dual atomic measures (`n1-beurling-rigidity`)
- Theorem A. A positive purely atomic tempered measure mu on R with Fourier transform mu^ = mu, atom kappa at 0, and every atom >= kappa, is kappa times the Dirac comb of Z.
- Corollary A. A generalized Dirichlet series with coefficients >= its residue and zeta's exact functional equation (gamma factor pi^(-s/2) Gamma(s/2), conductor 1, simple pole, finite order) is residue times zeta. A Beurling system with that functional equation and residue 1 is the primes.
- Lemma T. There is no self-dual first-order deformation of the integers, with or without varying kappa.
- Dependencies: Bohr means; the equality case of abs(phi(t)) <= phi(0) for positive-definite phi. Elementary; no Euler product, no uniform discreteness assumed.
- Theorem B (any residue): PROVED-rel Meyer 1970 plus a one-dimensional coset-ring reduction; neither was re-read.
- Referee outcomes: three referees, all hold; all PROVED steps (S1-S3, A1-A4, Corollary A, B1, B2, B4, the 2.4 lemma, Lemma T) re-derived. All three reject the label "new": novelty was not checked by the author, and Theorem A is the equality case of a standard positive-definiteness fact. Scope corrections: the statement is limited to zeta's own functional equation; it does not exclude Beurling systems with other functional equations.
- Numerics confirmed: tangent smallest singular value decays about 0.32-0.375 per prime; integer-mass self-dual fakes need atoms below the atom at 0.
- Content: identification only. No zero-free bound, no height-uniform statement.

### 1.2 Convex-tube continuation (`n1-tube-hull`)
- Theorem T. If f is holomorphic with polynomial vertical growth on a tube over a convex base B, the base contains a Dirichlet-recessive piece (sigma_0, inf) x N of the region of absolute convergence, and f equals an absolutely convergent Dirichlet series sum c_n(y) lambda_n^(-z) there, then every coefficient c_n continues to the projection of B.
- Corollaries (under the same hypotheses, including finite clearing products): T1, a convex holomorphy tube cannot pass the supremum of the family zeros; T2, reflected chambers cannot be joined, since zeros on the critical line exist.
- Lemma 1 (model): the group <alpha, beta> has order 6 and permutes z, z+s-1/2, z+s+w-1; the hull at s = 1 lies in Re(w+z) > theta, sharp by exact LP; with the 1/L reflection the group is infinite. Sextic model: <K6, Theta3, Pm> is B3 of order 48; Theta3 fixes x.
- Referee outcome: holds; six statement/attribution defects. For bases that only meet the absolute region, T1 and T2 hold modulo a Bochner growth transfer that is not written out. Referee strengthening: the hull bound x >= 1 (theta with ZF(theta) input) holds over all 24 images with X o g nonnegative, so it does not depend on the ARGUED list of 8 legitimate images.
- Not established: that the Bochner hull of the manuscript's estimate-supplied region adds nothing to the 7/8 method; that the model reflections are functional equations of the triple series.

### 1.3 Exact cubic large-sieve identity over F_q[T] (`n3-ff-cubic-sieve-rational-deflation`)
- Lemma B. For q = 1 mod 6, prime moduli pi of degree m, all monic d of degree n, k0 = 2m-2-n:
  sum_d abs(sum alpha_pi chi_pi(d))^2 = q^n abs(alpha)^2 + q^(n-m) [ (q-1) sum_{deg r <= k0} abs(<alpha, g~(r,.)>)^2 - sum_{deg r = k0+1} abs(<alpha, g~(r,.)>)^2 ].
- Consequence (PROVED): the sieve is bounded by q^n abs(alpha)^2 once the Gauss family of conductor degree <= k0 is removed. The converse ("flat only if") is not proved; non-flatness for smaller k is MEASURED (k0 <= 4). The statement "FF-WLS" indexed by n > k+1 is false.
- Dependencies: functional equation of L(u, chi); classical inputs; no RH.
- Referee outcome: holds; Lemmas A and B re-derived line by line and reproduced with independent code at q = 13 (not run by the lane) and q = 7, entrywise error 3e-14 to 4e-12.
- MEASURED only: (1-q^4 u^3) psi(r,u) = (1-q^3 u^3) P_r(u) with deg P_r <= deg r + 1.

### 1.4 Function-field Mayer operator (`n1-ff-mayer`)
- For N irreducible of degree n over F_q and chi even non-trivial: M(u) = W D(u), W a u-independent Hermitian unitary involution, D(u) normal with n+1 explicit eigenvalues.
- Theorem 1. ev_0 (1 - M(u))^(-1) = Phi_all(u) / ((q-1) L(u, chi)), Phi_all a polynomial lattice sum non-vanishing at zeros of L. Requires L(1/q, chi) != 0 as a classical input and V+ read as the torus invariants (referee additions). det(1 - M | invariants) is divisible by lcm(L(u,chi), L(u,chibar)), not by the product in general.
- Referee outcome: holds; all PROVED steps re-derived; independent code confirms Theorem 1 to 1e-13 on three cases not run by the author.
- Use: the phase-rotation control in row N3 below.
