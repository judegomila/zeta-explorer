## Round 1

Five lanes run and refereed, seven proposals rejected at screening. No statement about zeros of zeta; no abscissa below 0.874886647; RH open.

### Lanes (one line each)

- `n1-tube-hull`: precise obstruction (referee: holds, six statement defects). Theorem T: on a convex tube with polynomial vertical growth, every Dirichlet coefficient continues to the projection of the base, so a convex holomorphy hull cannot pass the supremum of the family zeros; the sextic model group is B3 (order 48), the theta reflection fixes x, hull output = input theta. Not established: the Bochner growth transfer for bases that only meet the absolute region; that the model reflections are functional equations of the triple series.
- `n1-mayer-dirac`: precise obstruction (referee: holds). The Lagrange/dilation identity holds for every g (no eigen-equation, three-term relation or Hecke input); on the Eisenstein channel the boundary term is the cusp flux zeta(rho-1), never zero; the only channel-restricted object is [(1-L_b)^(-1)1](0) = 2 zeta(2b-1)/zeta(2b), a restatement. Shifted-weight control has eigenvalue-1 points on both sides of Re b = 1/4 (measured, uncertified).
- `n1-ff-mayer`: precise obstruction (referee: holds). Over F_q[T], M(u) = W D(u) (W Hermitian unitary involution, D normal); ev_0(1-M)^(-1) = Phi_all/((q-1)L(u,chi)) (needs L(1/q,chi) != 0 as input; det divisible by the lcm, not the product). The sign-flipped operator -M on the same spaces has eigenvalue-1 events at radii 0.24-0.92, so no property invariant under M -> -M locates the zeros; the radius is Weil's bound, unchanged.
- `n1-beurling-rigidity`: proved, novelty unverified (referees: holds; label "new" not supported, likely a short corollary of the equality case for positive-definite functions). Theorem A: positive atomic self-dual measure with all atoms >= atom at 0 is a multiple of the Dirac comb of Z; so zeta's exact FE plus coefficients >= residue forces zeta. Lemma T: no self-dual first-order deformation of the integers. Identification only; restricted to zeta's own FE; Theorem B relative to Meyer 1970 (not re-read).
- `n1-maass-selberg-norm`: precise obstruction (referee: two PROVED labels too strong). Rows are cubic Kubota coefficients twisted by the quadratic symbol of modulus m, so Maass-Selberg for the Kubota / sextic Eisenstein series does not give the row norm (other automorphic realisations: open). Flat Gram spectrum of the mark vectors is sufficient for K-loss removal, not necessary (correct form: infinity-to-one norm of G against its trace). Measured (Z <= 8000): marked norm / diagonal in about [0.6, 1.9], same as a random control, where the lemma allows 5-31. Model: 31/36 if the K-loss goes; infimum stays 13/15 unless the remaining fraction of the exponent is below 1/18. n = 2 test not run.

### Lessons

1. New control: phase rotation. Any transfer-operator argument must fail for e^(i theta) M (theta = pi already suffices over F_q[T]). Unitary factorisation, Krein form, norm, normality, dilation/Dirac identities all pass this control and are closed.
2. Mayer-operator identities that hold for arbitrary g are closed; the zeta-specific content sits in one matrix element (cusp resolvent 2 zeta(2b-1)/zeta(2b)), which is a restatement. In the function-field model the L-factor enters only through the Euclid/gcd lattice-sum identity.
3. Holomorphy-domain convexity for multiple Dirichlet series returns output = input (Theorem T). The only exit is an extra reflection that moves x: the functional equation of the theta-dual m-series (MS (5.3)), not derived.
4. No Beurling system carries zeta's FE with coefficients >= residue, so that control does not exist; the direction gives identification, never an inequality on zeros.
5. The K-loss in the marked row norm is not visible at tested scales. Removing it is one lemma (decorrelation large sieve for theta twists by m p versus m p', using the pole-killing quadratic twist, target: infinity-to-one norm of the mark Gram), but it pays only if at least 17/18 of the exponent goes and ends at 31/36 > 5/6.
6. Combinable: (a) K-loss removal + slot capacity (map 6.3) + below-level mixed moment (map 6.2) have not been priced jointly in the exact LP; (b) Theorem T + a derived m-series functional equation; (c) the phase-rotation control added to the control table for every operator lane.
7. Least blocked, by honest ranking: (i) the m-series functional equation MS (5.3), the only identified object that could change the hull group; (ii) the n = 2 version of the row-norm question (quadratic rows are coefficients of a metaplectic Eisenstein series, so Maass-Selberg does apply there), which is the nearest concrete test of the "effective order 2" requirement (map 6.1). Both are within zero-free machinery; neither is known to reach 1/2.

### Lessons

1. Lee-Yang is closed in both forms. Zero-free approximants to 1/zeta are free (truncated Euler products), so "zero-free family + Hurwitz" is a uniform bound on sum_{p<=x} p^(-s), false for zeta because of the pole and equal to GRH for pole-free twists. Multi-affine spin deformations are averages of Euler products and are multiplicative only at vertices; stability fails already over F_q in genus 2.
2. Eisenstein Gram / Maass-Selberg positivity with scalar c is closed in rank 1 (innerness plus one residue sign) and, on measured evidence, in rank 2 (tests only the symmetry rho -> 1 - conj(rho) and the root number). New control added: functional-equation-symmetric off-line quadruples. A higher-rank kernel is worth a lane only if the Euler product enters through something other than the scalar c.
3. Winding, degree and Jacobian statements in an auxiliary variable (lattice point z) count off-line zeros of the neighbouring family members; the stability radius at the arithmetic anchors is a gap between zeros of two different L-functions. This is the uniformity wall in the form "repulsion between zeros of zeta and L(s, chi_D)".
4. Even-moment positivity does transfer a family bound to every member (L1-L3 are correct), but conductor-variable duality is coefficient-blind and self-dual at the large-sieve point. The function-field proof works because the base sum is complete; a transplant needs a way to lengthen x without lengthening the periods in d.
5. Two independent measurements agree on the 7/8 architecture: the only non-random direction in the cubic row systems is the Kubota residue (Gauss-sum) direction and its cubic twists; every row system without the pole (quadratic twist, conj-alpha or alpha factor, shifted slot) behaves as independent vectors of size rows + marks. The K-loss and the (KD)^(2/3) term are therefore the same phenomenon, and the open lemma is one statement: (L*) = WLS = decorrelation of the actual rows from cubic-twisted Gauss-sum vectors of conductor up to U^0.75. Bounded-rank deflation is closed.
6. Combinable: (a) n2-kloss PAIR(l)/Gershgorin reduction + n2-spike explicit spike family (project out conj g(d) chi_r(d) N(d)^(-1/6) for N(r) <= R, then bound the remainder) + n1 slot capacity and below-level mixed moment, priced jointly in the exact LP; (b) L1-L3 (family moment implies member zero-free region) + a complete-sum family; (c) the symmetric-quadruple control and the phase-rotation control added to the control table.
7. Least blocked, by honest ranking: (i) prove WLS in the pole-free form (a weighted large sieve for rows orthogonal to the twisted-Gauss spike family; Type II correlations of row coefficients with twisted cubic Gauss sums), the one open lemma both architecture lanes point to; it ends at 31/36, above 5/6, and does not reach 1/2. (ii) The n = 2 row-norm question from round 1 (metaplectic Eisenstein series, where Maass-Selberg applies) remains not run and is still the nearest test of "effective order 2". (iii) Outside the architecture: an even-moment family with complete base sums (lesson 4), and repulsion between zeta zeros and L(s, chi_D) zeros (lesson 3); neither has a candidate lemma.

### Lessons

1. New control, to be added to the table: TH(w) = theta^w, w near 1 (Lagarias-Rains). It keeps the functional equation, the pole, the positive kernel, the gamma factor, a Riemann-Roch interpretation and (as recalled, not re-derived) one-dimensional Hecke uniqueness for 0 < w < 8; it lacks an Euler product for w outside {1,2,4,8}. Its all-on-line window around w = 1 shrinks on both sides with height (measured to T = 2000). Consequence: any statement that is open in the kernel, any finite-height statement, and any uniqueness statement holds for TH(w) and cannot give RH.
2. Multiplicativity alone is not the input: w = 4 and w = 8 have Euler products and off-line zeros (44 of 202 on-line at w = 4). This agrees with the E_a and NR rows: the input must be multiplicativity with unimodular local parameters and the correct size at every prime.
3. Deformation and continuity in a parameter through zeta are closed in three settings: theta^w (zeros leave and return, no birth map), Pellikaan's section weight u over function fields (margin set by zero collisions, shrinking with q and with genus on measured cases), and heat flow (earlier rounds). The margin at the arithmetic point is a zero-gap quantity that tends to 0 with conductor or height. Twin-ladder bridging from an endpoint product is closed in this form.
4. Expansions at a CM point and resolvents at Heegner divisors are functional-equation-only. The square-root-of-central-value structure holds identically for DH; the second-sheet Green matrix differs from the first by an explicit rank-2 Eisenstein term and coincides at off-line zeros of single Epstein classes. Arithmetic there sits in an unstructured sign sequence (sgn d(n)) or in a Gross-Zagier sum convergent only for Re s > 1.
5. "Effective order 2" by symbol(polynomial) rows in one variable is closed: integrality of the frequency-0 term puts every weight-0 target at shift 1; shift 1/2 needs odd-weight (Jacobi-sum / Hecke) targets, which is the existing 7/8 class. Still open inside that lane: rows in two or more variables, targets on structured nonzero frequencies, the range Q = Z^M with M <= 1.
6. The cubic large sieve over F_q[T] is an exact self-dual identity: flatness for alpha is equivalent in the proved direction to removal of the Gauss-twisted family up to conductor K^2/D for the same alpha. Rationality removes error terms, not the growing spike family. This confirms round 2 lesson 5 with a proof in the model: the one open statement is the Type II correlation of the actual row coefficients with twisted cubic Gauss sums ((L*) = WLS); FF-WLS must be re-indexed by k0 = 2m-2-n.
7. Combinable: (a) the exact F_q[T] sieve identity + round 2 PAIR(l)/Gershgorin + the explicit spike family: state and test (L*) over F_q[T], where the Gauss-sum correlations are computable exactly and Weil bounds are available; (b) theta^w window law + Pellikaan interval + heat escape law: a single statement "margin of any FE-preserving deformation at the arithmetic point equals a local zero-gap", usable as a screening rule, not as a route; (c) TH(w), symmetric quadruples and single Epstein classes as standing controls in the three questions of the obstruction map.
8. Least blocked, by honest ranking: (i) (L*) over F_q[T] via the exact identity of lesson 6 (ends at 31/36 in the model, does not reach 1/2); (ii) two-variable or metaplectic rows for "effective order 2" (the n = 2 row-norm question, still not run after three rounds; the one-variable case is now excluded); (iii) a mechanism that separates w = 1 from w = 4, 8 and from E_a, i.e. uses unimodular local parameters at all primes jointly (Ramanujan-type input, e.g. through symmetric-power or Rankin-Selberg positivity giving a largest-value bound rather than a mean value); no candidate lemma exists for (iii). No route to RH is in sight from this round.

### Lessons

1. "Effective order 2" by {0, +-1} symbols is closed in three forms. One-variable symbol(polynomial) rows (round 3); spin of prime ideals in cyclic cubic fields (cocycle = quadratic symbol of K x rational Jacobi symbol x 2-adic Hilbert symbol; conjugate matrix is a row permutation); Borel Eisenstein coefficients on the double cover of GL(3) (L-type +chi_d(p); the only Moebius-times-symbol term is the degenerate Gauss sum on d = d0 m^2 at argument s + 2w). Common reason: an order-2 symbol squares to an indicator, so it absorbs mu only when the cocycle is trivial, which is mu inserted by hand; and every duality available is a quadratic Poisson duality with the same dual class. The cubic method works because g(p)^2 is a new object (conjugate Gauss sum times Jacobi sum), not an indicator. An order-2 analogue would need a non-symbol object: coefficients of the non-generic theta or of cuspidal forms on a double cover of rank >= 2, whose values are not {0, +-1}. Only that remainder is untested.
2. The Connes-Consani geometric lanes are closed at the level of the structure sheaf and of C-coefficients. Scaling site: Frobenius-fixed sections are x -> n x, zeros = degree, no subtraction; the count is psi = psi. Complex lift: Re s is a degree (power of the leafwise canonical bundle, centre K^{1/2} = functional equation), periodic orbits carry weights 0 and 2 only, the leafwise Kaehler class is zero. A weight-1 polarised object is absent in both; this is the Hilbert-Polya / Deninger requirement restated, not a lever. Unexamined inside these lanes: W-coefficients (Connes-Consani section 7.1), the square of the scaling site with Frobenius correspondences, non-idempotent (S-module, Witt) coefficients. None has a stated first lemma.
3. New standing control for geometric lanes: a pseudo-Anosov mapping torus (Alexander polynomial t^4 - 3t^3 + 5t^2 - 3t + 1). It has a Lefschetz trace formula and the symmetry t <-> 1/t and its roots are off the unit circle. Any argument that uses a flow, a trace formula and a functional equation without a polarisation (Kaehler / Hodge index) holds for it and is wrong.
4. Additive tensor powers (Kuenneth / Kurokawa multiple Euler series) measure the pole: abscissa of absolute convergence k for the k-fold series, zero sums behind log branch points, and the divided-difference identity holds for arbitrary weights (DH, sign twists). Deligne's gain needs the pole to enter once over a base and the frequencies to lie on a lattice; neither holds over Z in this form. Closed.
5. Family positivity in a spectral window is the first mechanism this campaign found where the zeta member is isolated by positivity and DH fails at a multiplicative step (Hecke linearisation). It still stops at mollifier length A = 1/2 unconditionally (abscissa >= 1), because lengths beyond T^{1/2} need signed cancellation over Kloosterman moduli, and its detector needs an unproved lower bound for |zeta(1/2 + it)| on the critical line. Reopen only with a tool that sums over moduli with signs without Kuznetsov. Wall Q2 (every window, uniform in height) is unchanged.
