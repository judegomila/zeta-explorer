### Three questions for any new idea, before work starts

Q1. **Which step fails for each control?** Name, for each of DH, HEAT, TW+, TW-, EA, NR, B, WT (and U, the Liouville twist and a finite modification where formulable), the step of the argument that is false or not formulable. "Not formulable" does not count as a quantitative handle (stack-combinator section 2). If every step holds for one control, the argument is wrong or proves less than claimed.

Q2. **Is the conclusion uniform in height and valid in every window?** State whether the key inequality holds for all t with constants independent of t, and in each single window rather than on average. Mean-value statements give proportions or densities, never an empty set (MV-CAP). Finite certificates restate verified height. Positivity on finite tests has no margin (eq48 Theorem 4). Fixed-window-count filter (row S12): a functional-equation test in m windows with m, the window length H and the tolerance eta independent of T is passed by a height translate zeta(s + i alpha) above a height T_0(m, H, eta); an argument whose functional-equation input is of that form fails against WT, and the window count at which the test becomes rigid is of Riemann-Siegel size, about sqrt(T)/log T.

Q3. **Where does multiplicativity enter, as opposed to positivity or size of Lambda?** Three independent results (pick-induction Theorem R; g-channel-geometry Thm C; sq-causal-cascade Thm 3) show positivity/size/support of Lambda with the functional equation cannot suffice. Identify the step that uses a(mn) = a(m)a(n) with unimodular local parameters at every prime, and check that it acts to the left of Re s = 1 (the prime chain is absolutely convergent only there).

## 5. The common wall

Every route in section 3 ends at the same requirement, which can be stated in six parts.

1. **Uniform in height, in every window.** RH (or any zero-free statement) needs an inequality valid at every height t with constants independent of t and in each window separately. Mean-value inputs give proportions or densities (MV-CAP: P27, P29, Q9, Q18, Z17); finite certificates restate verified height with margins that decay like exp(-c gamma) or faster (P3, P5, G7, Q15, S5-S7); positivity on finite tests has no margin (P2), and the one formulation with a gap (Q16) has a gap of size 4/(3 log(T/2 pi)) that tends to 0.
2. **Identities that use only the functional equation hold for the controls.** The Weil/Pick/Krein/Hankel/Loewner/Stieltjes/Schur-Blaschke/Klein identities (P1, P7, P8, G1-G3, G6, G13, Q1-Q3, Q6, S8) hold verbatim for DH, the backward heat family, E_a and the T-NR twin. In each of them RH is the sign of a quantity the identity does not determine.
3. **Prime-side statements hold only to the right of 1/2 or 1.** Lambda >= 0 with the pole gives the classical region (P13); exact positive-definiteness of log zeta holds on Re s > 1 (P29); the Cayley expansion converges in Re z > sigma_0(alpha) and its anticausal part is zero for every sign twist (Q4). No statement in the lanes transports a prime-side inequality across 0 < Re z < 1/2 except by convexity/mean values (lossy), exact uniqueness (no inequality), or the residue theorem (exact, empty) (S1).
4. **The prime chain is l^1 on Re s > 1 and l^2 on Re s > 1/2.** The summed J-defect of the Euler chain is bounded iff Re s > 1 and square-summable on the Bohr torus iff Re s > 1/2, with variance 1/(8x^2) + O(1) at Re s = 1/2 + x (Q10). A finite chain is contractive on Re s >= 1/2 only for about (log t)^2/16 primes (Q11), and that bound holds for every sign twist. The critical line is a mean-square threshold; RH is the largest-value statement (Q13, S4).
5. **Local conditions at single primes are empty for zeta.** Temperedness at p is satisfied vacuously by the standard vector (L4); it kills DH, E_a and T-NR but combined with any flow-sensitive row gives only Re s > 1 (L1); Euler-at-p identifies the function and does not locate zeros (L2); Hecke relations on the lattice variable hold for every lattice (G16); all-order positivity pins each prime weight with zero tolerance but is conditional on the positivity it would prove (P6).
