# RH research modules — scope and evidence boundary

This collection separates exact identities and conditional reductions, finite interval certificates, failed sufficient estimates, superseded versions, and unresolved hypotheses. It does not prove RH or a new global zero-free strip, assert novelty, or represent internal AI-assisted reviews as external peer review or formal verification.

## Review units

1. **Foundation and route atlas:** the one-sided Landau/shell derivations and a mathematical history of the main attempted routes. Existing repository research is linked at immutable commits rather than copied wholesale. Unmerged historical PRs remain distinct review units.
2. **Suzuki scalar arithmetic:** exact recurrence and prime-only logarithmic memory, finite-total-error credit variation, and the complete all-real scalar certificate through arithmetic cutoff X = 10^7, including the origin. The runnable finite certificate tests the constant box, not the entire supported even form.
3. **Spectral and signed complement:** actual whole-even coercivity for 1 < lambda <= sqrt(16001/1000), with the complete infinite Fourier tail and an existential positive constant; the strict X = 16.1 lower-surrogate failure; and selected signed-clipping/head-complement results with their separate scopes.

## Three boundaries that must remain visible

- A negative **lower surrogate** does not imply a negative actual form. Positive tails do not control unaccounted head/tail coupling. Minimum LDL pivots are not eigenvalue floors.
- Summable debt, interpolation, or compression errors do not bound the retained signed arithmetic supply. A finite positive prefix does not prove an all-scale lower bound.
- An accepted analytic statement is not automatically a complete executable replay. The provenance and reproduction notes specify exactly which code, inputs, receipts, and regenerated caches are included.

## Provenance and execution

User-facing source copies omit private conversation links, account identifiers, delegation text, and absolute executor paths. Each curated source maps its original SHA-256 to its published SHA-256 with the transformation stated. Runtime scripts are byte-identical where possible; regenerated output goes into disposable directories rather than overwriting frozen reference evidence. Large regenerable scalar caches and discovery-factor arrays are deliberately excluded from app payloads.

Hashes establish identity, not truth. A same-library independent replay remains different from a second arithmetic implementation or proof-assistant proof. Every theorem still requires its stated analytic hypotheses.
