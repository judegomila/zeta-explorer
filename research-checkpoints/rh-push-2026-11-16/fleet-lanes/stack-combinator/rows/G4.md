## Conventions used in the control columns

Output of almost every row here is a half-plane exponent (abscissa theta in "F(s) != 0 for Re s > theta") or a bound on
a bilinear form that feeds one. None is a critical-line statement. Three recurring control patterns, referred to by tag:

* **[EB] Euler-blind inequality.** Holds for arbitrary coefficient vectors of the given l2 norm (large sieve,
  Cauchy-Schwarz, Poisson with a smooth variable, Halasz). DH PASS; TWIST PASS (signed or nonnegative eps_n, the
  coefficient vector is arbitrary); EA PASS after rescaling the norm (coefficients of size n^a only change ||.||);
  HEAT N/A (no Dirichlet-coefficient object).
* **[TF] target-free identity.** A statement about Gauss sums / theta coefficients / kernels that contains no
  L-function. True regardless of the control, so it cannot separate: PASS for DH, TWIST, EA; HEAT N/A unless stated.
* **[MU] needs mu x (completely multiplicative unitary Hecke character).** The step reads Sum_c mu(c) nu(c) N(c)^{-s}
  as 1/L(s, nu), or uses the Euler branch of log L / L'/L on a family closed under sextic twists. DH FAIL (linear
  combination gives c1/L1 + c2/L2, never 1/f). TWIST FAIL: eps_n Lambda(n) is not the log-derivative of a Hecke
  L-function; reciprocity, the theta reflection and the twist-closed family all need a Hecke character
  (and eps = -1 gives 1/zeta, which has a zero at s = 1). EA: the method is translation-covariant in s; applied
  factor-wise to nu = N^{+-a} it yields theta + a, not theta. Unitarity |nu| = 1 is the size normalisation; the
  literal statement "zero-free for Re s > 7/8" is false for E_a when a > 3/8 (zeros at Re = 1/2 + a). HEAT N/A.

"(stated)" = the lane or its referee says it; "(inferred)" = deduced here from the uses field.
