# Cutoff-free actual-radical Xi ideal and translation-frame barrier

## 0. Exact outcome and claim boundary

Let

\[
 \mathcal E(f)(u)=u^{1/2}\sum_{n\geq1}f(nu),\qquad u>0,
 \tag{0.1}
\]

be the locked arithmetic source map.  This note proves that every actual
source-realizable global radical obtained from this map belongs, on the open
critical strip, to the same principal analytic ideal generated by `Xi`.
Writing

\[
 s={1\over2}-iz,\qquad
 C(s)={1\over2}s(s-1)\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2),
 \tag{0.2}
\]

one has

\[
 \boxed{
 \widehat{\mathcal E(f)}(z)
 =\zeta(s)m_f(z)
 =\Xi(z)A_f(z),\qquad
 A_f(z)={m_f(z)\over C(s)}.}
 \tag{0.3}
\]

Inversion symmetrization, source dilation, the source dilation generator,
and finite linear combinations change only the cofactor `A_f`.  A source
logarithmic moment does the same only when the differentiated source
independently satisfies both locked source nulls.  A raw logarithmic moment
need not remain in the actual source class.  None of the admitted operations
removes or shifts the common `Xi` divisor.

Multiplication of the physical logarithmic profile by its coordinate does
differentiate `Xi`, but it leaves the source-radical class.  More precisely,
if `K=mathcal E(f)` and `t` denotes the logarithmic coordinate, then a source
representation `tK=mathcal E(g)` would require a Mellin factor containing
`zeta'/zeta`.  It is meromorphic at any zeta zero not already canceled by
the source Mellin factor.  Equivalently, the exact weighted-integer term
which differentiates zeta is not the locked source map.  A divisor-breaking
profile also adds a global bulk functional to the finite residual identity;
its residual is no longer a truncation-only residual.

For a finite-window projection, each physical Fourier coefficient has the
exact form

\[
 \boxed{
 (p_a)_n=L^{-1/2}\Xi(\omega_n)A_a(\omega_n)+e_{a,n},
 \qquad \omega_n={2\pi n\over L},}
 \tag{0.4}
\]

where `e_(a,n)` is the omitted-tail coefficient.  Hence the exact
complete-period translation-frame floor on a band `B` is

\[
 \boxed{
 c_B=\min_{n\in B}\sum_a|(p_a)_n|^2.}
 \tag{0.5}
\]

Thus a family with subquadratic-exponential cofactor energy and
`exp(-c lambda^2)` tails can have an `exp(-o(lambda^2))` floor only after a
new `Xi` minimum-modulus estimate on the whole sampled band.  At an exact
grid collision with a critical-line zero, only the exponentially smaller
tail errors remain.

There is a separate exact translation obstruction.  For the locked limiting
anchor `K`, its one-sided logarithmic tail satisfies

\[
 T(b):=\int_b^\infty|K(t)|^2\,dt
 \asymp e^{7b-2\pi e^{2b}}.                            \tag{0.6}
\]

For `K_tau(t)=K(t+tau)` on `[-log lambda,log lambda]`,

\[
 \boxed{
 \|1_{I_\lambda^c}K_\tau\|_2^2
 =T(\log\lambda+\tau)+T(\log\lambda-\tau).}
 \tag{0.7}
\]

Uniform outer leakage of order `exp(-c lambda^2)` therefore forces the
translation range to stay bounded.  But translations supported in a fixed
range `|tau|<=S` have, even before the `Xi` weights are inserted, frame
floor on modes `0,...,N` at most

\[
 \boxed{
 c_{N,S}(p)
 \leq \|p\|^2{(2\pi S/L)^{2N}\over\binom{2N}{N}}
 \leq(2N+1)\|p\|^2\left({\pi S\over L}\right)^{2N}.}
 \tag{0.8}
\]

For `N` comparable to `lambda^2` and fixed `S`, this is
`exp(-Theta(lambda^2 log log lambda))`, not
`exp(-o(lambda^2))`.  A complete-period average diagonalizes the frame, but
includes translations for which the outer leakage is not exponentially
small at the `lambda^2` scale.  This is the exact boundary/frame dichotomy.

The note does not rule out a very high-complexity polynomial--`Xi` family on
a specially selected zero-avoiding grid.  Such a route would still require
new minimum-modulus, cofactor-conditioning, and moving-order residual
estimates.  It is not a divisor-free bypass.

No actual bottom capture, dangerous-space theorem, `Xi` minimum-modulus
bound, new zero theorem, converse, or RH conclusion is proved.
The standard eight are untouched.  The formal state remains `P0_INVALIDATED`.

## 2. Every actual source radical has the common Xi divisor

Use the locked transforms

\[
 \widehat F(z)=\int_0^\infty F(u)u^{-iz}{du\over u},
 \qquad
 m_f(z)=\int_0^\infty f(v)v^{1/2-iz}{dv\over v}.
 \tag{2.1}
\]

Let `f` be in the source class: it is even, has sufficient decay or compact
support for the admitted Mellin continuation, vanishes at the origin, and
has zero Lebesgue integral.  In the initial absolute-convergence half-plane,
the substitution `v=nu` gives

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \widehat{\mathcal E(f)}(z)
 &=\sum_{n\geq1}\int_0^\infty
       u^{1/2}f(nu)u^{-iz}{du\over u}\\
 &=\sum_{n\geq1}n^{-1/2+iz}
       \int_0^\infty f(v)v^{1/2-iz}{dv\over v}\\
 &=\zeta\!\left({1\over2}-iz\right)m_f(z).
 \end{aligned}                                         \tag{2.2}
\]

The source null at the integral cancels the zeta pole, and analytic
continuation gives (2.2) on the admitted open strip.  With

\[
 \xi(s)=C(s)\zeta(s),\qquad
 \Xi(z)=\xi\!\left({1\over2}-iz\right),               \tag{2.3}
\]

the factor `C(s)` has neither a zero nor a pole when `0<Re(s)<1`.
Consequently (0.3) is an exact analytic factorization on that strip.  Every
nontrivial zeta zero occurs with its full multiplicity in the first factor.

Let `J` be inversion in multiplicative coordinates, equivalently reflection
in the logarithmic coordinate.  Then

\[
 \widehat{J\mathcal E(f)}(z)
 =\widehat{\mathcal E(f)}(-z)
 =\Xi(z)A_f(-z),                                      \tag{2.4}
\]

because `Xi(-z)=Xi(z)`.  Thus the inversion-even and inversion-odd profiles
satisfy

\[
 \boxed{
 \widehat{S_\pm\mathcal E(f)}(z)
 =\Xi(z){A_f(z)\pm A_f(-z)\over2},
 \qquad S_\pm={1\over2}(I\pm J).}                    \tag{2.5}
\]

This proves the principal-ideal assertion for both parities.  It is stronger
than qualitative zero evaluation: it retains the full zero multiplicity.

## 3. Exact source operations preserve the divisor

### 3.1 Dilation and physical translation

For real `tau`, define the unitary source dilation

\[
 (D_\tau f)(x)=e^{\tau/2}f(e^\tau x).                 \tag{3.1}
\]

Term by term,

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal E(D_\tau f)(u)=\mathcal E(f)(e^\tau u),
 \qquad
 m_{D_\tau f}(z)=e^{iz\tau}m_f(z).}                  \tag{3.2}
\]

Hence a global logarithmic translation is exactly source realizable, but it
multiplies the cofactor only by the zero-free phase `e^(iz tau)`.  The source
conditions at zero and at the integral are preserved.

### 3.2 The source dilation generator

Put

\[
 \mathcal L={1\over2}+x{d\over dx}.                   \tag{3.3}
\]

For a source on which termwise differentiation is admitted,

\[
 \boxed{
 {d\over dt}\mathcal E(f)(e^t)
 =\mathcal E(\mathcal Lf)(e^t),
 \qquad
 m_{\mathcal Lf}(z)=iz\,m_f(z).}                     \tag{3.4}
\]

The Mellin identity follows by integration by parts:

\[
 \int_0^\infty(\mathcal Lf)(v)v^s{dv\over v}
 =\left({1\over2}-s\right)\int_0^\infty f(v)v^s{dv\over v}
 =iz\,m_f(z).                                        \tag{3.5}
\]

Every finite generator jet therefore has transform

\[
 \widehat{\mathcal E(\mathcal L^rf)}(z)
 =(iz)^r\Xi(z)A_f(z).                                 \tag{3.6}
\]

This is physical differentiation, not multiplication by the physical
coordinate.  It cannot differentiate `Xi`.

### 3.3 Raw logarithmic moments need not be actual sources

Let `M_r f(x)=(log|x|)^r f(x)` away from the origin.  At the purely Mellin
level,

\[
 m_{M_rf}(z)=\partial_s^r m_f(z)
 =i^r\partial_z^r m_f(z).                             \tag{3.7}
\]

The arithmetic factor remains the undifferentiated `zeta(s)`:

\[
 \widehat{\mathcal E(M_rf)}(z)
 =\zeta(s)\partial_s^r m_f(z)
 =\Xi(z){\partial_s^r m_f(z)\over C(s)}.             \tag{3.8}
\]

Equation (3.8) does not by itself put `M_r f` in the actual source class.
That class requires both the origin null and the Lebesgue-integral null.
Adding actual sources cannot repair a failed null: every such summand already
has value zero under both null functionals.

There is an explicit Schwartz counterexample.  Put

\[
 \phi(x)=x^2e^{-x^2},\qquad
 f(x)=\phi(x)-2\phi(2x).                              \tag{3.9}
\]

Then `f` is even, `f(0)=0`, and a change of variables gives

\[
 \int_{\mathbb R}f(x)\,dx=0,
 \qquad
 \int_{\mathbb R}(\log|x|)f(x)\,dx
 =(\log2)\int_{\mathbb R}\phi(y)\,dy>0.             \tag{3.10}
\]

Thus `M_1f` retains the origin null but loses the integral null.  Whenever
`M_rf` independently satisfies both nulls and the remaining source
regularity, (3.8) is an actual-source factorization and retains the same
`Xi` divisor.  Dilation derivatives, admissible logarithmic source moments,
parity combinations, and arbitrary finite mixing of actual sources therefore
cannot supply different `Xi` zero factors.

## 4. Divisor breaking is not an actual radical operation

Let

\[
 K(t)=\mathcal E(f)(e^t),\qquad
 \widehat K(z)=\zeta(s)m_f(z)=\Xi(z)A_f(z).           \tag{4.1}
\]

With the Fourier convention `exp(-izt)`, multiplication by `t` gives

\[
 \widehat{tK}(z)
 =i\partial_z\{\zeta(s)m_f(z)\}
 =\zeta'(s)m_f(z)+i\zeta(s)\partial_zm_f(z).          \tag{4.2}
\]

Here `zeta'` means differentiation in `s`, and `ds/dz=-i` has already been
included.  If `tK=mathcal E(g)` for another admissible source, division by
`zeta(s)` would force

\[
 \boxed{
 m_g(z)={\zeta'(s)\over\zeta(s)}m_f(z)
          +i\partial_zm_f(z).}                        \tag{4.3}
\]

The right side is meromorphic at every zeta zero at which `m_f` does not
cancel the logarithmic-derivative pole.  An admissible source Mellin
transform is holomorphic on the strip.  Thus coordinate multiplication is
not an actual-source operation unless the source supplies the cancellations
which restore the removed divisor.

The same distinction is visible before Mellin continuation.  Define the
weighted integer transform

\[
 \mathcal E_{\log n}(f)(u)
 =u^{1/2}\sum_{n\geq1}(\log n)f(nu).                  \tag{4.4}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 t\mathcal E(f)(e^t)
 =\mathcal E((\log|x|)f)(e^t)-\mathcal E_{\log n}(f)(e^t),}
 \tag{4.5}
\]

while

\[
 \widehat{\mathcal E_{\log n}(f)}(z)
 =-\zeta'(s)m_f(z).                                   \tag{4.6}
\]

The first term in (4.5) retains `zeta` algebraically, but it is an actual
source radical only when `(log|x|)f` independently satisfies both source
nulls.  The second is the nonradical arithmetic term which breaks the
divisor.  A frequency modulation shifts the `Xi` divisor and a resonant
divided difference adds a subtraction value; both fail the same
zero-evaluation test unless their new term vanishes at every relevant zero.

### 4.1 The bulk residual which truncation cannot remove

Let `H` be any global profile, let `p=T H` be its finite restriction and
projection, and put `R=H-p`.  Bilinearity gives, for every compact test
vector `v`,

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal W_Z(p,v)
 =\mathcal W_Z(H,v)-\mathcal W_Z(R,v).}               \tag{4.7}
\]

For a source radical, the first term is exactly zero and the finite residual
is controlled by the truncation remainder `R`.  For a divisor-breaking
profile, `mathcal W_Z(H,cdot)` is a global bulk functional independent of
the window tail.  Sending `R` to zero does not remove it.  Proving that this
bulk term equals a vanishing scalar target times the compact inner product
would be a new global eigenvector theorem, not a consequence of source
realizability or truncation.

## 5. Finite-window coefficients retain the common factor

Put

\[
 I_\lambda=[-a,a],\qquad a=\log\lambda,qquad
 L=2a,qquad \omega_n={2\pi n\over L}.                \tag{5.1}
\]

Let `C_lambda` restrict to `I_lambda`, let `P_N` be the orthogonal periodic
Fourier projection, and set

\[
 p_a=P_NC_\lambda K_a,qquad
 \phi_n(t)=L^{-1/2}e^{i\omega_nt}.                    \tag{5.2}
\]

Projection does not change a retained Fourier coefficient.  Therefore

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 (p_a)_n
 &=L^{-1/2}\int_{-a}^aK_a(t)e^{-i\omega_nt}\,dt\\
 &=L^{-1/2}\widehat K_a(\omega_n)+e_{a,n}\\
 &=L^{-1/2}\Xi(\omega_n)A_a(\omega_n)+e_{a,n},
 \end{aligned}                                        \tag{5.3}
\]

where

\[
 e_{a,n}=-L^{-1/2}\int_{\mathbb R\setminus I_\lambda}
               K_a(t)e^{-i\omega_nt}\,dt,qquad
 |e_{a,n}|\leq L^{-1/2}\|1_{I_\lambda^c}K_a\|_1.    \tag{5.4}
\]

Let `U_tau` be periodic translation.  Fourier orthogonality gives, for any
band vector `g`,

\[
 {1\over L}\int_0^L|\langle g,U_\tau p_a\rangle|^2\,d\tau
 =\sum_n|g_n|^2|(p_a)_n|^2.                           \tag{5.5}
\]

Summing over a finite family proves (0.5).  In particular, if
`Xi(omega_(n_0))=0`, then

\[
 c_B\leq\sum_a|e_{a,n_0}|^2
 \leq {1\over L}\sum_a\|1_{I_\lambda^c}K_a\|_1^2.  \tag{5.6}
\]

More generally, the elementary square inequality gives

\[
 \sum_a|(p_a)_n|^2
 \leq {2\over L}|\Xi(\omega_n)|^2
                \sum_a|A_a(\omega_n)|^2
       +2\sum_a|e_{a,n}|^2.                           \tag{5.7}
\]

Suppose on a band of `O(lambda^2)` modes that

\[
 \sup_n\sum_a|A_a(\omega_n)|^2=e^{o(\lambda^2)},
 \qquad
 \sum_a\|1_{I_\lambda^c}K_a\|_1^2
 \leq e^{-2c\lambda^2+o(\lambda^2)}.                 \tag{5.8}
\]

Then (5.7) shows that

\[
 c_B\geq e^{-o(\lambda^2)}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \min_{n\in B}|\Xi(\omega_n)|\geq e^{-o(\lambda^2)}.
 \tag{5.9}
\]

Thus multiple actual radicals do not bypass the sampled minimum-modulus
problem.  Cofactors can compensate small nonzero values only by paying a
corresponding conditioning or source-cost price.  At an exact zero they
cannot compensate at all.

The periodic orbit in (5.5) is the exact algebraic diagonalization on the
circle.  It is not silently identified with `P_N C_lambda K_tau` for a
globally translated radical: periodic translation wraps across the seam,
whereas global source dilation does not.  Realizing a complete-period orbit
by actual global translates therefore requires control of precisely the
outer boundary terms studied next.

## 6. Exact tail asymptotic and the boundary-safe translation range

The locked limiting source and arithmetic profile are

\[
 h(x)={\pi\over2}x^2(2\pi x^2-3)e^{-\pi x^2},
 \qquad
 K(t)=\mathcal E(h)(e^t).                             \tag{6.1}
\]

For `u>=1`, every summand is positive.  The `n=1` summand and the admitted
integral-comparison upper bound give constants `0<c_0<C_0` such that

\[
 c_0u^{9/2}e^{-\pi u^2}
 \leq K(\log u)
 \leq C_0u^{9/2}e^{-\pi u^2}.                         \tag{6.2}
\]

For the lower bound one may take

\[
 c_0={\pi\over2}(2\pi-3),                             \tag{6.3}
\]

because

\[
 2\pi u^2-3-(2\pi-3)u^2=3(u^2-1)\geq0.              \tag{6.4}
\]

The source is Fourier invariant with the two source nulls, so Poisson
symmetry gives `K(-t)=K(t)`.  With `U=e^b`, (6.2) yields

\[
 T(b)\asymp\int_U^\infty u^8e^{-2\pi u^2}\,du.       \tag{6.5}
\]

For `U>=1`, integration by parts for the upper bound and integration over
`[U,U+U^(-1)]` for the lower bound give

\[
 \int_U^\infty u^8e^{-2\pi u^2}\,du
 \asymp U^7e^{-2\pi U^2}.                             \tag{6.6}
\]

Equations (6.5)--(6.6) prove (0.6).

For `K_tau(t)=K(t+tau)` and `|tau|<a`, a change of variables and evenness
give the exact identity

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \|1_{[-a,a]^c}K_\tau\|_2^2
 &=\int_{a+\tau}^\infty|K(x)|^2\,dx
   +\int_{a-\tau}^\infty|K(x)|^2\,dx\\
 &=T(a+\tau)+T(a-\tau).
 \end{aligned}                                        \tag{6.7}
\]

The smaller threshold is `a-|tau|`.  Hence, while that threshold tends to
infinity,

\[
 -\log\|1_{I_\lambda^c}K_\tau\|_2^2
 =2\pi\lambda^2e^{-2|\tau|}+O(\log\lambda+|\tau|).
 \tag{6.8}
\]

If a translation family has `sup|tau|<=S` for one fixed `S`, it retains an
`exp(-c_S lambda^2)` outer scale.  Conversely, if its translation radius
tends to infinity, the coefficient `e^(-2|tau|)` tends to zero, so no one
positive `c` gives an `exp(-c lambda^2)` outer bound uniformly over the
family.  Translations within bounded distance of either endpoint omit a
fixed positive portion of the nonzero profile and have order-one leakage.

This is a statement about the outer leakage which drives the admitted
truncation-residual proof.  It does not assert that no unrelated signed
cancellation could ever be discovered for a different nonradical family.

## 7. Binomial upper bound for every boundary-safe translation orbit

Work on the circle of length `L`.  Let

\[
 p=\sum_{n=0}^Np_n\phi_n,                             \tag{7.1}
\]

and let `nu` be any probability measure supported in `[-S,S]`.  Define its
translation-orbit frame floor by

\[
 c_{N,S}(p)=\inf_{\substack{g\in\operatorname {span}
       \{\phi_0,\ldots,\phi_N\}\\ \|g\|=1}}
 \int_{-S}^S|\langle g,U_\tau p\rangle|^2\,d\nu(\tau).
 \tag{7.2}
\]

If one `p_n` vanishes, the corresponding coordinate gives `c_(N,S)=0`.
Otherwise set

\[
 b_n={(-1)^{N-n}\binom Nn\over\sqrt{\binom{2N}{N}}},
 \qquad
 g_n={\overline {b_n}\over\overline {p_n}},
 \qquad
 d^2=\sum_{n=0}^N|g_n|^2.                             \tag{7.3}
\]

Vandermonde's identity gives

\[
 \sum_{n=0}^N|b_n|^2
 ={\sum_{n=0}^N\binom Nn^2\over\binom{2N}{N}}=1.     \tag{7.4}
\]

Therefore

\[
 d^{-2}\leq\max_n|p_n|^2\leq\|p\|^2.               \tag{7.5}
\]

The correlation of the normalized vector `g/d` is the binomial polynomial

\[
 |\langle g/d,U_\tau p\rangle|
 ={1\over d\sqrt{\binom{2N}{N}}}
   |e^{2\pi i\tau/L}-1|^N.                           \tag{7.6}
\]

For `|tau|<=S`,

\[
 |e^{2\pi i\tau/L}-1|\leq {2\pi S\over L}.          \tag{7.7}
\]

Inserting this one unit vector into (7.2) proves the first inequality in
(0.8).  Since the central binomial coefficient is the largest coefficient
in the expansion of `(1+1)^(2N)`,

\[
 (2N+1)\binom{2N}{N}\geq4^N,                         \tag{7.8}
\]

which proves the second inequality.

If the translation measure has total mass `W` instead of one, the right
side is multiplied by `W`.  Thus polynomially many discrete shifts,
even/odd parity pairs, or subquadratic-exponential weights do not change the
leading conclusion.  For fixed `S`, `N` comparable to `lambda^2`, and
`L=2 log lambda`,

\[
 \log c_{N,S}(p)
 \leq-2N\log L+O(N)+\log\{W\|p\|^2\}
 =-\Theta(\lambda^2\log\log\lambda)                 \tag{7.9}
\]

whenever the last logarithm is `o(lambda^2 log log lambda)`.

For any fixed derivative jet, differentiating the polynomial in (7.6)
only lowers its zero order from `N` to `N-O(1)`.  The remaining factor grows
only polynomially in `N`, so (7.9) is unchanged at leading order.  A
moving jet deep enough to change this conclusion is precisely a
high-complexity polynomial--`Xi` construction; no uniform moving-order
source conditioning or exponentially small residual is supplied here.

## 8. Consequence for the proposed dangerous-space route

Assume hypothetically that an actual arithmetic theorem localized the full
dangerous projector to an `O(lambda^2)` physical band.  If an actual radical
synthesis on that band had frame floor

\[
 c_\lambda\geq e^{-o(\lambda^2)}                      \tag{8.1}
\]

and combined residual norm

\[
 \|E_\lambda\|\leq e^{-c\lambda^2+o(\lambda^2)},     \tag{8.2}
\]

then

\[
 {\|E_\lambda\|\over\sqrt{c_\lambda}}\longrightarrow0
 \tag{8.3}
\]

would indeed close the already-proved bottom-projector implication.
Equations (2.5), (5.7), (6.8), and (7.9) show why the proposed operations do
not supply (8.1):

1. every actual radical retains the common `Xi` divisor;
2. breaking that divisor leaves a global bulk residual;
3. complete-period translation loses the exponential outer scale; and
4. boundary-safe translation has a superquadratically small frame floor on
   a quadratic band.

The remaining logical possibility is a high-complexity common-divisor
family with quantitative sampled minimum modulus, controlled cofactors, and
uniform moving-order leakage.  None of those inputs is proved by source
cyclicity, analytic nonvanishing, a dangerous-space count, or the present
truncation estimates.

## 9. Stopping line

This note proves a no-go for the proposed **divisor-free** actual-radical
bypass.  It does not prove that every possible common-`Xi` high-complexity
frame fails.  A successful continuation must add at least one genuinely new
actual-source theorem:

1. a sampled `Xi` minimum-modulus and cofactor-conditioning estimate on a
   quadratic band;
2. a moving-order radical family with a simultaneous exponential residual
   and frame theorem; or
3. a different global near-eigenfamily whose bulk residual is controlled
   without falsely calling it a truncation radical.

No such theorem is imported or inferred here.  The formal state remains
`P0_INVALIDATED`.
