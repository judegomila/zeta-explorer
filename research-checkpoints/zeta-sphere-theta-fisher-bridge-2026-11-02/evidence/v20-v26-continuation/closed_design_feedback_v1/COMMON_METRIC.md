# Common actual-prefix metric for the remaining payment

Status: analytic derivation submitted for independent audit. No all-degree payment sign claimed.

Work at normalized a=0, fixed lambda, with actual positive normalized pivots sigma_0,...,sigma_N only. Put Y_j=T_j(D)psi_lambda/sqrt(sigma_j). Coefficient a derivatives vanish at0; the first parameter derivative is hY_j+K_j, with K_j=T_j'(D)psi_lambda/sqrt(sigma_j). Use the ordered old NEXT-parity columns V, and remaining CURRENT-parity columns W. Together U=(V,W) is the minimal closed positive design. All coefficients below use a-dependent columns with the fixed normalization sqrt(sigma_j(0)); no next pivot normalizes anything.

Positive half-line Gram B=integral h U U^T=[[A,C],[C^T,D]], and c=integral h U q for q=D_h[T_N g_a]. Let gamma=(x,y)=B^-1c, alpha=A^-1 c_V. The gain L=c^TB^-1c-c_V^TA^-1c_V. The true signed feedback is f=r^T A'^-1r, r=c_V'-A'alpha. The inverse A' is justified by the original positive old parity prefix, not B conditioning.

Exact cancellation gives
 L'-f=2 y^T(c_W'-C'^Tx)-y^TD'y
       -(c_V'-A'x)^T A'^-1(c_V'-A'x).                    (1)
Proof: x=alpha-A^-1Cy, expand the derivative of L, then complete the square in r. The C^TA^-1A'A^-1C contribution from differentiating the positive Schur complement cancels exactly. Every source pairing c' can be reduced by the previously audited integration by parts to current-prefix jets.

In this actual normalized parity basis,
 A'=I/2, D'=I/2, J=C'.
These are the original raw normalization sigma=2 times the half-line derivative. Thus
 L'-f=2y^T c_W'-2y^TJ^Tx-(1/2)||y||^2
       -2||c_V'-(1/2)x||^2.                              (2)
Equivalently,
 L'-f=2y^T(c_W'-2J^Tc_V')
       -(1/2)y^T(I-4J^TJ)y
       -(1/2)||x-2c_V'+2Jy||^2.                         (3)
The original sufficient test is still 2 times (1) >= kappa_0 M_N. It is not legitimate to discard the final negative square, to infer a sign for I-4J^TJ, or to replace x,y by coefficients from a different regression. This is a common TRUE prefix metric, not auxiliary positive-parent coercivity.

## Exact theta-prefix recurrence for the mixed metric

Use natural degree order. Let H_ij=B'_ij and define
 M_r,ij=integral_0^infinity h^r Y_iY_j,
 K_i=sum_l Cconn_i,l Y_l,
 R=2M_1+Cconn M_0+M_0 Cconn^T.
Then H=2M_2+Cconn M_1+M_1 Cconn^T.
All of these are actual complete-theta prefix quantities. The symbol connection Cconn is strictly lower degree and known from the current monic polynomials.

The bilinear Green identity is
 H(Y_i,DY_j)+H(DY_i,Y_j)=-R_ij.                           (4)
Here H on differentiated features includes the symbol derivative identity (xT)'=T+xT'. To prove (4), integrate derivatives of h^2Y_iY_j and h(K_iY_j+Y_iK_j); the h and h^2 boundary factors vanish at0 and full theta decays superexponentially at infinity.

Write b_i=sqrt(sigma_(i+1)/sigma_i), for i<N, and b_-1=0. The actual physical recurrence DY_i=b_iY_(i+1)-b_(i-1)Y_(i-1) yields, for i,j<N,
 b_j H_(i,j+1)+b_i H_(i+1,j)
  =b_(j-1)H_(i,j-1)+b_(i-1)H_(i-1,j)-R_ij.             (5)
This keeps both actual parity ladders and their true pivot ratios. For diagonal i=j<N it telescopes:
 H_(i,i+1)=-(sum_(k=0)^i R_kk)/(2b_i).                  (6)
No next pivot is required, including at i=N-1. The full opposite-parity J can be generated by ascending odd anti-diagonals: start each anti-diagonal at its central adjacent entry from (6), then solve (5) outward using already known lower anti-diagonals. Every division is by a positive prefix b_i only. This makes the cancellation channel explicit; it does NOT supply a bound on the cumulative signed R_kk or on the true b_i ratios as degree grows.

A precise necessary condition for the mixed metric H to be positive semidefinite follows from its actual parity diagonal blocks I/2:
 abs(sum_(k=0)^i R_kk) <= b_i, for every i<N.            (7)
Indeed |H_i,i+1|<=1/2 is necessary. More generally H>=0 iff ||2J||<=1. Neither (7) nor mixed-H positivity is proved for all theta degrees here; nor is mixed-H positivity itself sufficient for the scalar payment. This exposes the exact cumulative residue / actual-pivot comparison a contraction approach would have to pay, rather than hiding it in a parent norm.
