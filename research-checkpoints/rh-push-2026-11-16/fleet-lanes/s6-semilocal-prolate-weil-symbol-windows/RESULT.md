## 3. Combination 1: spectral sum and gap of A_S for a growing set of places

Finite consequence tested. For S = {inf, 2, .., p_k}: gap(k) = 1 - sup abs(lambda_n(A_S)) and Sigma(S) = sum abs(lambda_n)^3 / sqrt(1 - lambda_n^2). Survival would need: zeta's values following one law in k, every control following another, and T-NR (off-line zeros, identical operator) showing that zeros are not seen, which by itself already excludes a survivor in the sense of the campaign. The test was run to record the laws.

Method (`c1_spec.py`): FFT-Mellin on the grid v in [-34, 30] of lane s2; top states by Lanczos with full reorthogonalisation (residuals below 1e-14); the remaining part of the trace of f(A^T A) by stochastic Lanczos quadrature (8 to 48 Rademacher probes projected off the converged top states, 30 or 40 steps; the 15-step and 30-step quadratures agree to 0.3 percent). The statistical error is the standard error over probes; runs with 4 probes (2.56e7 points) carry an error that the printed value underestimates.

## 5. Combination 3: trace-side multiplier and the region in the local parameter

### 5.1 The disagreement

Source (`rh-route-i-semilocal-sonine-trace-identity-2026-09-23.js`): "dropping -cos(pi t) gives min -1.28; flipping the p = 2 sign gives min -1.01". Round 2 (lane s2): every swap of the local factor gives a non-negative multiplier. Both are reproduced with one code (`c3_symbol.py`):

In the second the kernel is not the kernel of a unitary operator (the term -cos(pi t) is the coefficient c_{-1} = -1/p of rho_2 = (1 - 2^(s-1))/(1 - 2^-s); without it the multiplier of the kernel is 1/(1 - 2^-s) on the line, of modulus up to 3.41). For every local factor rho_p is unimodular on the line, F_S is unitary and the trace Tr(theta(g * g~) P Phat_S P) is non-negative (lane s2, F1, PROVED). The source sentence "controls go negative" is a statement about mismatched pairs. Both lanes are right about what they computed.
